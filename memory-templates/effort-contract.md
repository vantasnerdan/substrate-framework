# Effort Achievement Contract

Use for durable multi-step work. Earn the complete requested improvement with
proportionate evidence; scientific campaigns additionally earn all ten achievements.
Apply the shared reward and evidence contract in `AGENTS.md` and the applicable
skill. Fill sections with concrete evidence, not repeated policy. Begin each
section with a prose sentence for memory-index disclosure.

```md
---
description: <positive object or improvement>
author: <agent-id>
created: '<ISO-8601>'

tags:
- substrate-framework
- effort
category: efforts
confidence: working
status: active
---

## Objective and Baseline
State the complete positive result, its consumer, release/commit, accepted claims
and source actually read, and memory/source searches. Separate authority from
provenance. List user constraints, conventions, units, imports, and ownership.

## Achievement Graph
Freeze parent objective, obligations, candidates, and attempts. Each node records
positive_intent, requires, pass_licenses, does_not_license, maximum_verdict,
failure_scope, and unlocks. Order recall, selection, object/ensemble/observable,
analytic closure, numerical remainder if needed, implementation, exposing
verification, consumers, and applicable promotion.

## Approach and Supervision
For scientific discovery, keep physics-discovery's brief and promising seeds here
or link the existing campaign brief. Preregister actual competing mechanisms
(or one fixed-theorem route), criteria, discovery exposure and fresh tests.
Link bounded worker contracts. Record the supervisor's
equation/quantifier audit, current graph/AST dependency analysis, alternatives,
consumer payoff, and decision before dependent work.

## Attempts and Continuation
Preserve commands/source/first output and mechanisms append-only. Use the physics
skill's compatibility preflight and repair ladder; an environment abort is
provenance, not scientific rejection. Retrieve nearby and structurally related
constructions alongside new generation. Record what transfers, the assumption
behind a failure or transformation behind a success, and the next useful check.

## Evidence and Validation
Map each document claim to sourced derivation, proof/check, or citation. Link
analytic closure and frozen design for a numerical remainder. Record the
computed predicate, proposition, objective bridge, exact command, independent
status/tally inventories, and meaningful sensitivity or statement/axiom audit.
Use the physics oracle reference for independence and cross-sector maps.

Record targeted tests, affected consumers, graph/AST scope rationale,
scripts/validate_changed.py decision, fixed/scoped/full command and one
content-addressed receipt. Run git diff --check and commit separately.
Reuse unchanged scientific receipts for prose-only edits.

## In-Scope Repairs and Positive Frontier
List hidden assumptions, fitted inputs, promises or broken consumers inside
the proposed scope, their resolving artifact and status. Explicit hypotheses,
honest exclusions and unfinished parent work remain visible frontier.

## Results and Canonicalization
Lead with the strongest meaningful result and reproduction command. Record
applicable API/tests, claim review/registry, release, immutable campaign,
generated documentation and accepted-memory synchronization. Keep proposal
memory distinct. For a repairable harvest name minimum repair and landing check.

## Completion, Continuation, or User Pause
Record achievement of the bounded objective; scientific completion follows the
ten achievements in AGENTS.md. Scientific exhaustion additionally links the
complete candidate/equivalence inventory, continuation records, independent
alternative-generation challenge, infinite-class coverage and reviewed empty
frontier. Otherwise continue the active objective on its campaign branch.
Honor an explicit stop with committed/archived drafts, receipts, ownership and
next executable route; it is a pause, not completion or exhaustion.

## Cross-References
Link contracts, sources, claims, tests, campaign/release, issue and memory.
```
