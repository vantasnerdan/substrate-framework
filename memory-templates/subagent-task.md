# Bounded Worker Achievement

Use when delegation is authorized. The elite worker returns one useful, auditable
construction; the supervisor owns expansions, alternatives, and integration.
Apply the shared reward and evidence contract in `AGENTS.md` and the applicable
skill. Fill sections with concrete evidence, not repeated policy. Begin each
section with a prose sentence for memory-index disclosure.

```md
---
description: <single positive deliverable>
author: <agent-id>
created: '<ISO-8601>'
updated: '<ISO-8601>'
tags:
- substrate-framework
- delegated-task
category: efforts
confidence: working
status: active
---

## Achievement and Consumer
Name the exact deliverable, success evidence, parent obligation it advances, and
return boundary. A route verdict is scoped progress, not parent completion.

## Sourced Inputs and Ownership
List raw release/claims/equations/data, paths/hashes, parent contract, assumptions,
invariants, and the disjoint edit surface. Give reviewers evidence and criteria
without the author's preferred conclusion. Return PR/registry coordination to
the supervisor or designated owner.
For an authorized discovery assignment, use physics-discovery's compact brief;
record the inputs visible on the first look and reveal the favored route/history
after the first construction. Preserve sourced physical constraints throughout.

## Runtime
Record requested model/effort/permissions, actual-context evidence or uncertainty.
Use the user's specified settings (P253 mathematical workers: Sol 5.6 high).
Preserve fallback-era work as scratch until independently rederived under the
requested context; settings/provenance are not proof of correctness.

## Approach and Alternatives
State the bounded candidate, decisive method, expected failure mechanism,
available alternatives, structural selection criteria, and comparator exposure.
For numerics link analytic closure, strongest exact result, named remainder,
frozen design, and applicable small-ratio prescriptions before choosing a solver.
Exploratory work returns hypotheses/debugging evidence at that scope.
For discovery, return a concrete seed, useful consequence, missing bridge and
informative next check. Reconcile it with the revealed history before development;
different personalities alone supply neither distinct mechanisms nor independence.

## Execution and Evidence
List dependency-ordered steps, defining equations/units/signs/geometric factors,
importable APIs, exact commands, sensitivity/refinement and affected consumers.
Capture first-run source/output/status and failed attempts append-only.

## Supervisor Return Points
Return at assignment completion or the first structural obstruction. Report
what is established, exact missing construction, promising alternatives,
changed paths, reproduction commands, and in-scope repairs. The supervisor
checks current GitNexus or AST/source dependencies plus independent mathematics
and decides consume, repair, representation change, or candidate change.
Broader work begins with a new supervisor-owned assignment.

## Completion and Resume
Confirm the bounded achievement or give its one route verdict and mechanism.
Name what the next construction would establish, not merely what it avoids.
Record parent links and paused/uncommitted state when interrupted.
```
