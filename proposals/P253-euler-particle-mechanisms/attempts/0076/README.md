## Unit A: final 0072 exact tail and interaction calculus

### A1. VSH classification and physical tail domain

1. Freeze the Euclidean Fourier convention, sphere orientation, physical
   vector basis, VSH normalization, spherical Laplacian/curl/divergence signs,
   and real-field conjugacy/parity conventions.
2. Reconstruct every homogeneous degree-minus-two divergence-free velocity
   and its degree-minus-three vorticity from the stated VSH coefficients.
   Check radial and tangential components, excluded gauge/gradient rows,
   exceptional low harmonics, reality, parity, and completeness in the exact
   regularity class claimed.
3. Type the field at the origin and infinity separately. A literal
   `r^(-2)` field is finite-energy at infinity but singular at the origin;
   any whole-space finite-energy datum needs an actual smooth core/matching
   construction. Check `H^s`, weighted, vorticity, first-moment, flux, and
   boundary-term conditions rather than inferring them from homogeneity.
4. Verify which tail coefficients survive translations, rotations, parity,
   and divergence-free projection. Distinguish an asymptotic coefficient from
   a globally homogeneous field and from a stationary Euler solution.

**Maximum verdict:** an exact VSH parameterization and admissible asymptotic
finite-energy tail class on its stated punctured or whole-space domain.

### A2. Fourier transform, cross kernel, and Gaussian exposure

5. Derive the distributional Fourier transform of every retained VSH channel,
   including powers of `|k|`, phases, angular tensors, `2*pi` convention,
   reality, zero modes, contact terms, and the domain on which the transform

6. Reconstruct the two-tail kinetic cross energy in both Fourier and physical
   space. Check density, signs, double-counting, translation phase, angular
   contractions, convergence at `k=0` and infinity, and the exact separation
   scaling. State when the leading coefficient is nonzero and when symmetry
   or orientation cancels it.
7. Audit any claimed Coulomb-power kernel as a kinetic overlap of the actual
   vector fields. An orientation-dependent `d^(-1)` channel is not an
   isotropic signed electric charge, central mechanical potential, or force
   law without additional structure.
8. Recompute the Gaussian-regulated integral and its limiting regimes from
   the defining integral. Check width convention, normalization, positivity
   or sign changes, small/large-separation limits, removal of the regulator,
   and whether the Gaussian is a physical smooth core, an algebraic filter,
   or only an oracle comparator.
9. Audit the verifier by mapping each assertion to the exact formula it
   evaluates and using the smallest sign/normalization/parity mutation that

typed regulator and domain; no interaction automatically becomes a particle

## Joined oracle and verdict discipline

For each verifier/API predicate opened after activation, the review will
record

```text
computed predicate
-> exact mathematical proposition
-> licensed domain and source
-> remaining analytic closure
-> maximum verdict
-> effect on Unit A or Unit B only
```

The exact verifiers may establish VSH contractions, Fourier constants,
Gaussian integrals, stationary residuals, or weighted scaling identities.
Focused tests may establish importable API behavior. Neither substitutes for
the top Hölder mapping, transport commutators, common-lifespan approximation,
global stationarity, or coadjoint admissibility. Initial failed/repair receipts
remain valuable provenance and will be classified rather than erased; only
the final claim boundary receives the final verdict.

The final review will:

- give Unit A and Unit B separate verdicts;
- give stationary Routes A, B, and affine propagation Route C separate
  route-scoped verdicts;
- preserve the strongest exact VSH, Fourier, mechanical cross-kernel,
  Gaussian, Hodge, or local-evolution result supported;
- state any unsupported extension, the minimum correction, and the exact
  construction that would restore it;
- request at most one bounded correction package if claim-bearing artifacts
  disagree with the strongest supported result; and
- preserve all mechanical/KKS, particle, P5, electron, neutrino, and `0074`
  exclusions.

No chronology, test count, or source name creates authority. Conversely, a
failure of stationarity or coadjoint interpretation cannot weaken an exact
tail, interaction, or affine local-Euler theorem whose own analytic closure
passes.
