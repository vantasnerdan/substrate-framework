# Source inventory before new bodies

After direct action calculation, register Gallay--Smets, On the linear
stability of vortex columns in the energy space, arXiv:1811.07584v2
(2019-06-18), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.07584v2 . Abstract only before this
record: subexponential growth on finite-energy perturbations for a specified
profile class, not necessarily power boundedness or nonlinear stability.
Check strict/weak profile hypotheses and compact-support closure before
importing anything to0027's flat-core/flat-edge column.

Also inventory their Spectral stability of inviscid columnar vortices,
arXiv:1805.05064 and primary author PDF
https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~gallay/SpecStab.pdf . Search excerpts
name a closure class that can include compact support, but its exact topology,
mode quantifiers and energy-space consequence remain to be checked at source.
A spectral result for the column is not stability of the solitary excitation.

Wang, On the nonlinear stability of inviscid axisymmetric swirling flows in a
pipe of finite length, Physics of Fluids21(2009), DOI10.1063/1.3194305,
primary institutional preprint https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/deptdb/dept_reports/558.pdf
is inventoried only for the energy-Casimir method and actual finite-pipe/
periodic boundaries. It does not silently replace the unbounded domain.
