# P253/0055: action-scale and causal-cone selection from Euler

Root preregisters a P4 foundation test after `0043/0047`, `0046/0050`, and
`0051` isolated the physical state/analyzer ingredients.  This attempt asks a
separate question which no finite-dimensional algebra can answer: can
constant-density incompressible Euler select a universal nonzero action unit
and a relativistic finite propagation cone for the same localized carrier?

The target is an exact structural theorem, not a numerical fit.  No measured
electron/neutrino value, `hbar`, light speed, circulation quantum, equation of
state, stochastic law, or detector rate is an input.  Accepted carrier,
KKS/action, retained-state, and finite-band results enter only at their pinned
scopes.  Active `0052/0054` carrier work is excluded.

## Frozen transformations and observables

Start from the exact Euler similarity

    u_(A,B)(x,t)=A u(Bx,ABt),       A,B>0,

at fixed material density.  Derive the scaling of energy, impulse, angular
momentum/KKS action, helicity, circulation, length, time, and every proposed
topological class.  Test whether any existing discrete topology fixes the
continuous action, or whether it only labels components while similarity
moves the action inside each component.

Separately derive the pressure and Hodge/Biot--Savart response to a compact
vorticity perturbation.  Determine whether a localized perturbation has a
strict domain of dependence for the full velocity/pressure observables, and
distinguish an exact full-field cone from a finite group velocity on a
restricted band with algebraic exterior leakage.

## Route A: selection by conserved Euler labels

Test circulation, helicity, impulse, angular momentum, energy, topology, and
their combinations.  A route succeeds only if the allowed autonomous carrier
dynamics selects one nonzero action value across a natural neighborhood,
rather than accepting a continuously chosen invariant from initial data.
If two dimensionful conserved values can fix a scale, identify their physical
selection mechanism and prove it is universal rather than a family label.

## Route B: structured-background band

Use an exact same-substrate periodic or localized background only if it
constructs a positive carrier band with controlled pressure and complement.
Derive its dispersion and group velocity.  Then quantify the full-field tail
outside the nominal band cone.  A bounded group velocity earns a controlled
effective signal sector; it is not exact Lorentz covariance while the Hodge
and pressure fields retain instantaneous algebraic response.

For a lattice of neutral cells, calculate the leading far multipole.  If the
coupling decays as `r^-3` in three dimensions, test the summed long-range tail
rather than importing a short-range finite-cone theorem.  Multipole
cancellation or a faster-decaying exact cell is a competing route.

## Route C: minimal explicit foundation extension

If bare Euler cannot select either structure, compare the smallest extensions
without hiding them in notation:

1. a dimensionful circulation/action law or microscopic stochastic action,
   including its state space and probability rule;
2. a hyperbolic compressible or relativistic substrate law with a finite
   characteristic speed and its incompressible limit; and
3. a scale-selecting boundary/background/constitutive energy with its new
   parameters and symmetry cost.

Each receives a conditional implication and a parameter/input ledger.  The
attempt will test whether one addition supplies both action and causal
structure or whether independent laws are required.  It will not silently
replace the frozen incompressible Euler foundation; any adopted extension
would require a separate foundational proposal and owner agreement.

## Exact oracle and verdict boundary

The strongest oracle is dimensional and group-theoretic: explicit transformed
fields and integrals, a same-topology continuous scaling family, pressure/
Biot--Savart kernel asymptotics, and a compactly supported exposing
perturbation.  An importable API will encode these transformations and reject
dimensionally inconsistent claims.  No production numerics are licensed.

The result may establish route-scoped impossibility for topology-only action
selection or a strict full-field cone without becoming a P4 or particle
no-go.  Structured invariant sectors and explicit extended foundations remain
positive candidates.  LP4 requires the state, measurement, action, exchange,
and propagation mechanisms to meet on one carrier; this attempt addresses
only the action/causal conjuncts.
