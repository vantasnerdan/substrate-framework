## Frozen substantive checks

The review will pin the hashes of both source bodies, every load-bearing exact
script, and the existing receipts, then check the following once at that
boundary:

1. **One actual Euler state.** Verify that the two rings and every perturbed
   datum are one axisymmetric no-swirl divergence-free Euler datum on the stated
   domain, not two independently evolved solutions or an assigned center ODE.
   Track physical density, relative-vorticity measure, circulation, impulse,
   energy, signs, boundary/axis regularity, and the translation quotient.
2. **Exact Cao-to-Buttà--Marchioro map.** Distinguish all core parameters and
   prove the claimed amplitude, space, and time rescaling. In particular, map
   the Cao circulation/profile/speed and small-core parameter to the
   Buttà--Marchioro size `epsilon`, intensity of order
   `|log epsilon|^-1`, support bounds, center/radius data, and observation time.
   A shared symbol or dimensional analogy is not a transfer.
3. **Deficit and modulus.** Re-derive the sign and normalization of the exact
   conserved energy/impulse deficit, every cross or support-error term, and the
   logical inversion of the Cao compactness/stability modulus. Check all
   quantifiers and any uniformity in core size, separation, family parameters,
   or time. Uniqueness or qualitative compactness must not be promoted to an
   unstated coercive Hessian, rate, or additive two-body gap.
4. **Real relative-open neighborhood.** Identify the precise topology and
   admissible same-leaf class. Exhibit or prove a relatively open set containing
   genuine shape/redistribution perturbations not generated solely by common or
   independent axial translations. Verify positivity, rearrangement/label,
   weighted-integrability, support, separation, and conserved-quantity
   constraints without future-history preparation.
5. **Support and relative control.** Separate sharp support localization from
   mass concentration and orbital norm control. Check that the hypotheses keep
   both cores away from the symmetry axis and mutually disjoint for the full
   claimed interval, and that the chosen centers and relative velocity are
   derived from the Euler vorticity rather than imposed. Track which estimates
   are exact, asymptotic as `epsilon -> 0`, or only modulus statements.
6. **Time endpoint.** State the interval with its full quantifier order,
   epsilon dependence, collision/separation condition, and any Euler scaling.
   A fixed small-positive interval, an arbitrary fixed interval, and an all-time
   statement are distinct. No continuation beyond the primary theorem or the
   proved support-control endpoint is inferred.
7. **Oracle sensitivity.** Inspect exact algebra and theorem applicability as
   the strongest oracles. Mutate one load-bearing sign/deficit normalization,
   one scaling exponent or `|log epsilon|` factor, and one support/time
   hypothesis where the existing verifier is intended to detect them. Passing
   scripts are regression evidence unless they derive the checked predicate.
