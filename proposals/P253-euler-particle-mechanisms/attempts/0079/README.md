## Physical doublet conventions

Let `E_R` be the real four-dimensional spectral subspace and let
`J:E_R->E_R` be the compatible complex structure fixed by the positive
relative Hessian and KKS form, with

    J^2=-1,
    A_0|_(E_R)=omega_* J,
    E_2(x,y)=Omega_KKS(x,Jy)>0.                           (1)

Its complex positive-frequency space `E_+` must have complex dimension two.
For a real perturbation generator `V`, freeze the convention

    V_C=(V-J V J)/2,
    V_A=(V+J V J)/2.                                    (2)

Then `[V_C,J]=0` and `{V_A,J}=0`.  `V_C` is the number/action-preserving
complex-linear part; `V_A` is the Bogoliubov/squeezing part.  With physical
KKS-orthonormal basis `e_1,e_2` of `E_+`, define

    H_a=-J P V_C^(a)P,
    H_a=h_(a,0) 1+h_a dot sigma,                         (3)

where `H_a` is Hermitian in the positive energy-action metric.  The exact
noncommuting-control predicate is

    h_1 cross h_2 !=0.                                  (4)

Two nonzero matrices, two static eigenvalue shifts, or two collinear Pauli
vectors do not satisfy (4).
