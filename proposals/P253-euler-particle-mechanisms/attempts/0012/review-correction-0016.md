# P253/0012 correction receipt from independent review 0016

Date: 2026-09-06

The independent 0016 audit supports the same-family finite-time theorem and
requires one minimum topology correction:

- “relative-open” now means the quotient/relative topology induced by smooth
  \(d\nu\)-preserving diffeomorphisms supported in the prescribed initial
  tubes and small in \(C^1\);
- the open set is centered at a specifically chosen diffeomorphic image
  outside the maximizing translation orbit \(\Sigma\), rather than at the
  undeformed carrier;
- merely choosing a generator that is not an axial translation is no longer
  used to infer deformation, because a field tangent to carrier level sets
  can leave the vorticity unchanged;
- no openness is claimed in the full \(L^1+L^2+P(|\cdot|)\) Cao orbital
  topology.

The quantitative deficit, impulse-exchange, cross-energy, support, scaling,
and time formulas are unchanged. The existing exact oracle was therefore not
rerun.
