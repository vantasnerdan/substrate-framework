## Unit A check 1: exact carrier map and centralizer

1. State the physical Euler field, carrier domain, vorticity, velocity,
   pressure/Hodge closure, regularity, support, density, and parameter range.
2. Write the carrier map and its inverse with every coordinate, Jacobian,
   orientation, periodicity, and boundary/collar condition. Verify whether it
   is a coordinate identity, a diffeomorphism, a volume-preserving map, or a
   coadjoint action; these meanings cannot be interchanged.
3. Derive the pushforward/pullback of velocity, vorticity forms, divergence,
   curl, pressure, and the KKS pairing. Check sign and density normalization.
4. Define the centralizer in the actual transformation group. Compute its
   identity component, discrete components, stabilizer, and action on each
   connected regular carrier level. Separate algebraic commutation from an
   actual globally defined Euler symmetry.
5. Verify injectivity, surjectivity, and smooth dependence only in the spaces
   stated by the target. Axis, critical levels, separatrices, support edges,
   and disconnected positive-core components receive separate treatment.

**Maximum verdict:** the exact carrier-map and centralizer theorem on its
declared regular domain.

## Unit B check 2: supervisor collar-sequence topology test

    Then calculate the ordinary vorticity-generator graph term

domain, topology, and parameter quantifiers. Accepted suppliers enter only at
their registered scope. A familiar Hodge, centralizer, Bogovskii, or normal-
