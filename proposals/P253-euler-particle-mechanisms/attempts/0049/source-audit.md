# Source and transfer audit for the detector bridge

The accepted inputs are deliberately narrow.

- `C-CST-018` supplies prepared finite-window classical optical amplitudes,
  a positive canonical quadratic action in actual observed initial variables,
  and full material momentum/angular current. It does not supply an invariant
  mode space, a positive detection current, a detector, or stochastic events.
- `C-CST-008` supplies a stationary spatial marked selection and compact
  isovortical KKS pairs. Its Poisson marking is a construction of a spatial
  ensemble of good cells. It is not a theorem about temporal exponential
  hitting times of material trajectories.
- `P253/0042/0045` supplies the exact local retained Euler state and causal
  unresolved response `W[v,w0]`. It says how a proposed detector must retain
  its unresolved initial state; it does not close that state to a random clock.
- `P253/0043` and `0046` supply author-level sufficiency boundaries for
  classical Hilbert/KKS structures. Their pending/completed reviews are kept
  separate, and neither is treated as a measurement theorem.

The independent-exponential-clock and uniform-threshold calculations below
are elementary probability identities proved directly. No literature theorem
is imported to claim that Euler generates the required clock law. A future
deterministic rare-event route would need a primary-source theorem whose
hypotheses are checked on the same detector flow; none is imported here.

The threshold convention is `lambda<=I` with
`F(x)=Prob(lambda<=x)`, including atoms.  The sequential reset is explicitly
open-system/coarse-grained or external: reversible measure-preserving Euler on
a finite invariant phase volume cannot have an autonomous many-to-one time
map.  Reversible first-exit/scattering with escaped degrees retained remains a
separate same-substrate candidate.
