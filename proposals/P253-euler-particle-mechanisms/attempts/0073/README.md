## Fixed claim layers and separate verdicts

The review will keep the following layers separate:

1. the exact regular-cell constrained DA Weyl/singular-sequence construction;
2. the operator-theoretic bridge from that construction to the explicitly
   chosen Fredholm essential spectrum;
3. fixed-mode compactness and finite-row stability;
4. nonlinear chart/norm equivalence and full Piola/Hodge transfer;
5. isolation of proposed modes from the full constrained column spectrum; and
6. fixed-domain nonlinear uniqueness and any resulting gap/doublet/equation
   (38) conclusion.

A defect or open dependency in a later layer cannot erase an independently
proved earlier layer. Conversely, an exact cell recurrence, symbolic identity,
or singular sequence cannot inherit a full spectral gap or nonlinear branch.
Each of the eight units below receives an explicit finding; the final YAML
will separately identify the strongest established theorem and every remaining
conditional dependency.

## Unit 1: positive-part interface bootstrap to `C^{2,alpha}`

1. Freeze the fixed-domain equation, coefficient regularity, boundary and
   interface, spatial dimension, exponent `p`, admissible `q`, and the exact
   range of `alpha`. Identify which constants are uniform over the claimed
   parameter/cell family.
2. Require the actual bootstrap across the positive-part interface. First
   obtain a uniform `W^{2,q}` estimate with `q>3`; use Morrey to obtain the
   required `C^{1,alpha}` control; then prove that the complete right-hand side,
   including its positive-part composition and transformed coefficients, is
   `C^{0,alpha}` across the zero interface; only then apply the applicable
   boundary/interior Schauder theorem to obtain `C^{2,alpha}`.
3. Check compatibility at the interface, physical boundary, symmetry axis,
   collar, and exterior matching surface. Derivatives of the positive-part
   nonlinearity must not hide a jump or distribution, and coordinate changes
   must retain the stated Hölder class.
4. A `C^1` shortcut, a pointwise composition assertion, or Schauder invoked
   before Hölder control of the full right-hand side does not prove this unit.

**Maximum verdict:** the exact uniform `C^{2,alpha}` bootstrap on the declared
fixed-domain positive-part problem.

## Unit 2: Fredholm convention and left-parametrix bridge

5. State the closed densely defined operator `A:D(A) subset X -> X`, its graph
   norm, the constrained DA subspace/quotient, and the precise essential
   spectrum convention: Fredholm, left Fredholm, semi-Fredholm, Weyl, Browder,
   or another explicitly defined notion.
6. Recheck the proposed singular sequence in that exact space: unit lower
   norm, uniformly bounded graph norm, weak convergence to zero, satisfaction
   of all constraints, and `(A-lambda)u_j -> 0` in `X`.
7. For a non-self-adjoint operator, derive rather than assume the implication
   from that weak-null graph singular sequence to the selected essential
   spectrum. If the route is failure of left Fredholmness, exhibit the
   left-parametrix/compact-remainder statement in the chosen domain and show
   explicitly that applying it to the sequence contradicts its normalization.
   Treat range closure, finite kernel/cokernel, quotient representatives, and
   compactness in the correct topology.
8. Distinguish approximate point spectrum or failure to be bounded below from
   full Fredholm essential spectrum whenever the convention requires more.

**Maximum verdict:** the exact regular-cell constrained DA essential-spectrum
inclusion under the stated Fredholm convention. This is the core result to
preserve unless a concrete failure occurs in its own construction or bridge.

## Unit 3: fixed-`n` nonzero low-frequency compactness

9. Freeze one nonzero integer Fourier label `n`; do not use an estimate proved
   only for fixed `n` uniformly as `|n|` grows or at `n=0`.
10. Prove compact DA support for every relevant profile. For the induced
    compact curl/vorticity source, derive zero spatial mean and a uniformly
    controlled first moment. Use those rows to control the Fourier multiplier
    at `k=0`; Biot--Savart is not an unconditional inhomogeneous
    `H^{s-1}->H^s` gain there.
11. Establish the local one-derivative Biot--Savart/Hodge gain in the exact
    spaces, control the exterior tail and pressure/Leray contribution, and use
    Rellich only on a genuinely compact restriction. Combine the local and
    tail pieces to prove compactness on the declared constrained graph domain.
12. Check that finite circulation, displacement, centering, or other rows are
    preserved and that the compactness statement survives the quotient rather
    than merely a chosen representative.

**Maximum verdict:** fixed-nonzero-mode compactness on the exact constrained
DA graph domain.

## Unit 4: canonical action-angle chart and finite-row profiles

13. Verify that the regular cell admits a genuine canonical volume
    action-angle chart, not merely convenient streamline labels. Derive the
    volume form/Jacobian, angle periods, orientation, action normalization,
    global cell coverage, and transition behavior near excluded boundaries.
14. Require integer Fourier labels `m,n` with the target's actual periodic
    variables. Check single-valuedness and axis/interface regularity and type
    each oscillatory profile in the common operator domain `D(A)`.
15. Audit the high-frequency regime, including which integer is `N`, the
    parameter `epsilon`, the order of limits, and the condition
    `N*epsilon -> infinity`. Track all derivatives and errors uniformly in
    that regime.
16. Any finite-row removal must use profiles at the same fixed nonzero `n` and
    in the same graph domain as the singular sequence. Prove that the row
    correctors preserve support, constraints, normalization, weak-nullness,
    and vanishing residual; do not silently change Fourier sector or domain.

**Maximum verdict:** a globally valid regular-cell canonical chart and an
integer-mode graph-domain Weyl construction with exact finite-row control.

## Unit 5: nonlinear weighted quotient/chart and reverse Hardy boundary

17. Distinguish the linear tangent norm from the metric/topology on the
    nonlinear orbit or displacement quotient. State the stabilizer quotient,
    slice, weights, support restrictions, and the actual nonlinear chart map.
18. Prove both directions of the claimed local norm equivalence with uniform
    constants. A weighted vorticity estimate in one direction does not show
    that the nonlinear quotient chart controls the displacement norm.
19. At the support/interface boundary where the base coefficient vanishes,
    derive the needed reverse Hardy or equivalent interface estimate with its
    boundary trace, vanishing order, Sobolev index, and coefficient regularity.
    Test collar-concentrated divergence-free displacements orthogonal to the
    stabilizer; support separation from exterior stabilizers and nonzero
    toroidal-harmonic rows must be handled explicitly.
20. If only the weighted-orbit topology or an ambient-vorticity sandwiched
    trace survives, state it positively and separately from the ordinary
    nonlinear displacement quotient. Failure of a stronger topology does not
    weaken an exact weighted tangent theorem.

**Maximum verdict:** the strongest two-sided local chart/quotient statement
actually supported, with weaker weighted or sandwiched topologies preserved as
independent positive results.

## Unit 6: full-vector Piola/Hodge curl-div ledger

21. Write the complete fixed-domain Piola transform for vector components,
    Jacobian, density, inverse metric, curl and divergence. Track the coordinate
    basis and axis compatibility rather than substituting a scalar model for a
    vector identity.
22. Expand every transformed curl, divergence, Hodge/Biot--Savart, pressure,
    Leray, and commutator term. Record all contributions proportional to
    `delta*n`, `delta*n^2`, or derivatives of the chart. At the load-bearing
    scaling `n` comparable to `1/delta`, no `delta*n` term may be hidden in an
    `O(delta)` remainder derived for fixed `n`.
23. Prove the maps at the positive-part interface, symmetry axis, collar,
    exterior domain, and matching surface. Include harmonic/zero-frequency
    rows, decay/tails, boundary conditions, and compatibility of inside and
    outside Hodge inverses.
24. Establish the exact `D(A)` graph-norm-to-`X` control used by the operator
    comparison, including full pressure and constraints. A scalar residual or
    local coefficient estimate does not prove the required operator bound.

**Maximum verdict:** a uniform full-vector fixed-domain operator transfer at
the stated high-frequency scaling.

## Unit 7: isolation from the entire constrained column spectrum

25. State the proposed `m=0` mode(s), eigenvalue(s), multiplicities, Krein
    signs, and constraint rows on one common analytic domain.
26. Compare them with the entire constrained full-column spectrum, not merely
    a selected scalar block or one Fourier sector. Include all azimuthal and
    axial sectors, pressure/Hodge coupling, stabilizer and symmetry modes,
    point/continuous/essential spectrum, and domain-dependent boundary or
    exterior modes.
27. Prove a uniform isolating contour or gap in the same operator topology in
    which continuation is claimed. Check resolvent bounds, parameter
    continuity, spectral pollution, and possible crossings with translation,
    rotation, relabeling, or other neutral branches.
28. A local dispersion calculation, positive quadratic form, or isolated
    reduced-block eigenvalue is valuable evidence but does not isolate a mode
    from the full constrained spectrum.

**Maximum verdict:** full-column spectral isolation only if the common-domain
resolvent/gap proof closes; otherwise the proposed gap and doublet remain
explicit conditional continuations.

## Unit 8: fixed-domain `C^2` uniqueness and global regular cells

29. Specify the nonlinear fixed-domain map, domain/codomain, parameters,
    constraints, and the norms in which it is twice continuously
    differentiable. Check this against the interface bootstrap and every
    Piola/Hodge derivative actually used.
30. State exactly what uniqueness means: local uniqueness in one chart,
    uniqueness modulo symmetries/stabilizer, uniqueness within one cell, or a
    global family statement. Derive the applicable inverse/Fredholm and
    cokernel conditions rather than inferring uniqueness from regularity.
31. Cover every claimed regular cell on one compatible atlas and prove the
    constants remain uniform. Define `omega_min`, show it is strictly positive
    on the entire relevant parameter/cell set, and justify compactness or
    endpoint control needed to pass from pointwise positivity to a global
    minimum.
32. Audit overlaps, interface/axis/exterior boundaries, excluded critical
    cells, and parameter endpoints. Local `C^2` regularity or local uniqueness
    cannot by itself establish global regular-cell uniqueness or a uniform
    `omega_min`.

**Maximum verdict:** the precise fixed-domain differentiability and uniqueness
theorem actually proved. Any consequence for a full gap, a doublet, or
equation (38) remains conditional on Units 5--7 as well as this unit.
