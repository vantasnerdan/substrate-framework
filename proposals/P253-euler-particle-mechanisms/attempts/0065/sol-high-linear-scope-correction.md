# Sol-High pre-review linear-scope correction

The supervisor audit found that the first author draft overextended a valid
Fourier representation theorem from translation-invariant linear isotropic
multipliers to nonlinear/configuration-dependent topological maps. The
claim-bearing files now restrict that theorem to nonzero wavevectors on a
declared tempered/finite-energy multiplier domain. Contact distributions at
`k=0` and nonlinear/configuration-dependent maps remain candidates with their
energy, zero-mode, reciprocity and causality obligations.

The parity statement is repaired: pseudoscalar times polar wavevector is an
axial longitudinal vector. A polar target forbids it by `O(3)` parity; an axial
target permits it but the transverse projector annihilates it. The puncture
energy statement is strengthened by the sphere-flux Cauchy--Schwarz bound,
which proves the `1/epsilon` divergence for the whole fixed-flux class and is
saturated by the radial representative.

The unchanged exact oracle was not rerun. Its parity predicate is explicitly
transverse and remains correct.
