## Source and authority audit

exact version and proposition. The review will distinguish source-level Cao

coverage argument, and standard Bessel/Debye identities from the physical

## Unit A — fixed-mean-radius external-epsilon Cao path

1. Freeze `kappa` and the exact vorticity-weighted mean radius `R`; verify that
   the continued path uses precisely the `B_R` constraint rows and not the
   distinct physical-impulse `B_I` system.
2. Reconstruct the relative-epsilon Banach charts, parameter identification,
   common domain, and local uniqueness. Determine whether uniqueness is only
   chart-local or has been promoted beyond its supplier.
3. Derive the geometric area scale directly, including the coefficient and
   uniform remainder in
   `s_area=C_s epsilon+O(epsilon^2)`.
4. Check every endpoint and buffer in the `C0` IVT argument. The interval must
   be covered by actual fixed-`(kappa,R)` solutions in one common chart; an
   asymptotic sign at an unavailable endpoint does not prove coverage.
5. Separate exact local continuation and finite buffered coverage from any
   conditional quantitative range or global uniqueness statement.

**Unit A verdict:** adjudicate the exact external-epsilon solution path and
the actual buffered coverage interval at the accepted `0084 B_R` boundary.

## Unit B — response reduction and physical normalization frontier

6. Reconstruct the full variation giving the response coefficient and check
   all pressure, boundary, Hodge, density, sign, and normalization terms in
   the reduction to `integral xi dot F_12`.
7. Verify which tangent, generator, and symplectic/KKS pairing the integral
   represents. An algebraic force pairing does not itself supply a physical
   action normalization.
8. Determine whether the relevant curl or pairing is proved nonzero on the
   actual constrained tangent. Preserve a correct exact reduction even when
   nonvanishing and KKS normalization remain open.

**Unit B verdict:** state the strongest exact response identity separately
from the two missing physical constructions.

## Unit C — circulation clock, Doppler maximum, and Bessel ledger

10. Maximize the full transverse Doppler ratio with its subluminal domain and

11. Derive the cylindrical/vector Fourier decomposition rather than treating

    frequency factors.
12. Recheck the applicable Debye inequality, its order/argument regime,
    constants, endpoint buffer, and whether it bounds amplitudes, energy, or
    flux.

**Unit C verdict:** adjudicate the exact physical clock, sharp kinematic
ceiling, and vector-Bessel suppression independently.

## Unit D — fixed radial-index route
