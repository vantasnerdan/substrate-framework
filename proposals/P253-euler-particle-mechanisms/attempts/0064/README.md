# P253/0064: electron charge and Coulomb-coupling mechanism on a localized Euler carrier

## Frozen parent and rung

The parent remains the exact Issue #203 electron-and-neutrino mechanism, with
the electron developed first. This attempt addresses the P3/P5 charge and
long-range-interaction rung for one smooth localized finite-energy or
finite-excess-energy Euler excitation. It does not reduce P2 persistence, P4
quantum/relativistic structure, magnetic coupling, the quantitative electron
relation, or any neutrino obligation.

## Exact question

Determine whether a conserved signed observable of one localized smooth Euler
carrier can source a Coulomb-scale interaction from the same incompressible
Euler velocity, pressure, action, or an already accepted collective background
mode. The target must include:

1. a signed carrier observable with a physical opposite-sign counterpart;
2. a far interaction energy proportional to `q_1 q_2/d` and force proportional
   to `q_1 q_2/d^2`, or an explicitly controlled limit with those powers;
3. the corresponding same-field current and pressure/action work;
4. a statement of every background and preparation hypothesis.

Circulation, helicity, impulse, a pressure coefficient, or a tag is not called
electric charge unless the interaction and current map are derived.

## Frozen conventions and domain

Use constant density `rho>0` and smooth divergence-free Euler fields on
`R^3`. Isolated candidates have finite energy or finite excess energy above a
specified common background. Compact vorticity has its decaying whole-space
Biot--Savart velocity with all harmonic and boundary rows retained. A
punctured domain, source/sink, singular filament, imposed gauge field, or
massless collective background is a distinct candidate hypothesis and is not
silently identified with smooth source-free Euler on `R^3`.

For compact vorticity `omega`, use

    u(x)=(4*pi)^-1 integral omega(y) cross (x-y)/|x-y|^3 dy.

For pressure use

    p=rho*(-Delta)^-1 partial_i partial_j(u_i u_j),

with the positive `(-Delta)^-1` convention fixed in P253/0042. Interaction
energy is the cross term `rho integral u_1 dot u_2`; force is the derivative
under relative translation only when that differentiation is justified.

## Candidate routes frozen before derivation

### Route A: compact-vorticity Biot--Savart charge

Derive the complete far multipole expansion including the zero total
vorticity identity, hydrodynamic impulse, and the first nonzero velocity term.
Compute the leading two-carrier cross energy and translation derivative with
convergent boundary terms. Test whether any signed conserved scalar produces
the Coulomb `d^-1` energy and `d^-2` force. Distinguish a route-scoped power-law
refutation from a no-go for collective-background mechanisms.

### Route B: smooth harmonic flux monopole

Test the only direct `u=O(r^-2)` flux field that could mimic a three-dimensional
monopole. Apply divergence theorem, smoothness, finite-energy, and whole-space
Hodge constraints. If nonzero flux requires a source singularity, puncture, or
additional continuity equation, record that exact foundation change and do
not import it into Euler.

### Route C: pressure-Poisson monopole or dipole

Integrate the exact pressure source and its first moments, retaining the
surface conditions. Determine the first permitted pressure far multipole for
general localized Euler data and the stronger stationary compact-velocity
cancellation from P253/0060. Test the resulting force power and sign law.

### Route D: collective massless background mediator

If A--C cannot yield Coulomb scaling, test the accepted C-CST-018 same-field
collective action only at its exact prepared finite-window/second-variation
scope. A positive route must construct a static massless Green channel on one
physical background, a localized carrier-to-channel source functional with
opposite signs, the reciprocal action/current work, and a controlled transfer
from the prepared response to the actual carrier interaction. A formal
`1/|K|^2` inverse or an assigned defect charge is insufficient.

Failure of D activates a materially different explicit foundation candidate
rather than converting the route failure into a parent no-go.

## Selection criteria

Compare routes by exact Euler closure, localization, signed opposite state,
interaction exponent, reciprocal energy/action, current conservation,
assumption cost, background dependence, compatibility with the persistent
carrier, and predictive consequences for magnetic coupling. No empirical
charge or fine-structure number is used as a derivation input.

## Exact oracle plan

An analytic verifier will derive rather than hard-code:

- zero total vorticity for smooth compact support and the leading Biot--Savart
  moment contraction;
- the absence or presence of pressure source monopole and dipole moments;
- the flux obstruction for a smooth divergence-free whole-space monopole;
- the powers of `d` in the first permitted cross energy and force.

No production numerics are licensed. The small-ratio skill is inapplicable
unless a later route freezes a weak splitting or force coefficient near a
numerical floor.

## Success and route verdicts

Each route receives exactly one route-scoped verdict. Success requires the
same-field signed observable, opposite state, Coulomb power, reciprocal
current/action coupling, and domain hypotheses. A multipole obstruction in
smooth isolated Euler leaves Route D and explicit foundation alternatives
active. Nothing in this attempt alone establishes electric charge, an
electron, a neutrino, P5, or parent completion.
