# P253/0043: physical quantum state, observable, exchange, and dynamics bridge

Root preregisters one concrete P4 construction on the exact Euler
coadjoint-orbit inputs established in `0005/0011` and the exact classical
field/action boundaries established in `0013/0018`. The carrier slot is the
same persistent localized family sought by P2; conditional calculations may
use its finite nonzero internal angular momentum `j`, but may not replace it
with an unrelated spin texture or prescribed target history. The purpose is
to determine which quantum/statistical structure follows from Euler and which
additional physical axiom, if any, would be unavoidable.

Earlier source bodies and familiar quantum facts were exposed before this
attempt; no comparator blinding is claimed. No particle name, measured
constant, or electron/neutrino value may enter the derivation. `hbar`, a Born
rule, and a choice of exchange character are outputs to derive or explicit
external postulates to expose, never silent units.

## Frozen target object

Construct on one and two copies of the same localized Euler carrier:

1. a state space with a declared physical equivalence relation and norm;
2. an observable algebra containing the actual Euler energy, momentum,
   internal rotation, position/tag observables, and their domains;
3. a time evolution induced by the same Euler Hamiltonian, including its
   linearity or nonlinearity on states;
4. a probability/measurement rule and a composition rule for two separated
   carriers;
5. the unordered two-carrier configuration space, an actual exchange path,
   and the resulting line-bundle holonomy or representation;
6. an action normalization and a mechanism selecting its integral class; and
7. a collective energy-momentum law with either a derived finite invariant
   speed or an exact obstruction from the Galilean incompressible substrate.

A result earns LP4 only if these are physically joined on the same carrier
sector. A formal complex coordinate, prequantum line, Koopman Hilbert space,
or projective representation earns its exact mathematical scope and no more.

## Route A: Euler Koopman and classical probability

On an invariant carrier phase space with an Euler-invariant measure, construct
the Koopman space, multiplication observables, and unitary pullback evolution.
Test whether its observable algebra is commutative, whether phases have any
physical interference observable, and whether its norm supplies more than a
classical probability law. This route receives a positive verdict for every
exact state/observable/dynamics statement it establishes. It satisfies P4
only if noncommuting observables, composition, measurement, and exchange are
derived rather than renamed classical ensemble structure.

## Route B: geometric quantization of the physical internal orbit

For the actual compact internal symplectic orbit, begin from the physical KKS
form and its period, construct the prequantum bundle when its integral class
exists, choose or derive a polarization, and calculate the resulting Hilbert
space and rotation representation. The calculation must keep

    (1/(2*pi*hbar)) integral_(S2) Omega = 2j/hbar = N.

It must then test the continuous Euler scaling of `j`, distinguish existence
of an arbitrary integer `N` from selection of odd `N` or `N=1`, and identify
whether a polarization follows from carrier dynamics. Setting `hbar=2j` is a
definition, not scale selection, unless a separate same-substrate mechanism
fixes both sides.

For two identical separated carriers, construct the ordered and unordered
configuration spaces and pull the physical line bundle around a realized
exchange loop. Determine whether the Euler bundle itself fixes the trivial or
sign character. Merely observing that the unordered configuration space
admits a `Z2` character does not select fermionic statistics.

## Route C: positive-frequency wave/action quantization

On a carrier sector with a positive periodic physical metric, construct the
real symplectic linearized phase space, its dynamically selected complex
structure if one exists, the one-particle Hilbert completion, and the induced
quadratic Hamiltonian. Compare its classical wave amplitudes with a quantum
state: derive or expose the missing rule for discrete action, superposition,
measurement, and tensor/Fock composition. The hyperbolic Gavrilov sector from
`0032/0038/0039` is an adverse comparator, not the positive input. A different
stable carrier may supply the needed positive sector.

## Route D: relativistic collective dynamics

Compute the exact Poisson algebra and dispersion of collective translation
and internal modes. Test Galilean boosts and the incompressible pressure
constraint directly. A finite-speed Lorentz or Dirac limit must arise from a
same-substrate background/band structure with controlled errors and must be
joined to Routes B or C. An imposed wave speed or leading asymptotic cone is
not an exact relativistic bridge. If exact Lorentz symmetry is obstructed,
append a failure-derived effective-band candidate with its approximation
order and physical regime stated.

## Verification and verdict boundary

The strongest oracle is an exact finite-dimensional orbit/bundle calculation,
an explicit two-carrier exchange pullback, and a direct algebraic comparison
of Euler/Koopman observables with the proposed quantum observable algebra.
Any reusable orbit, line-bundle, or action-selection calculation belongs in
an importable module with exposing tests. Numerical spectra are outside this
attempt until an analytic reduction leaves one declared small remainder.

Each route terminates separately as established, refuted with its mechanism,
or blocked on a named construction. Failure of an automatic derivation does
not prove that no augmented substrate mechanism exists; it activates a new
candidate. This attempt does not identify an electron or neutrino, infer
charge from Lamb neutrality, assume LP2/LP3, or complete P253.
