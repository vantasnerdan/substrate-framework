## Frozen review method

### Unit A — primary-source Hill elimination and center coefficient

1. Read the actual HF primary source at its pinned version and reconstruct its
   hypotheses, variables, independent variable, sign conventions and domain.
   Determine whether the target's scalar Hill elimination is a literal
   consequence of that system or an additional approximation.
2. Recompute the smooth-center indicial system and invariant paired coefficient
   `15/256`. Check every normalization, eliminated component, regular branch,
   conjugate/reciprocal pairing and center regularity condition. Audit the HF
   post-Equation-20 nonreturned-covector saturation warning: a local Hill
   exponent does not itself imply a returned physical covector or iterated
   observable growth. Determine exactly why the proposed
   `d(F(P)+ell theta)` phase removes that saturation, including global
   single-valuedness through its integer character/frequency. A symbolic
   factorization is algebraic evidence only unless the source-to-Euler
   hypotheses are also satisfied.

### Unit B — exact finite-Cao Floquet calculus

3. Reconstruct the exact finite-Cao first-integral reduction on a compact
   interior circuit, including a globally single-valued integer material
   phase, period, orientation and all density/geometric factors.
4. Verify that the transported covector returns in the declared physical
   frame. Separate genuine monodromy from coordinate-frame rotation, and check
   continuous detuning removal rather than absorbing a nonperiodic phase into
   the state.
5. Derive the exact reciprocal Floquet multiplier and identify the symplectic,
   determinant-one or Wronskian identity that enforces reciprocity. State its
   graph/domain and parameter scope, keeping every leading asymptotic separate
   from the exact multiplier.

### Unit C — compact-interior finite-Cao transfer

6. Check the fixed-member transfer of the center coefficient and Hill remainder
   to an actual finite-Cao carrier on compact support strictly inside the
   regular core. Verify the chart, coefficient regularity, support separation,
   precise thin-`delta` and compact regular-orbit quantifiers, and error
   estimate.
7. Distinguish a fixed-carrier/fixed-circuit asymptotic from a uniform theorem
   over circuits, charge, thinness or the free-boundary collar. No endpoint or
   uniform-`C^1` theorem may be imported. Preserve the strongest
   coefficient/remainder theorem actually proved.

### Unit D — constrained packet and weighted-global input

8. Reconstruct the high-frequency packet in the exact dynamically accessible
   vorticity/tag tangent: divergence/curl and Hodge rows, transported tag,
   Gauss constraints, compact support, zero/first moments and the full-pressure
   tail.
9. Audit the A2 weighted-global input topology. Verify the weight, Sobolev
   order, affine/low-frequency rows, weak-null property, graph-domain
   normalization and density of compact-curl packets in the declared domain. A
   locally small residual cannot substitute for membership and control in the
   stated global input space.

### Unit E — Maxwell/Gauss corrector and common-graph propagation

10. Derive the full constrained transverse-Maxwell Sylvester corrector and
    longitudinal Gauss reconstruction, including signs, projections, charge
    continuity, low-frequency behavior, Hodge/Leray rows and collar tails.
11. Check that the Euler, tag and Maxwell components share one closed graph
    domain and that the Egorov/Duhamel remainder is small in the claimed graph
    norm after every corrector. Track all derivative losses, the strict
    noncharacteristic hypothesis and circuit-count dependence; do not infer a
    full constrained remainder from its principal symbol.

### Unit F — fixed-local observed essential norm

12. Freeze the operator actually tested as

        J_loc S_g(j T_*): X_in^s -> X_DA,loc^s/sym,

    where `X_in^s` is the weighted global complete constrained-state/graph
    domain, and `J_loc` is one fixed local observation operator into the local
    dynamically accessible quotient while also collecting the declared
    instantaneous tag and field rows. Freeze one fixed physical observation
    window. Verify that neither the window nor `J_loc` depends on circuit count
    `j` and that the map is bounded on the common graph/input domain.
13. Prove or refute a lower bound with one constant `c_obs>0` independent of
    `j`. Packet tubes and frequency thresholds may depend on `j`, but `J_loc`,
    the physical observation region and `c_obs` may not. The claim must test
    `||J_loc S_g(t_j)||`, not demand or infer the unobserved global essential
    norm `||S_g(t_j)||_ess`; a packet escaping a moving observation window does
    not earn local observable growth.
14. Audit the weak-null sequence and compact-operator annihilation needed for
    an essential-norm statement in this exact input/output pair. Keep finite
    propagation time per circuit distinct from nonlinear all-time dynamics.

### Unit G — exact-leaf finite-row restoration

15. Reconstruct the smooth exact-leaf nonlinear path and same-`H^s`
    directional differentiation, separating formal tangency from an actual
    curve on the constrained Euler--Maxwell/material leaf.
16. Independently test the carrier-specific finite-row submersion. Require
    the claimed post-`(36bh)` independence. Require explicit compact smooth
    generator witnesses whose retained row matrix is invertible for impulse,
    center and total momentum, including the Gauss-reconstructed
    electromagnetic contribution to total momentum. Density transfers only
    row independence already proved on those witnesses.
17. If that witness matrix or its inverse estimate is absent, downgrade only
    the exact leaf-path correction. Do not use a finite-row omission to erase
    independently valid Hill, packet, graph-propagation or observation atoms.

### Unit H — fixed-thinness charge window

18. Check the quantifiers in the charged continuation: for each fixed
    sufficiently thin `delta`, determine whether there exists `g_0(delta)>0`
    such that the exact-return observed-propagator and exact-leaf-path
    obstruction persist for signed `0<|g|<g_0(delta)`, and whether that
    obstruction refutes the fixed member's uniform-Lipschitz all-time
    observable estimate.
19. Do not promote pointwise-in-`delta` continuity to a uniform wedge
    `g^2<=c delta` without an explicit uniform estimate. Check sign symmetry,
    dependence of constants and whether the carrier/domain changes with
    `delta`. This quantifier supplies no one-datum escape theorem.

### Unit I — complementary charged-column/Hill continuation

20. Independently derive the whole-core coefficient functions at every
    resonant point,

        K(s,tau)=4 Z(s,tau) R(s,tau)/Omega(s,tau)^2

    and `b(s,tau)`, with the target's exact orientation and normalization.
21. Verify separately the `ZR<0` hyperbolic sector and the resonant `K>=1`
    test. Neither finite exposing sector proves coverage of the complementary
    family.
22. Test the restored positive candidate uniform regime
    `0<kappa_0<=K<=1-eta` together with common positive-Krein signs. Identify
    the precise profile/charge/thinness domain on which those inequalities are
    proposed and what construction remains to establish them.
23. Keep the `x->0` zero-frequency accessibility and graph/resolvent problem,
    together with the Hill full bulk/interface/exterior construction, as
    explicit active frontiers. A zero limiting frequency is not automatically
    an admissible eigenmode, a protected gap or a nonlinear instability.
