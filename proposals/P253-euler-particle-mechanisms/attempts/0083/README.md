## Geometry, rows, and scale conventions

Let `s_epsilon` be the physical core scale fixed by an explicit level-set,
area, or normalization definition that is invariant under meridional
reparametrization.  Set

    delta(epsilon) = s_epsilon/R.                        (3)

In normalized coordinates

    y=((r-R)/s_epsilon,(z-z_epsilon)/s_epsilon),          (4)

the circulation normalization, free boundary, Green kernel, chemical
potential, translation speed, and center rows must all be carried.  “Fixed
limiting column” means convergence to one profile determined by the fixed
pair `(kappa,R)` and the declared normalization, not merely convergence to
some member of a parameterized column family.

The preferred differentiable scale conclusion is

    s_epsilon
      = C_s(kappa,R) epsilon [1+eta(epsilon)],
    delta(epsilon)
      = [C_s(kappa,R)/R] epsilon [1+eta(epsilon)],
    C_s(kappa,R)>0,
    |eta(epsilon)|+|epsilon eta'(epsilon)| -> 0.          (5)

Thus

on a punctured interval.  Equation (5) is an output, not a premise.

The carrier path also carries a concrete, separate nonvanishing test for the

activation and, for a compact divergence-free displacement `xi` in the

terms by parts to put the off-diagonal response in the form

    <e_1^#, delta L_[xi,Omega] e_2>
        = integral_(core) xi dot F_12.                  (17)

Here `e_1^#` is the physical adjoint/KKS dual once normalized, `Omega` is the
actual carrier vorticity, and `F_12` includes the induced whole-space Hodge
velocity, pressure/Leray contribution, coefficient variation, interface
cancellation, and every center/circulation/mean-radius row.  It is not the
local transport term alone.

Let `P_row` be a bounded primal oblique projection onto compact
divergence-free displacements tangent to the selected regular core cell and
satisfying the finite physical rows.  A self-adjoint `P_DA` may replace it
only after self-adjointness is proved in the physical pairing.  If (17)
vanished for every such `xi`, the dual witness would vanish modulo gradients.
The exposing analytic predicate is therefore

    curl(P_row^* F_12)(x_*) != 0                        (18)

at one regular-cell point, with the curl interpreted in the reduced
meridional slice and the toroidal character retained.  Prove the local DA
Helmholtz/Bogovskii construction and finite-row restoration, then use a compact
patch around `x_*` as a dual test displacement.  A quantitative version must
track the patch radius, the right-inverse/row-correction constants, and the
physical mode/adjoint norms to give

    sup_(||xi||_X<=1) |<e_1^#,delta L_[xi,Omega]e_2>|
       >= c_12 > 0.                                    (19)

Repeat the same calculation for the traceless diagonal functional

    <e_1^#,delta L_[xi,Omega]e_1>
      -<e_2^#,delta L_[xi,Omega]e_2>
        = integral_(core) xi dot (F_11-F_22),           (20)

and test `curl(P_row^*(F_11-F_22))` on an allowed axisymmetric or otherwise
selection-compatible slice.  Nonzero (18) and its diagonal analogue supply

Equations (17)--(20) are deliberately separate from the large-`ell_N`
