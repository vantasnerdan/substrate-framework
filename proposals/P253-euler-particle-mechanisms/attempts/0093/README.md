## Source and authority audit

After activation, every primary source actually used will be checked at its
exact version and proposition. The review will distinguish standard
cohomology and cotangent-bundle facts from claims about the actual whole-space
Euler--Maxwell/tag phase space. Independently reviewed framework suppliers
will be reused only at their pinned statements. A source proving integer
characters of `U(1)` will not silently supply quantization of a classical
Noether momentum, and a magnetic bundle over a puncture sphere will not
silently become an electric charge theorem on nonsingular compactified whole
space.

## Unit A — dimensions, gauge rescaling, and signed real tag

1. Freeze SI or declared physical dimensions for the Maxwell potential,
   coupling, tag density, charge, action, and any rescaled connection. Verify
   every factor introduced by converting a real gauge parameter to a
   dimensionless compact phase.
2. Reconstruct the local gauge transformation and the current/moment-map
   pairing. Check signs, boundary terms, normalization of the constant gauge
   generator, and the domain on which it is differentiable.
3. Determine whether the material tag remains a real signed continuum under
   the actual Euler transport and source constraints. A rescaling or compact
   target coordinate must not silently discretize its amplitude.

**Unit A verdict:** adjudicate exact dimensions and gauge algebra separately
from any compactness or charge-selection interpretation.

## Unit B — compact phase and continuous classical momentum

4. Verify the topology and period of the proposed compact material phase and
   the chosen character `exp(i m theta)` with `m` integral. Distinguish a
   chosen representation label from a dynamically selected value.
5. Write the full classical cotangent symplectic form and moment map. Check
   whether the momentum conjugate to the compact phase ranges over the real
   line on the classical phase space even when `m` is fixed.
6. Identify any additional prequantization, polarization, boundary, orbit, or
   reduction hypothesis needed to turn the integer character into a discrete
   physical momentum. Do not import quantum kinematics into the classical
   Euler field without an explicit map.

**Unit B verdict:** preserve the exact compact-character construction and
state whether it supplies selection, only sufficiency, or a counterboundary.

## Unit C — bulk homology and electric boundary moment map

7. Recompute the relevant homology/cohomology groups for nonsingular
   compactified whole space and for a puncture/linking sphere. Verify
   `H2(S3)=0` and the precise sense in which `H2(S2)=Z` supports a magnetic
   flux/Chern class.
8. Check gauge bundle regularity, decay, allowed large gauge transformations,
   and boundary components. Determine whether a nonzero electric charge is a
   Gauss-law boundary moment map at spatial infinity rather than the Chern
   class of a bulk spatial two-cycle.
9. Separate magnetic flux quantization, electric charge normalization, and
   quantum charge spectrum. Similar integral formulas or duality language do
   not identify these objects without the corresponding phase-space map.

**Unit C verdict:** adjudicate the exact topological obstruction and retain
the strongest valid boundary-charge statement.

## Unit D — Euler vorticity versus compact BF data

10. Type the Euler vorticity two-form, its exactness/closedness, support and
    periods on the actual domain, and its transformation under relabeling.
11. Test whether it can serve as an independent compact two-form gauge field
    with large-gauge periods and an integer BF level. Check all units,
    degeneracies, gauge symmetries, boundary terms, and whether the would-be
    field is merely determined by the Euler velocity.
12. Audit any cited BF source at its actual dimension, compact gauge group,
    action normalization, and quantum/classical scope. Record a missing
    supplier as a frontier, not as a no-go for every extension.

**Unit D verdict:** state exactly what direct Euler vorticity supplies and what
new compact field/level construction would be required.

## Unit E — fixed-charge action rank and conditional phase root

13. Reconstruct the fixed-`Q` constraint and the physical action functional.
    Exhibit or refute the proposed tangent counterdirection, checking that it
    preserves every fixed row and remains in the stated phase space.
14. Compute the rank of the charge map against independent action directions.
    A single scalar constraint cannot select a unique multi-action state
    unless additional relations are proved.
15. Derive the common-phase stationary/root condition with all signs,
    Hessian/nondegeneracy requirements, parameter domains, and endpoint
    alternatives. Distinguish existence of a conditional algebraic root from
    existence, uniqueness, accessibility, or dynamical attraction of a
    physical carrier.

**Unit E verdict:** preserve the fixed-charge counterdirection and any exact
conditional root while keeping physical action selection at its actual
supplier boundary.

## Unit F — parent and interpretation boundary

16. Enumerate the missing constructions before any P2/P4 or parent inference:

17. Keep measurement/reset, Born weights, exchange/statistics, electron and

18. Ensure every no-selection statement is route-scoped. Failure of topology

**Unit F verdict:** preserve the strongest exact sufficiency/no-selection
