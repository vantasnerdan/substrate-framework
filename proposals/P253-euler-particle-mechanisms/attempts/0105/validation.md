The exact verifier derives these algebraic predicates:

1. the power-law dipole source is an exact profile derivative;
2. the two Volterra moments reduce to the closed moment formula;
3. the full-core response agrees with the importable API;
4. its factored sign function and smooth-center coefficient are derived
   exactly, while the positive edge limit follows analytically from the
   positive limiting moment;
5. the general cancellation ODE has exact first integral `s P_s^3 G_P`; a
   nonzero constant produces the forbidden `v~s^-1` center behavior, while
   the regular kernel is exponential and nonzero at a finite `P=0` edge;

   negative near the center and positive near the edge; this profile is not a
   Lane--Emden/Poisson solution and supplies no physical evidence;
7. the material contour tangent is the weighted normal-flux pairing; and
8. the explicit radial displacement realizes the requested raw tag tangent;
   and
9. the finite-Cao action-coordinate construction has exactly the weighted
   contour-zero-mean compatibility condition.  The periodic primitive is an
   analytic construction with a typed one-normal-derivative loss
   `C^(k+1,alpha)->C^(k,alpha)` (and `H^(k+1)->H^k`), while the verifier checks
   its finite weighted row.

Focused tests cover the public formulas and decidable invalid domains. The
checks do not prove the finite-Cao steady/free-boundary mapping, closed range,
strict-band cutoff response, or a charged full-core-tag branch. Repository
validation is agreement evidence only. No production numerics are used.
