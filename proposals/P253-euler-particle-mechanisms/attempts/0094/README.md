## Route A: actual fixed-charge reduced Hamiltonian

On a supplied positive canonical carrier mode with physical action `J>=0`,
derive from the same joint action the fixed-character/fixed-charge reduction

    E_red(J;Q,lambda_carrier)
      =E_0(Q,lambda_carrier)+nu_eff J+a_2 J^2+a_3 J^3+... .   (1)

Here `lambda_carrier` denotes the actual circulation, radius, core, and tag
parameters.  The coefficient `nu_eff` may include the real part of the
eliminated Maxwell self-energy only after a renormalized principal-value
resolvent and reciprocal Feshbach map are constructed; the reviewed outgoing
shell law supplies its imaginary boundary part, not this real shift.  Compute the first
nonlinear Euler, tag, and field coefficients on the declared constrained
domain.  Separate a formal normal form from an actual invariant/center-
stable reduction; the strong saddle and Maxwell continuum from 0085/0086
prevent a two-mode truncation from serving as the P2 persistence theorem.

Before any hard coefficient is known, prove the local sign classification.
If `nu_eff>0`, then `J=0` is the constrained boundary minimum and no analytic
higher-order term creates an arbitrarily small nonzero minimum.  If a derived
control makes `nu_eff<0` and `a_2>0`, the leading nonzero minimum is
`J_*=-nu_eff/(2a_2)` at leading order.  It becomes an earned local minimum
only after writing `E_red'=nu_eff+2a_2 J+R'(J)`, proving a uniform bound such
as `|R'(J)|<=C J^2`, choosing `|nu_eff|` small enough that the critical point
lies inside the reduction radius, applying the scalar IFT, and verifying
`E_red''>0`.  Its continuous dependence on detuning and carrier parameters is
still not a universal action selection.  An integer resonance or compact
character labels a branch only after the energy fixes its amplitude.

**Positive criterion:** earn a nonzero nondegenerate minimum on an actual
same-carrier invariant or constrained steady manifold, with every coefficient
derived from reviewed action data and no inserted target action.  Also test
whether its value survives the exact Euler similarity and continuous charged-
carrier parameters.
