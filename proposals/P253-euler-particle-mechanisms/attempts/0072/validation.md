# Validation and claim boundary

The exact verifier derives the homogeneous Fourier constants, radial/toroidal
`l=1` multiplicity block, forbidden flux row, the oriented field's
divergence/curl/helicity and finite norm, the anisotropic positive cross
kernel, and the fixed-frame symbol's reality, transversality and constant
norm. A proper pi rotation flips that symbol. The oracle also checks the exact
Gaussian regularized Coulomb limits and the unequal integrated-stress entries
that refute stationarity of the minimal realization. Focused API tests
exercise the same reusable identities.

The analytic derivation carries the complete harmonic expansion, general
translation kernel, weighted pressure remainder, local Euler invariance, and
observable-domain conclusions. The oracle does not prove a stationary or
persistent carrier, a center force, an autonomous average, or a quantum
interpretation.

The final exact receipt reruns the completed oracle after the mixed
radial--toroidal translation sign, parity typing, stress integration, and
stationary-residual checks were added. The final focused receipt exercises the
corresponding public API. Earlier successful receipts are retained as
historical intermediate evidence and are superseded for the final artifact
hashes by `final-exact-check.*` and `final-focused-pytest.*`.

The append-only `supervisor-scope-correction.md` changes only the interpretation
of the positive `C/d` energy and orders the stationary-tail continuation. It
does not change an equation, helper, verifier predicate, or test, so the final
receipts were not rerun for that wording correction.
