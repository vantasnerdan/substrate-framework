# 03 — CONTRAST discriminator: acoustic wave gas (predict-KILL)

Purpose per 01-verdicts-per-family.md: prove the emergent rigidity is
PINNING, not AVERAGING. The contrast family is the negative control: if a

## Family (model-level definition)

Acoustic wave gas: statistically isotropic ensemble of small-amplitude
standing/traveling sound modes on the same incompressible-free background
(barotropic sector, defined only for the contrast — the F-A medium needs
no compressibility). Defining property: the field is PHASE-AVERAGED and
carries no Lagrangian length — modes re-equilibrate on their own period,
so the equilibrated state is a functional of the action spectrum alone.

## Discrimination statement (exact at model level)

Equilibrated energy W_wave = W(spectrum). An isochoric coarse strain
(tr ε = 0) leaves the spectrum and its isotropy invariant: modes
re-populate the strained volume with the same statistics. Hence

  W_wave(ε) = W_wave(0)   for all deviatoric ε  ⇒  μ_wave = 0 identically,

in the family's own equilibrated regime (receipt R6b asserts the zero
form). This is the S2 lesson inverted: S2 died as a CHARGE channel with
dissipative type; here the wave gas is not proposed as the medium — it is
the control that shows which structure the medium's rigidity comes from.

## Why the discriminator is structural, not cosmetic

The F-A quadratic form is EXACTLY the material-length (memory) term
(receipts R6a/c): the response exists iff fine line elements CARRY
Lagrangian length into the deformation (frozen-in pinning), and vanishes
identically when the coupling is removed (R6a) — which is the wave gas's
defining property (R6b). The two families differ by memory presence, not
by averaging details; any shared averaging artifact would appear in BOTH.

## Regime statement (honest scope)

- Wave gas: μ_wave = 0 in its equilibrated regime. At deformation rates
  comparable to or faster than the wave period the gas is not equilibrated
  and the model definition above no longer applies (no claim either way).
- F-A: μ_aff > 0 in the frozen window (ω ≫ 1/τ). Both families are
  viscoelastic in general; the discriminator compares them IN their
  respective defined regimes — pinned memory (long τ) vs instant
  re-equilibration (short τ). The regime separation IS the physics:
  topology-pinned vs phase-averaged.
