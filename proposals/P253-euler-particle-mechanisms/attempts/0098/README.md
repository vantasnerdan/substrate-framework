## Review method and exposing checks

### 1. Route inventory and source applicability

1. Reconstruct the exact statements and dependency edges of `A0/A1`,
   `C1/C2/C3`, `E0/E1`, and every additional frozen route before assigning
   verdicts.
2. Check each primary citation at the exact version, proposition/equation,
   hypotheses, phase space, boundary conditions, and normalization used.
3. Separate imported theorems from equations rederived by `0094`; a source
   analogy or topology slogan does not supply a missing Euler construction.
4. Verify that reviewed suppliers are consumed only at their accepted scope
   and that no active or excluded attempt supplies evidence.

### 2. Compact character, BF, and gauge-moment units

5. Audit dimensions, signs, density and `2*pi` factors, gauge transformations,
   boundary terms, decay, and finite-energy domains in every compact-character
   or BF expression.
6. Distinguish a chosen integer character from a dynamically selected
   classical momentum or action. Check whether any compact level, moment map,
   or cohomology class is actually supplied by the same Euler carrier.
7. Verify the stabilizer and quotient used by every diagonal gauge moment,
   including where tag or vorticity profiles vanish and whether a purported
   generator is tangent to the declared leaf.
8. Keep a conditional common-phase or BF construction conditional unless its
   Euler field, global form, level, and admissible gauge group are all built.

### 3. Reduced energy and nonlinear action-locking units

9. Reconstruct the reduced functional from the physical action, energy,
   impulse, charge/tag constraints, and all Lagrange multipliers. Check units,
   signs, background/relative subtraction, and which variations are actually
   accessible.
10. Verify every Hessian, Schur complement, coercivity or saddle statement on
    its declared domain, including stabilizer zero modes, finite rows,
    complement inversion, and nonlinear remainder estimates.
11. Distinguish a stationary critical point, formal reduced minimum, local
    constrained branch, and dynamically invariant locking mechanism. None
    inherits the next conclusion without its own construction.
12. Test whether any asserted discrete action value is derived, merely chosen
    as a character/level, or still conditional on a missing same-carrier
    coupling.

### 4. Fixed-aspect leading scale balance

13. Recompute `E0` independently at fixed aspect ratio, including all powers,
    dimensional constants, signs, density factors, self/cross/background
    terms, and the order of its remainder.
14. Identify the variable selected by the leading stationarity equation and
    verify that it is translational impulse. A scale relation involving that
    impulse must not be relabeled an internal action, KKS quantum, or P2
    mechanism.
15. Check `E1` separately at its exact author boundary; it receives no support
    merely because `E0` has a correct leading asymptotic.

### 5. Evidence and interpretation

16. Inspect the exact verifier and focused receipt predicates. Determine which
    identities they independently derive, which inputs are encoded, and the
    maximum scientific verdict each run supports.
17. Reconcile the activation/precision chronology, completion receipt, mixed-
    base manifest, claim-bearing artifacts, and stale-claim scan.
18. Preserve all positive route atoms while keeping open dependencies and
    parent conclusions explicit. No finite check count promotes the parent.

## Final decision contract

The final review will issue one verdict for each named and additional frozen
`0094` route, then report the strongest combined theorem and the next concrete
dependency. A route is `established as stated`, `refuted` with its exact
mechanism, or `blocked` with its missing construction. Explicit hypotheses and
honest parent exclusions are not defects. A bounded correction will be
requested only for concrete contrary evidence and will preserve every stronger
true statement that survives.
