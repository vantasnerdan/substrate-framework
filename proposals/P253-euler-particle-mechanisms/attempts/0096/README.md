1. Reconstruct the Hessian/response derivative as a continuous functional on
   the actual constrained tangent or quotient space. Verify its uniqueness,
   sign, density weights, complex conjugation, and finite-row terms.
2. Identify the annihilator of the constrained tangent. Check that two

3. Audit every use of a Riesz map: specify the Hilbert structure, domain and

   functional.
4. Reconstruct the dual-norm or exposing lower bound without choosing a
   preferred representative unless the choice is proved canonical.

**Unit A verdict:** adjudicate the unique dual response independently of any
ambient `L2` representative convention.

## Unit B — KKS cancellation, Sturm positivity, and geometric domains

**Unit B verdict:** adjudicate the claimed exact `gamma_12,col>0` and preserve
