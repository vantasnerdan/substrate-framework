The whole-space nonaxisymmetric Hodge kernel is derived directly from the
Euclidean Newton/Biot--Savart kernel in exact toroidal coordinates. Cao's
axisymmetric scalar Green kernel is used for the base-carrier branch and its
matching coefficients, not substituted for the three-dimensional perturbation
operator.

The Hanzawa, Piola, Hodge-decomposition, singular-integral, commutator, and
closed-form facts used below are proved in the needed coordinate form. Their
names are not evidence for the source-specific domain or the resonant rate.
