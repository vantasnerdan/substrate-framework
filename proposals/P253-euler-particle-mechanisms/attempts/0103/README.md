## Frozen review method

### Unit A — variable two-label forced equilibrium lock

1. Reconstruct the two material labels, their parity and dimensions, the
   proposed variable coefficient, and the forced Ertel/lock equation without
   dividing across a label zero set.
2. Verify equilibrium versus dynamical scope, compatibility versus actual
   force selection, and the exact support boundary. Check whether cutoffs
   create derivative or interface distributions and whether every inner,
   outer and infinity flux used in the result vanishes in the stated class.
3. Audit the relation between the two labels and the physical force. A
   prescribed source or equilibrium identity does not by itself establish a
   material-leaf family, normalized response, or chiral/flavor lock.

### Unit B0 — contour reduction and Lorenz/Ampere normalization

4. Starting from the frozen Maxwell convention, eliminate the potential and
   independently derive the contour response functional. Check Lorenz-gauge
   sign, Ampere normalization, charge/current factors, density, `4 pi`, and
   oriented line/surface measures.
5. Require the same global Green normalization for every field, contour and
   source term. Track additive constants and affine/logarithmic tails rather
   than silently using different local Green functions for different rows.
6. Verify convergence, gauge independence, integration by parts, support and
   boundary terms. State whether the contour calculation is exact, asymptotic,
   formal, or a source-space witness.

### Unit B1 — actual normalized Cao response

7. Identify the actual Cao mode, left covector, KKS/energy norm and common
   physical pairing required by the response. Determine whether both the
   source and detector are normalized in that topology.
8. Distinguish a nonzero raw or source-specific contour functional from a
   lower bound after physical normalization. If the latter is absent, preserve
   the former and name normalized response as the missing construction.

### Unit B2 — finite-Cao transfer

9. Check whether any limiting or model contour response is transferred to a
   finite exact Cao carrier with a uniform remainder, a common domain and
   continuous mode/covector labeling.
10. A leading nonzero coefficient earns finite-Cao nonvanishing only when the
    error is smaller on an explicitly nonempty parameter interval. Otherwise
    retain it as the next quantitative transfer achievement.

### Unit C0 — global Liouville exponential profile and cancellation

11. Starting from the proposed radial profile, independently apply the exact
    radial Laplacian and derive its Liouville exponential Poisson law, including
    sign, source amplitude, length scaling, origin regularity and total source
    normalization. Do not infer the equation from a copied closed form.
12. Use the same globally normalized Green solution to recompute the first
    speed derivative and its claimed exact cancellation. Check every factor,
    integration by parts, differentiation under the integral, additive affine
    freedom and logarithmic tail.
13. Determine the literal domain of the profile. If its far-field potential is
    logarithmic, verify that the column field behaves like `1/r` and that its
    two-dimensional energy per unit length has the corresponding logarithmic
    divergence. Separately verify the claimed finite cross-sectional
    vorticity. A smooth exact Liouville realization can therefore have finite
    vorticity integral while remaining nonlocalized and not being a
    finite-energy Cao column or compact carrier.
14. Decide whether the cancellation is profile-specific or structural. It
    cannot establish a universal response sign without a coverage argument.

### Unit C1 — annular inner/outer Maxwell-source range

15. Independently form the two source moments for inner and outer annular
    controls and recompute their determinant, including orientation,
    normalization and the claimed value `-1/4`.
16. Enforce the actual linearized relation
    `delta chi=delta(zeta/lambda)`, including variation of `lambda` when it is
    not frozen and the domain where division is valid. Independent formal
    source components are not automatically independent admissible label
    variations.
17. Classify the determinant only as source-space controllability unless the
    exact free-boundary and material-leaf map is supplied. The `A/B` controls
    are frozen here as an unconstrained Maxwell-source range only: the
    constant-lock relation `chi=zeta/lambda`, charge, tag-leaf, positivity and
    free-boundary rows may reduce or collapse its apparent two-dimensional
    range. Preserve the absence of a universal sign: two unconstrained source
    directions can expose sign control without deciding every physical
    profile.

### Unit C2 — physical Cao/free-boundary/material-leaf lift

18. Audit whether the annular controls satisfy the exact Cao profile equation,
    circulation/center/charge rows, free-boundary compatibility, regularity,
    Hodge constraints and coadjoint/material-leaf tangent conditions.
19. Construct or identify the actual linear map from admissible
    `(delta P, delta zeta, delta chi)` variations on the fixed material leaf to
    the two source moments `(A,B)`, enforcing
    `delta chi=delta(zeta/lambda)` and all finite rows. Determine its range;
    rechecking the unconstrained `-1/4` determinant does not prove this map is
    onto.
20. Require a bounded lift and response pairing in the same topology. If no
    such lift is constructed, keep the exact Liouville solution, cancellation
    and unconstrained source determinant intact while naming the physical lift
    as open.

### Unit D — noncompact axis phase versus compact defect

21. Separate the circle-valued azimuthal phase on `R^3` minus the full axis
    from a compact puncture, knot, vortex core or moving defect. Check
    cohomology class, period, regularity, energy, support and every inner/outer
    boundary term.
22. Determine whether the target constructs a compact finite-energy defect and
    its transported moving domain, or only identifies the existing noncompact
    axis phase. Do not convert one into the other by terminology.

### Unit E — exclusions and evidence agreement

23. Reconcile the v2 completion and expansion receipts, final manifest, source
    audit, validation, result, derivation, verifier and importable API/tests.
    Scan for stale universal-sign, normalized-response, finite-Cao,
    finite-energy-Liouville, compact-defect or parent language.
24. Attribute exact-v2 and focused-v2 checks only to their encoded
    algebraic/API predicates. Static-v3 and repository-v3 are structure and
    agreement evidence. Do not rerun unchanged evidence merely for counts;
    only a predicate defect may trigger the single bounded correction.
25. Preserve that no P4/P2 closure, Lorentz chiral representation, flavor
    mechanism, particle, electron, neutrino, P6 or parent conclusion follows
    from the retained exact and source-space atoms.
