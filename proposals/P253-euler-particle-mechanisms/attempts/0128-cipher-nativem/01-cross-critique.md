- Q3 (front continuity, UNSASKED and sharpest): 0124 measured "crossing count 1
  node at full step" — as du varies continuously, src=0 crossings happen
  DISCRETELY, node to node. So front position is stepwise in du: not just
  non-differentiable but DISCONTINUOUS at crossing events. Hadamard calculus
  needs (at least) continuity in the perturbation. What must be true: the
  front-displacement norm is continuous in du across node-crossing events.
  Nobody proved it; 0124's evidence points the other way. If front motion is
  stepwise, R-B's Taylor expansion fails before the 20% trigger is even
  reached. Answer wanted: continuity proof, or a discretization where the
  front moves continuously (and its cost).
