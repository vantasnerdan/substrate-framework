## Frozen coherent-claim checks

The strongest possible result is an exact source-applicable statement that the
published pair is a stationary point of the correctly signed Euler loop action,
has the stated symmetry Jacobi fields, and cannot be a local maximizer of that
autonomous action on the declared full admissible loop space because its second
variation has both signs at arbitrarily high temporal frequency, together with
the precisely scoped source period--twist/Jordan relation. The review checks:

1. **Actual source orbit and domain.** Identify whether the object is periodic
   in the laboratory frame or only after axial translation, the exact period
   and half-period exchange, patch regularity, axisymmetric no-swirl phase
   space, circulation/intensity normalization, density convention, and allowed
   parameter set. Verify that the loop closes in the stated quotient and that
   relabeling, ring exchange, axial translation, and time phase are not
   conflated. Track whether each assertion belongs to the finite-core Euler
   solution or only to its limiting filament system.
2. **Action and KKS sign.** Starting from the full constant-density Euler
   action on the appropriate coadjoint leaf, independently derive the chosen
   symplectic potential/KKS form and Hamiltonian sign through the Euler
   equation. Check every density factor, orientation, Lie-bracket convention,
   translating-frame momentum term, period normalization, endpoint condition,
   chart/cocycle contribution, pressure-exact quotient, and harmonic row. The
   separately recorded sign correction must propagate consistently through
   the first and second variations rather than merely changing prose.
3. **Stationarity of the relative loop.** Verify that the actual source
   solution, with the necessary translation generator or quotient included,
   is a critical point of the displayed autonomous functional for all declared
   dynamically accessible periodic variations. A solution of the Euler PDE is
   not automatically a closed critical loop if endpoints, quotient, gauge, or
   momentum augmentation are mismatched.
4. **Phase and translation Jacobi fields.** Differentiate the exact orbit with
   respect to time phase and physical axial translation and check the complete
   linearized Euler/action equation, periodicity or relative-periodicity,
   constraint tangency, regularity, normalization, and independence modulo the
   stabilizer. Distinguish symmetry eigenvectors at multiplier one from
   generalized vectors and from the positive/negative temporal-frequency
   branches. These neutral directions do not by themselves prove either
   stability or instability.
5. **Full high-time-frequency second variation.** Recompute the complete
   Hessian before imposing background simplifications. Establish that the
   proposed oscillatory variations are real, periodic, sufficiently regular,
   divergence-free/dynamically accessible, tangent to the same leaf, compatible
   with the relative endpoint condition, and not pure gauge or stabilizer
   directions. Retain every kinetic, KKS, Hamiltonian, constraint, frame, and
   boundary term. Prove uniform control of lower-order terms as temporal
   frequency grows and exhibit both positive and negative directions for all
   sufficiently high frequencies, not merely a sampled ansatz or principal
   symbol detached from admissibility.
6. **Autonomous-maximizer obstruction.** Check the precise topology and
   variation class in which indefiniteness rules out a local maximum, including
   whether the quotient removes phase/translation zero modes. State the
   obstruction only for the displayed autonomous full-loop action. It does not
   rule out action stationarity, energy--momentum or rearrangement extrema at a
   time slice, constrained maximizers on a smaller admissible class, alternative
   variational principles, periodic-orbit existence, or nonlinear stability.
7. **Period--twist/Jordan relation.** At the source filament boundary, derive
   the parameter-differentiated family and its return relation with the exact
   period, phase, translation, and sign conventions. Check which derivative is
   nonzero, what fixed quantities are held constant, the normalization of the
   symmetry vector, and whether the derivative gives an eigenvector or a
   generalized Jacobi vector. Determine exactly when a nontrivial Jordan chain
   follows. Do not transfer that chain to the finite-core Euler contour
   monodromy without an independently proved operator/domain continuation.
8. **Logical bridge.** Require the chain
   `published relative-periodic Euler pair -> correct leaf/action and admissible
   loop space -> complete second variation -> two high-frequency signs -> no
   local autonomous full-loop maximum`, while keeping the filament
   period--twist statement at its source level. Each arrow must be supported;
   failure of one arrow narrows only this coherent route and is not a global
   variational, persistence, or carrier no-go.
