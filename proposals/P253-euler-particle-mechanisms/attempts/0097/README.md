## Typed state and exact questions

Let `chi=(chi_1,...,chi_N)` be real material scalars transported by one Euler
velocity,

    D_t chi := partial_t chi + u dot grad chi = 0.          (1)

Let `X(t)` be a material trajectory. A flavor observable is a declared map
from the physical state to a finite-dimensional normalized vector or density
matrix. It counts only if its basis is fixed by an independently derived
interaction current; a freely chosen basis rotation is bookkeeping.

A local polar material current has the form

    j_V^mu=(rho_V,rho_V u),                                (2)

with continuity following from (1) and incompressibility. A local axial
current requires a pseudoscalar density or an additional oriented tensor.
A chiral current requires a derived relation between polar and axial parts,
not merely the notation `V-A`.

Here `j^mu` is only a Galilean continuity-current tuple. It is not a Lorentz
four-current, still less a Lorentz chiral current, until the shared P4 state
space and representation supply that transformation law.

The attempt will keep four distinctions explicit:

- material-label basis versus propagation eigenbasis;
