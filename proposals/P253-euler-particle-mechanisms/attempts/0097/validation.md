# Validation

The exact verifier checks:

1. the material Leibniz rule for a constant mixing matrix with arbitrary tag
   residual, followed by the passive specialization;
2. zero elapsed propagation phase gives zero transition probability;
3. the vector-algebra identities used in the Euler helicity flux derivation;
4. scalar and pseudoscalar current parities are distinct;
5. the exact two-state transition probability;
6. the momentum-packet coherence formula;
7. the high-momentum relativistic frequency coefficient derived directly
   from the square-root dispersion, and the resulting propagation phase;
8. an exact noncommuting propagation/current matrix witness. The necessity
   and simultaneous-diagonalization statement remains an analytic argument.

The derivation supplies the differential/divergence steps completing the
helicity conservation proof; the verifier independently checks its exposing
vector identities. These exact checks do not validate a physical weak current,
a neutrino carrier, a mixing coefficient, or nonlinear persistence.
No production numerics are used.

The focused API suite separately checks that a moving unitary frame contributes
the declared `H_U=i(D_t U)U^*` connection. It is not attributed to the exact
verifier tally.
