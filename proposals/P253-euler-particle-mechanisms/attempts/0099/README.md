## Frozen review method

### 1. Exact unit inventory and dependencies

1. Recover the literal author statement for every `N0`, `N1a`, `N1b`,
   `N1c`, `N2a`, `N2b`, `N3`, and `N4` unit before judging it.
2. Audit the dependency graph so that a passive kinematic identity, a
   conserved current, a conditional propagation equation, and a dynamical
   carrier construction remain distinct achievements.
3. Check every imported theorem at its exact source version, hypotheses,
   spacetime/domain, regularity, decay, gauge and normalization conventions.
4. Consume reviewed framework suppliers only at their accepted scopes; no
   author chronology, later attempt, or passing tally creates authority.

### 2. Passive tags, Euler currents, and conservation

5. Recompute every continuity or transport identity, including density,
   divergence, material derivative, pressure/Hodge terms, boundary fluxes,
   decay, and the precise local-versus-global conservation statement.
6. Verify whether each proposed vector or axial current is an actual
   same-field Euler observable, a passive-tag construction, an identically
   conserved differential expression, or a conditional added sector.
7. Check parity, orientation, sign, support, finite-energy/Sobolev class,
   integral charge and topological assumptions independently.
8. Distinguish a nonzero conserved integral from a local chiral current,
   anomaly equation, propagating degree of freedom, or selectable flavor
   state.

### 3. Conditional flavor and chiral propagation

9. Reconstruct the proposed propagation system, its Hilbert/state space,
   generator, hermiticity or symplectic structure, dispersion, frequency and
   unit conventions, and any projection or truncation.
10. Derive any oscillation or mixing formula from the actual generator and
    initial data. Check whether parameters are carrier-derived, freely
    inserted, or merely conditional.
11. Verify the bridge from an Euler/tag current to the proposed propagating
    amplitudes, including common domain, source coupling, back reaction,
    conservation, and local well-posedness. A formal finite matrix does not
    supply this bridge.
12. Keep vector/axial locking, anomaly, mass, handedness, Lorentz/chiral
    representation, and detection statements separate unless the target
    constructs each one.

### 4. Route-specific positive and failure boundaries

13. For every established author route, independently derive the load-bearing
    sign, factor, conservation or spectral identity and verify its natural
    Euler/gauge domain.
14. For every refuted route, confirm the exact mechanism and preserve any
    positive subclaim; do not turn a route failure into a P6 or parent no-go.
15. For every blocked route, name the missing construction and verify that it
    is not already supplied by a sibling unit or reviewed dependency.
16. Preserve conditional lemmas as usable positive results without promoting
    their unsupplied hypotheses to Euler conclusions.

### 5. Corrected evidence attribution

17. Inspect the exact verifier body, inputs and captured output. Record only
    identities it independently derives; hard-coded expected values or
    declared booleans are claim-boundary checks, not independent proofs.
18. Inspect the focused command, public module and tests named by the manifest.
    Record their import/API/regression coverage separately from verifier
    evidence and analytic proof.
19. Reconcile the author-scope correction receipt, completion receipt,
    manifest, result, validation, and source audit for stale or crossed
    evidence-role claims.
20. Do not rerun unchanged evidence merely for a count. A predicate defect
    may trigger the single bounded correction; otherwise the recorded receipts
    are reused.
