## N0 — passive transported tags

For `D_t chi=0`, every material trajectory satisfies
`d chi(t,X(t))/dt=0`. Therefore every time-independent local function of a
finite passive-tag multiplet and every constant mixture `U chi` is constant
on the trajectory. If `y=U(t,x)chi`, then

    D_t y=(D_t U)U^* y=-i H_U y,
    H_U=i(D_t U)U^*.

For unitary `U`, `(D_t U)U^*` is anti-Hermitian and `H_U` is Hermitian, but
along one material curve this is a chosen frame connection. It becomes a
physical mixing connection only when an independent interaction fixes the
endpoint frame and the same state supplies nontrivial curvature or holonomy.

**N0 verdict: refuted for autonomous flavor oscillation by finite passive
tags.** This does not refute Euler modes, nonlocal retained variables, or a
genuine internal connection.

## N1a — Euler helicity axial current

For smooth incompressible Euler,

    partial_t u=u cross omega-grad(p+|u|^2/2),
    partial_t omega=curl(u cross omega).

Using `div omega=0`,
`div(A cross u)=u dot curl A-A dot omega`, and
`(u cross omega) cross u=|u|^2 omega-(u dot omega)u` gives

    partial_t(u dot omega)
      +div[(u dot omega)u+(p-|u|^2/2)omega]=0.

The pressure sign and kinetic coefficient are correct. Under reflection the
helicity density is a pseudoscalar and the displayed spatial flux is axial.
The result is a local Galilean continuity current on every smooth domain where
the flux is defined; a global conserved charge additionally requires the
corresponding boundary-flux or decay condition.

**N1a verdict: established as stated.** It is not a Lorentz chiral current or
a fermionic projector.

**N2a verdict: established as conditional kinematics.** It derives none of
