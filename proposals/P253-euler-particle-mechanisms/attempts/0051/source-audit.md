This attempt uses only pinned framework claims and already reviewed P253
boundaries.  It derives the finite-dimensional Schwinger--Hopf identities
directly and does not import a quantum model as Euler physics.

- `C-CST-008` supplies one positive two-real-dimensional KKS/Hessian block.
  It is one canonical oscillator, not two complex modes, and it expressly
  does not supply a global compact angle.
- `C-CST-011`, `C-CST-017`, and `C-CST-018` supply prepared finite-window
  physical response, KKS normalization, and full material-current rows at
  their accepted scopes.  The two polarization columns in the reviewed P251
  construction are canonically paired real directions.  Their nonzero cross
  KKS entry makes one oscillator.  Cartesian copies add response channels,
  but no accepted statement makes a four-real-dimensional autonomous
  invariant doublet or implements arbitrary `U(2)` mixing.
- `P253/0030/0035` supplies a positive full-pressure axisymmetric column
  energy and exact fixed-wave-number unitary group.  Each radial generalized
  eigenvalue is simple.  Real cosine/sine phases of one Fourier mode are the
  two quadratures of one complex oscillator, not a second complex mode.
- `P253/0043/0047` independently establishes the conditional KKS-sphere
  quantization and nonselection boundary.  In particular, existence of a
  compact symplectic sphere does not select its action class, an invariant
  measure, a Born rule, or exchange character.
- `P253/0046` derives the exact one-plane oscillator/Heisenberg boundary and
  finite-CCR obstruction.  It is under the independent `0050` review while
  this attempt is authored and is therefore corroboration rather than
  authority until that review closes.
- `P253/0049` gives an exact first-event identity under explicit analyzer,
  clock, capture, and reset hypotheses.  It does not supply any of those from
  Euler and remains separate pending `0050`.

The active `0048/0052` ring construction is not consumed as established
evidence.  Its bending sine/cosine pair is again one oscillator.  Its enlarged
internal block may eventually supply additional positive modes, but no
reviewed fixed-domain invariant doublet or physical mixing map currently
exists.

No external value of `hbar`, particle mass, charge, detector rate, or signal
speed enters this attempt.  The only new reusable object is the exact
finite-dimensional algebra conditional on a supplied physical canonical
doublet.  The positive Hessian/compatible complex structure, rather than the
KKS scale alone, fixes its energy-action complex coordinates; reciprocal
symplectic rescalings otherwise change the apparent `z` normalization.  In
those physical coordinates `J_a=|z_a|^2=H_a/nu_a`; the common-phase moment
map is `J=J_1+J_2`, the Stokes radius is `J/2`, and the reduced area is
`2*pi*J`.  This normalization is derived here and is not an additional
quantization premise.
