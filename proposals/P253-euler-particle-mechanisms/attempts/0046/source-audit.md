This attempt uses accepted claims as authority only at their registered
scopes.

- `C-CST-008` supplies an actual two-dimensional isovortical tangent block
  with `Omega(Q,S)=B!=0`, a positive Hessian

  `theta(Q)=1,theta(S)=0`, `G(Q)=B^2/P,G(S)=0`, and

- `C-CST-017` supplies a prepared periodic same-field response and canonical

  `Omega_can=[[0,M0],[-M0,0]]`, `M0=diag(rho I_T,j I)`, and an optical

The load-bearing source bodies checked for the last two rows are
`attempts/0265/joint-continuum.md`, `attempts/0262/local-normalizer.md`,
`attempts/0262/exact-gram.md`, and `attempts/0262/current-transfer.md` in
P251.  Their full-rank `theta,G,S` map is a map from prepared physical initial
data to simultaneous classical observations.  None declares those rows to be
noncommuting measurement operators, a compact spin orbit, or a quantum state.

No claim from the accepted registry selects an action unit, an occupation
number, a deck character, a probability postulate, or a Lorentz speed.  Those
are precisely the transfer questions below, not tacit inputs.

Here positive Hessian means the full matrix is positive definite: in the
displayed coordinates `Hq>0` and `Hq P-N^2>0`.  The finite-dimensional trace
test below applies to an exact CCR truncation of this noncompact canonical
plane.  It leaves open a separately constructed compact `su(2)` orbit or
reduction with its own physical Euler realization.
