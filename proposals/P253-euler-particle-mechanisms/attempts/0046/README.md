# P253/0046: can the accepted compact-pair action block realize a quantum two-state system?

Root preregisters a failure-derived P4 construction after `0043` proved that
Euler orbit topology permits, but does not select, quantum action and exchange
data. This attempt tests a materially different existing substrate object:
the accepted C-CST same-field two-polarization, KKS/Jacobi, current, and
compact-pair blocks. It asks whether those exact physical controls contain a
state/observable/dynamics mechanism that selects a two-state quantum sector,
rather than another coordinate or prequantum analogy.

The accepted claims are imported only at their pinned scopes. In particular,
C-CST-017/018 provide prepared nonzero-`K`, finite-window linear response,
physical angle/spin/current rows, and exact action normalization on their
declared compact field. They do not provide an invariant carrier manifold,
all-time autonomous evolution, quantum measurement, or particle identity.
No target history, preparation-dependent source, or selected accuracy limit
may be relabeled as a freely evolving quantum state.

## Frozen target and selection test

On the one accepted compact same-field background, construct or refute:

1. a normalized physical two-state space whose rays correspond to distinct
   substrate states;
2. at least two physically implemented noncommuting observables, with their
   complete Euler/material current readout;
3. an autonomous linear norm-preserving state evolution induced by the same
   Euler action, independent of a requested target history;
4. a physical probability/measurement rule distinguishing coherent
   superposition from a classical mixture;
5. a tensor/composition and exchange rule for two separated copies; and
6. a substrate mechanism selecting the action unit and, where applicable,
   the fermionic character and finite propagation speed.

All six are required for this block to advance LP4 beyond `0043`. A real
two-polarization response matrix, a complex amplitude, or an invertible
source-to-observable map is not by itself a two-level quantum system.

## Route A: quantize the optical KKS polarization plane

Recover the exact two real optical polarizations and their KKS/Jacobi form
from C-CST-017/018. Determine whether the physical Hamiltonian makes them a
positive autonomous oscillator. If it does, construct its complex structure
and quantization and calculate the spectrum. Test the dimensional fact that a
single canonical oscillator gives an infinite bosonic ladder, whereas a
two-dimensional Hilbert space requires a compact spin orbit, a constraint, or
a finite-level nonlinear mechanism. Retain every prepared-source and
finite-window hypothesis in the conclusion.

## Route B: use the full angle/spin/current observation algebra

Form the physical Poisson/KKS brackets of the accepted angle, `G`, `S`,
hybrid, and current observables on the same background. Test whether any
closed three-generator subalgebra has a fixed Casimir and compact `S2` orbit,
whether the two poles are distinct physical states, and whether the current
readout implements noncommuting measurements rather than simultaneous
classical functions. The existing full-rank response map may establish
tomographic control; it does not establish state collapse or Born
probabilities. Calculate the distinction explicitly.

## Route C: invariant two-copy/exchange realization

Use the accepted compact geometry and positive-density ensemble to ask whether
two separated copies form an invariant noncollision sector with an induced
line bundle and autonomous exchange path. Compare its action and current with
the exact `0043` `Z2` characters. Prepared independent cells or Haar averaging
do not select the deck sign. This route succeeds only with an actual
same-field two-copy phase space and physical holonomy.

## Route D: declared stochastic or microscopic augmentation

If Routes A--C cannot select probabilities or the action unit, compare at
least two explicit new hypotheses rather than hiding one in notation:

- a stochastic variational diffusion with a declared diffusion/action scale,
  deriving its state equation and testing compatibility with incompressible
  Euler and the compact carrier; and
- a topological circulation/action-selection rule with its permitted Euler
  phase space, showing whether Kelvin's continuously valued circulation
  actually becomes discrete without first assuming a single-valued quantum
  amplitude.

Each augmentation must list its new law, parameter, state space, conservation
rules, and empirical inputs. It earns a conditional extended-substrate route,
not an Euler derivation, unless the added law is independently derived from
the same substrate.

## Oracle and verdict boundary

The strongest oracle is the exact physical KKS/Poisson matrix and Hamiltonian
on the retained polarization block, followed by a dimension/spectrum and
observable-algebra calculation. A source-control rank test is evidence for
controllability only. Numerical spectra are not licensed unless exact
reduction leaves one declared small remainder.

Each route receives a separate verdict and must generate its next positive
candidate after failure. This attempt does not assume LP2, promote C-CST-018
beyond its accepted finite-window scope, identify an electron or neutrino, or
complete P253.
