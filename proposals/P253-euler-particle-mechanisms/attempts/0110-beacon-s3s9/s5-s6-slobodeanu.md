## S5 exact consumed scope

- Proposition 2 (duality): σ₂-critical map with potential `P` (strongly
  coupled FS + mass term) ⇒ `V = (∗φ∗ω)♯` is STEADY incompressible Euler with
  Bernoulli `P = P∘φ`. CONVERSE IS LOCAL: a steady Euler `V` yields a
  σ₂-critical submersion only in a neighborhood of `V_x ≠ 0`. Global case =
  "S-integrable" (extremely non-generic by S6).
- Proposition 3: with the Dirichlet term (full FS model), the map gives
  steady FORCED Euler `∇_V V = −grad p + F` with `F ⊥ V` — not our substrate.
- Proposition 4 (no-go): NO finite-energy axisymmetric-no-swirl C² solutions
  of the strongly coupled FS model on R³ for `P = (1−φ_3^a)^b` — via the
  Euler-side analogue [21]. Directly blocks the naive "S2 ring + R2 label"
  combination in the no-swirl class.
- Liouville: Derrick scaling kills pure-σ₂ finite-energy R³ critical points
  (⇔ S-integrable Beltrami Liouville [27]).
- Bounds: Hopf `Q(φ)` ↔ helicity `H(V) = π²Q(φ)`; `E ≥ C|Q|^3/4` ⇒
  fluid-energy-vs-helicity bound at fixed Bernoulli function.

  critical point of quartic+p potential energy (8).
- Critical set = equatorial S² = zeros of V; energy 46.058 vs bound 13.852
  (not a minimizer); helicity-energy gap noted.

  stereographic projection or hedgehog map DESTROYS both the FS solution and
  the Euler property. "A major achievement" would be finding a map that
  preserves them. The source itself closes the sphere→Euclidean transfer.
