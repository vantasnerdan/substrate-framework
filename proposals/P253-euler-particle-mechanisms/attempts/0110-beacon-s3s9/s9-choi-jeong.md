## Exact consumed scope

- Theorem A: arbitrarily L¹-SMALL localized outward perturbation of Hill's
  vortex `ξ_H = 1_B` (unit ball, travel speed `W_H = 2/15`) yields
  `diam(supp ξ(t)) ≳ t` for ALL `t ≥ 0` — linear-in-time filamentation
  (long tail), confirming Pozrikidis 1986 numerics.
- Corollaries: infinite-time patch-perimeter + cross-section growth (1.1);
  infinite-time gradient growth `‖∂_r ξ‖_1, ‖r²ω‖_1 ≳ t` for smooth data (1.2);
  finite-but-arbitrary-time exponential vorticity growth (1.3).
- Mechanism: Choi orbital stability [13] (variational, Eulerian) + dynamical
  bootstrap on particle trajectories (Lagrangian) giving sharp front speed
  (1.5); explicit Hill velocity (2.5) drags behind-particles into a tail.
- Hypotheses: AXISYMMETRIC NO-SWIRL; nonnegative `ξ_0` (one-signed, needed
  for the variational stability input); `ξ_0 ∈ L¹∩L²`, `r²ξ_0 ∈ L¹`;
  stability+filamentation in `(L¹∩L²)+impulse` norm (Theorem B/2.1).
