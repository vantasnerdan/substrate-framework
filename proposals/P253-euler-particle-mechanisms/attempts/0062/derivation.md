# Full-Hodge block and same-leaf topology calculation

## 1. Reviewed boundary and fixed object

Fix one already existing centered `p>=6` Cao ring; no cross-parameter carrier
map is used. In cylindrical coordinate basis `(partial_r,partial_theta,
partial_z)` write

    omega_0=zeta(r,z) partial_theta,
    W_0=W^r(r,z) partial_r-Omega_0 partial_theta
          +W^z(r,z) partial_z,                            (1)

where `W_0=v_0-c_0 e_z-Omega_0 R` and `R=partial_theta`.
The physical bracket convention is

    [X,Y]=(X dot grad)Y-(Y dot grad)X.                    (2)

The 0057 review leaves `HJ2`, the common closed DA graph, `GR`, and the exact
Riesz mode unproved. All four are outputs rather than inputs below. The exact
pointwise Green/Piola identities and limited `p>=6` no-sheet behavior are
retained without promoting them to operator estimates.

## 2. Exact toroidal harmonic block

Let a perturbation and its whole-space velocity have a nonzero toroidal
harmonic `n`:

    eta=e^(i n theta)(eta^r partial_r+eta^theta partial_theta
                         +eta^z partial_z),
    h=B_R3 eta=e^(i n theta)(h^r partial_r+h^theta partial_theta
                         +h^z partial_z).                 (3)

Define

    T_n=W^r partial_r+W^z partial_z-i n Omega_0.          (4)

Direct evaluation of

    A_0 eta=-[W_0,eta]-[h,omega_0]                       (5)

gives the full coordinate block

    (A_0 eta)^r
      =-T_n eta^r+eta^r partial_r W^r+eta^z partial_z W^r
          +i n zeta h^r,

    (A_0 eta)^theta
      =-T_n eta^theta-h^r partial_r zeta-h^z partial_z zeta
          +i n zeta h^theta,

    (A_0 eta)^z
      =-T_n eta^z+eta^r partial_r W^z+eta^z partial_z W^z
          +i n zeta h^z.                                (6)

Thus the pressure/Hodge field `h` occurs in every component. Equation (6) is
the exact harmonic differential expression, not yet a closed operator theorem.

The velocity is fixed by the global system

    curl h=eta,            div h=0,
    h(x)->0 as |x|->infinity.                             (7)

Equivalently, at the representative point `x=(r,0,z)`, put
`y=(r' cos vartheta,r' sin vartheta,z')` and let
`Q_vartheta` rotate cylindrical components into Cartesian components. Then
the exact harmonic kernel is

    (B_n eta_n)(r,z)
      =(1/(4 pi)) integral r' dr' dz' d vartheta
        e^(i n vartheta)
        [Q_vartheta eta_n(r',z') cross (x-y)]/|x-y|^3.   (7a)

The integration is over the whole physical support and the full angle. Formula
(7a) retains the vector-component rotation, physical Jacobian `r'`, toroidal
curvature, and exterior tail; none is present in a scalar core Green
substitution.

Outside `K_0`, `curl h=0`. Hence

    h_n^ext=grad Phi_n,       Delta Phi_n=0               (8)

for `n!=0`. The one-dimensional exterior circulation/harmonic field is
rotation invariant and therefore lies in the `n=0` block. It must be restored
in the zero harmonic generated by a mode and its conjugate. Orthogonality of
SO(2) characters proves it cannot be silently inserted into, or discarded
from, a nonzero block. The global field (7) enforces normal and tangential
transmission; a jump would add a distributional curl sheet.

In velocity variables the pressure perturbation is

    pi=-Delta_R3^-1 partial_i partial_j
          (v_0^i h^j+h^i v_0^j),                         (9)

with the translation/rotation generator terms retained before applying the
Leray projection. Equations (7)--(9), not a core Dirichlet Green function,
define the physical Hodge block.

## 3. Exact DA map, inverse, and carrier-map intertwining

For a divergence-free displacement
`xi=e^(i n theta)(xi^r,xi^theta,xi^z)`, the DA tangent is

    C_0 xi=-[xi,omega_0],                                (10)

so in `zeta>0`

    (C_0 xi)^r=i n zeta xi^r,
    (C_0 xi)^theta
       =-xi^r zeta_r-xi^z zeta_z+i n zeta xi^theta,
    (C_0 xi)^z=i n zeta xi^z.                            (11)

For `n!=0`, (11) has the pointwise inverse

    xi^r=eta^r/(i n zeta),
    xi^z=eta^z/(i n zeta),

    xi^theta
      =[eta^theta+xi^r zeta_r+xi^z zeta_z]/(i n zeta).  (12)

If `div eta=0`, the identity

    div[ xi,omega_0]
      =xi(div omega_0)-omega_0(div xi)
      =-i n zeta div xi                                  (13)

shows that the inverse displacement is divergence free. Therefore the
nonzero-harmonic DA map is locally bijective on the positive core after the
finite constraints are imposed.

The exact carrier-map derivative on displacements is

    M_0 xi=B_R3(C_0 xi)+[xi,W_0].                        (14)

Because `[W_0,omega_0]=0`, Jacobi gives

    A_0 C_0 xi
      =-[W_0,-[xi,omega_0]]-[B_R3 C_0 xi,omega_0]
      =C_0(B_R3 C_0 xi+[xi,W_0])
      =C_0 M_0 xi.                                      (15)

Equation (15) is the exact full-Hodge interior block conjugacy that was
missing from 0058. It proves that the direct vorticity block and invariant
carrier-map route have the same nonnormal range problem; neither can bypass
the other by notation.

The vector `M_0 xi` contains the decaying, noncompact field `B_R3 C_0 xi` and
need not itself be a collar-supported representative. This does not invalidate
(15): `C_0 M_0 xi` depends only on its germ at `supp omega_0`. Choose a cutoff
equal to one on a neighborhood of `K_0`, place its transition strictly where
`omega_0=0`, and correct the cutoff divergence by a Bogovskii field supported
in that exterior collar. The corrected compact lift differs from `M_0 xi`
only where `C_0=0`, so (15) is unchanged. This supplies the exact tangent lift;
uniform graph bounds for that lift remain part of the collar theorem below.

### The boundary obstruction is explicit

For the Cao power law, a boundary defining function `d` gives at the reviewed
limited scope

    zeta=O(d^p),             grad zeta=O(d^(p-1)).        (16)

For smooth `xi`, (11) therefore requires

    eta^(r,z)=O(d^p),
    eta^theta=-xi^normal partial_normal zeta+O(d^p)
              =O(d^(p-1)).                               (17)

Conversely, (12) requires both `eta^(r,z)/zeta` and the cancellation

    eta^theta+(eta^r zeta_r+eta^z zeta_z)/(i n zeta)     (18)

to vanish strongly enough that division by `zeta` is in the displacement
space. The reviewed no-sheet statement gives only the forward behavior in
(17); it does not prove the reverse Hardy/cancellation estimate (18), traces
of `A_0 eta`, or closedness of the range. Thus (15) is an exact interior
intertwiner but not the global graph isomorphism `HJ2` or the all-sector
resolvent `GR`.

For clarity, the positive-core inverse fixes the candidate weighted norm

    ||eta||_(Y_n^s)
      =||eta^r/(n zeta)||_(H^(s+1))
       +||eta^z/(n zeta)||_(H^(s+1))
       +||(eta^theta+(eta^r zeta_r+eta^z zeta_z)/(i n zeta))
             /(n zeta)||_(H^(s+1)).                     (19)

On every subdomain `zeta>=zeta_min>0`, (11)--(13) prove a two-sided
isomorphism between divergence-free displacements and divergence-free
vorticities in (19), with constants depending explicitly on
`1/(|n| zeta_min)`. Extending that statement to `zeta=0`, with uniform
interface and graph traces, is precisely the nontrivial weighted Hardy
achievement; writing (19) makes the singular weights visible rather than
hiding them in a closure symbol.

## 4. Physical KKS density on the harmonic block

The right-reduced orbit convention from 0005 is

    Omega_KKS(C_0 xi,C_0 chi)
      =-<m,[xi,chi]>
      =rho_0 integral_R3 omega_0 dot (xi cross chi) dx.  (20)

Since `omega_0=zeta partial_theta=r zeta e_theta` and
`dx=r dr dtheta dz`, pairing harmonics `n` and `-n` yields

    Omega_KKS(C_0 xi_n,C_0 chi_-n)
      =2 pi rho_0 integral_(K_0)
          r^2 zeta
          (xi_n^z chi_-n^r-xi_n^r chi_-n^z) dr dz.       (21)

This derives the physical symplectic density and its sign:

    dmu_KKS=2 pi rho_0 r^2 zeta dr dz.                   (22)

It includes density and toroidal measure and is not a filament
normalization. A mode `q` must still be constructed by the global block before
the energy-unit condition
`Omega_KKS(q,conjugate(q))=-i/nu` can be applied. Equation (22) fixes the
measure needed for that calculation but does not manufacture `q`.

## 5. The limiting-absorption ladder and its exact stopping point

If the full operator could be graph-conjugated near a simple resonance to

    S(lambda)=M_(D(I)-lambda)+K(lambda),                  (23)

the unperturbed scalar boundary values obey the exact distribution identity

    1/(D-lambda minus/plus i0)
      =PV[1/(D-lambda)] plus/minus i pi delta(D-lambda),  (24)

and at a simple root

    delta(D-lambda)
      =delta(I-I_*)/|partial_I D(I_*)|.                  (25)

Thus even the raw trace carries the action measure and slope; it is not an
unweighted declaration `N(I_*)=0`.

With the Hodge block retained, a distorted adjoint state would have to solve
the Lippmann--Schwinger equation

    psi_*^*=psi_0^*-(M_D^* minus/plus i0)^(-1)K^*psi_*^*,

    psi_0^*=e_*^* delta(D-lambda_*),                      (26)

and the physical range trace would be

    T N=<psi_*^*,U^(-1)N>.                               (27)

Equations (23)--(27) give a concrete target and the correct dependence on the
KKS measure (22). They do not prove that the boundary value or inverse in
(26) exists. Gallay--Smets prove compactness of their straight-column Hodge
perturbation in a different enstrophy space for nonzero axial wave number;
0057 expressly found no curved-ring analogue in the Cao DA graph.

The exact missing estimates are now localized:

1. the two-sided boundary map (12), (17)--(18), including `A_0 eta` traces;
2. bounded core/exterior Hodge transmission in that graph norm;
3. separate/mixed cross-sectional and toroidal commutators, because one
   combined eigenvalue difference can cancel;
4. the low-mode kernel/cokernel and zero-harmonic circulation block; and
5. a nonnormal limiting-absorption/Grushin bound for (23), including its
   adjoint domain.

Without these, `psi_*^*`, the exact first critical pair, and a residue-normalized
mode do not exist as constructed objects in 0062. Consequently

    V_*=<psi_*^*,U^(-1)P_* R_(j_*)>                      (28)

is **undefined, not zero**. There is no honest source coefficient to evaluate:
the missing input is the physical adjoint functional itself, not an absent
printed Taylor coefficient. The raw estimate
`j_*=Theta((delta^2 log(1/delta))^-1)` remains only a localization scale.

**Route-A operator verdict.** The exact harmonic differential block (6),
whole-space Hodge/exterior closure (7)--(9), positive-core DA inverse
(11)--(13), carrier-map intertwiner (15), and KKS density (21)--(22) are
established. The global graph conjugacy, limiting absorption, adjoint trace,
and `V_*` are blocked by the five named constructions above. This is a route
gap, not a spectral or branch no-go.

## 6. Exact centralizer topology

The carrier-map equation for any smooth same-leaf rotating solution is

    W=g_*Y,          [Y,omega_0]=0.                       (29)

In `zeta>0`, a Fourier calculation shows that `Y` is axisymmetric. Write its
poloidal part using a streamfunction `psi_Y`:

    Y^r=-(1/r) partial_z psi_Y,
    Y^z= (1/r) partial_r psi_Y.                           (30)

The centralizer condition is

    Y^r zeta_r+Y^z zeta_z=0.                             (31)

On every connected regular annulus of `zeta`, (31) gives

    psi_Y=F(zeta),

    Y_pol=(F'(zeta)/r)
       (-zeta_z partial_r+zeta_r partial_z),              (32)

while the toroidal component is an arbitrary axisymmetric function subject
to divergence, smoothness, and global matching.

Equation (32) proves that every regular `zeta` torus is invariant. A Poincare
map on a transverse meridional section fixes the action label `I=I(zeta)`;
at a rational rotation number the entire resonant invariant circle remains,
rather than breaking into isolated elliptic/hyperbolic points.

For a smooth diffeomorphism, (29) also gives exact flow conjugacy

    Phi_W^t=g o Phi_Y^t o g^(-1).                        (33)

Zeros, periodic orbits, stable/unstable manifolds, Poincare maps, and
separatrix incidence are carried by `g`. A smooth `g` cannot create an island
chain absent conjugate topology in an allowed `Y`.

By contrast, the generic candidate Hamiltonian

    H_pend=(alpha/2)(I-I_*)^2+epsilon V_* cos(phi)        (34)

has Hessian determinants `-alpha epsilon V_*` and
`+alpha epsilon V_*` at its two critical phases. If
`alpha epsilon V_*!=0`, one is a saddle, the other a center, and a separatrix
is unavoidable. Therefore (34) cannot be the normal form of a smooth
same-leaf relative flow on a regular positive-core annulus unless one of the
following holds:

1. the physical splitting coefficient vanishes (transparency);
2. the allowed centralizer already has conjugate critical topology; or
3. the map or field leaves the smooth DA class.

On a regular annulus, `F'(zeta)=0` makes the poloidal velocity vanish on a
whole level, not at an isolated saddle. A critical point of `zeta`, or the
support boundary where `zeta=0`, may have different topology, but neither is
the regular simple-action root assumed by the generic pendulum reduction.
The exterior centralizer is unrestricted by `[Y,0]=0` and remains tied to the
interior by (7)--(9); no positive-core conclusion is extended across the
boundary.

**Topology verdict.** A generic topology-creating pendulum layer is refuted
for smooth carrier-map conjugacy at a regular positive-core resonance. This
does not refute a smooth nonaxisymmetric Cao branch: such a branch would be a
deformation of the integrable invariant-torus foliation and must satisfy the
corresponding transparency conditions. Nor does it refute a layer based at a
proved compatible centralizer critical set.

## 7. The square-root cutoff and exact volume correction

Set `h=sqrt(epsilon)`. On a regular volume chart with density
`J(I,phi,sigma) dI dphi dsigma`, the inner generator

    X_S=(1/J)(partial_phi S partial_I-partial_I S partial_phi) (35)

is exactly divergence free because

    div_J X_S
      =(1/J)[partial_I partial_phi S
             -partial_phi partial_I S]=0.                (36)

If `S` vanishes to all orders at the chart boundary, (35) extends by zero and
no Bogovskii correction is required. This is the strongest positive
cutoff construction. If a non-volume chart or non-flat cutoff is used, the
Bogovskii correction must carry the same scales derived next; it is not an
`O(epsilon)` afterthought.

Let a nontrivial self-similar layer have amplitude `A_h` and radial profile
`chi((I-I_*)/h)` on a region whose other dimensions are fixed. Smooth bounded
Jacobian above and below gives, for every integer `r` reached by the profile,

    ||partial_I^r xi_h||_L2
      =Theta(A_h h^(1/2-r)).                              (37)

Mixed phase derivatives add
`K_*^t`, `K_*=1+|m_*|+|j_* ell|`. For the generic topology-changing
displacement `A_h=Theta(h)`,

    ||xi_h||_(H^s)=Theta(h^(3/2-s)),
    ||xi_h||_(H^(s+1))=Theta(h^(1/2-s)).                 (38)

The DA tangent (11) contains no radial derivative of `xi`, but its
`H^(s-1)` norm still has the lower scale

    ||C_0 xi_h||_(H^(s-1))
      =Theta(h^(5/2-s))                                  (39)

whenever the nonzero phase factor or normal profile coefficient is present.
For the frozen `s>7/2`, all exponents in (38)--(39) are negative. Thus this
self-similar square-root layer is not a small `H^(s+1)` volume-preserving
diffeomorphism and does not converge to the carrier in the declared DA graph
norm.

This is not cured by a Bogovskii solve: scaling a right inverse on a width-`h`
collar gives the same derivative ledger as the divergence defect. The exact
volume generator (35) eliminates the correction but not the Sobolev blow-up.
A global smooth deformation of amplitude `h` over an `O(1)` region would have
small norm, but by (33) it still cannot create new separatrix topology.

**Singular-layer verdict.** The frozen self-similar pendulum cutoff is refuted
as a route to a branch in the declared smooth DA neighborhood. It remains a
well-defined weaker/singular critical-layer candidate only after a separate
function space and distributional interface equation are licensed. No such
weaker object is called the Cao smooth branch.

## 8. Failure-derived smooth route

The two failures select transparency/integrable carrier matching as the
strongest same-carrier continuation. Parameterize the full allowed
centralizer by its regular-core functions `F(zeta)`, toroidal drift, boundary
jets, exterior harmonic potential, and zero-mode circulation. Define

    G(g,Y,c,Omega)
      =g_*^(-1)[B_R3(g_*omega_0)-c e_z-Omega R]-Y,       (40)

    Y in Cent(omega_0).                                  (41)

Then `G=0` is exactly the whole-space rotating Euler equation on the fixed
coadjoint leaf. Its nonzero-harmonic derivative is (14), and (15) proves that
its adjoint trace is the same missing trace as Route A. Unlike the pendulum
ansatz, (40)--(41) preserves the integrable orbit topology from the outset.

A smooth branch requires a tame right inverse for (14) restricted to the
integrable centralizer-compatible codomain and exact vanishing of every
distorted trace (27). Boundary/exterior centralizer jets can influence those
traces through global Biot--Savart, but positive-core axisymmetric functions
are not arbitrary nonzero-harmonic knobs. The next concrete theorem is
therefore a two-sided boundary DA/Hodge theorem plus a matrix-valued
limiting-absorption construction for (14); its output decides whether the
Euler/global-Green structure enforces transparency.

**Route-B verdict.** The invariant nonlinear map, exact derivative,
positive-core topology, and equivalence to the direct block are established.
A smooth integrable branch remains blocked by the same explicit boundary
graph and nonnormal adjoint construction. The representation change prevents
a false pendulum branch but does not solve the trace equations by itself.
