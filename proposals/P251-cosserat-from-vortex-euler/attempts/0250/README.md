## Registered candidate methods

### Route A — exact fixed-ring transport-frequency bands

Work directly with the exact finite-`R_*` Euler/Lin generator and its full
pressure projection. Seek regular invariant transport bands on the compact
ring whose actual physical observation kernels contain both sine and cosine
quadratures for the acoustic hybrid row and a nonzero two-output optical
angle/full-current matrix. Prove that finite positive band measures give
invertible moment matrices for every finite polynomial order and that the
resulting signed source weights have explicit finite norms. Complete the
argument from parity-polynomial approximation to arbitrary finite-window
`C^r` targets, retaining tag separation, material moments, pressure tails,
phase/energy cross forms and their norm-dependent remainder bounds.

This route earns success only from an exact fixed-ring band family and its
arbitrary-order approximation proof. A finite list of nonzero derivatives or
one finite Taylor-rank determinant is evidence for that list only.

### Route B — analytic continuation with a bounded right inverse

Start from a complete source-to-observation family whose fixed-periodic or
large-ring limit has the required dense finite-window range. Continue the
full finite-`R` Euler/Lin source map—including pressure, material observation,
both acoustic parities, optical full current and all required derivative
rows—to one finite `R_*`. Construct a right inverse on the chosen target
space with an explicit accuracy-versus-source-norm bound and a perturbation
estimate small relative to that bound. The estimate must close at the same
fixed `R_*` for the whole approximation sequence.

Analyticity of finitely many matrix entries and persistence of finite rank do
not establish this route. The proof must control the infinite approximation
ladder or supply a fixed Banach-space right inverse/tame family whose remainder
does not force `R` to grow with polynomial degree or accuracy.

## Frozen selection criteria

The routes are compared in this order:

1. one exact stationary finite-radius Euler background and actual full
   Euler/Lin pressure;
2. the required `exists R_*` before `for every T,r,epsilon` quantifier order;
3. complete acoustic hybrid and optical angle/full-current observations on one
   common physical preparation, with positive law/material tags;
4. arbitrary finite-window approximation, not merely finite Taylor rank;
5. explicit source-norm, right-inverse and finite-`R` remainder control strong
   enough for the downstream preparation diagonal;
6. retention of full phase/energy/current cross forms and compatibility with
   C-CST-017 conventions without reinterpreting its accepted coefficients;
7. smallest new analytic assumption and clearest transferable construction.

Route A is tried first because it could prove the desired quantifier order
intrinsically on the fixed background. Route B is the registered
representation change if the exact transport band or its observation rank is
absent. Neither route may be selected by numerical closeness to the periodic
answer.
