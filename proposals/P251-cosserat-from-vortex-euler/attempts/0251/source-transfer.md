# Primary-source transfer licenses

## Localizable analytic flows

[Peralta-Salas and Slobodeanu, arXiv2606.13462v1, Theorem1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13462v1)
assumes an analytic interior steady velocity, C1 extension, tangency to a
smooth bounded boundary, u·grad p=0, and constant Bernoulli on each boundary
component with nonzero boundary Bernoulli gradient. It yields axisymmetry
and a toroidal domain with convex poloidal boundary curves. Those boundary
hypotheses are part of the license; the abstract's shorter wording does not
remove them. It constrains the analytic localizable route, not arbitrary
smooth compact Euler flows or all generalized-Beltrami cores. It neither
constructs the required matching profile nor gives a zero-frequency inverse.
Candidate0248B is already axisymmetric, so this is compatible guidance,
not its refutation or a campaign obstruction.

## Actual compact-flow frequency supplier

[Baldi, DOI10.1007/s00021-023-00836-1, Theorem1.1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00021-023-00836-1)
constructs an analytic action-angle chart on a toroidal shell of the Gavrilov
flow. Particle motion has two frequencies depending on I, with
Omega1=chi(K(I))K'(I) and Omega2/Omega1=sqrt(I)R(I).
The ratio is strictly increasing; K and R are analytic, and the chart is
independent of the smooth localization cutoff. The action measures enclosed
fluid volume up to a fixed constant. This is an exact fixed-geometry
transport-frequency supplier for0250. It describes particle advection;
it does not identify that transport equation with the full field-changing
Euler/Lin response or its pressure/action/current observation gain. That
operator lift is the next useful construction. Publication date is
2023-11-07; the journal volume is26(2024), article1.

## Explicit periodic Beltrami crystals

[Crystal-like arrangements, DOI10.1007/s00162-025-00748-y](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00162-025-00748-y)
presents finite hexagonal Beltrami fields and uses integrated streamlines
to identify knotted/linked tube arrangements. Exact curl eigenfields can
supply steady periodic backgrounds; the pictured topology does not itself
supply our required analytic tube-persistence or full Euler-response proof.
This is a candidate source of explicit backgrounds, not a replacement for
the completed0245 geometry review or the missing0250 response operator.
No displayed numerical orbit or parameter was selected as a verifier here.
