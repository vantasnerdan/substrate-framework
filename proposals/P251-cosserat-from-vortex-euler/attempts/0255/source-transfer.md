## Strong-absorption compact support

[Pucci--Serrin--Zou, *J. Math. Pures Appl.* 78 (1999), Theorem
2](https://pucci.sites.dmi.unipg.it/lavori/psz.pdf) gives the compact-support
principle already scoped in 0253.  For the Laplacian its primitive criterion
is finite for `g(s)~s log(1/s)^p` exactly when `p>2`.  This licenses the 0253
edge threshold for an already-existing applicable nonnegative solution.  It
does not construct the signed-transition finite-radius critical point, its
disk support, its prescribed Bessel core, or a nonlinear continuation.

## Stable flat ground states

[Díaz--Hernández--Il'yasov,
arXiv:1611.03584](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03584) proves instability of the
paper's flat ground states in dimensions one and two, with possible stability
only in the stated higher-dimensional power-exponent regimes.  The
two-dimensional warning is consistent with equations (20)--(21), but the
paper's autonomous power nonlinearities do not prove instability for every
signed logarithmic law.  The route-specific conclusion used here is instead
the exact dilation contradiction for the frozen 0253 profile.

[Díaz--Hernández--Il'yasov,
arXiv:1808.03931](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.03931) constructs flat or
compact-support ground states for an autonomous eigenvalue problem with two
power exponents `0<alpha<beta<1`, using Pohozaev and constrained fibering
arguments on specially controlled star-shaped domains.  It is a concrete
license for trying a Nehari/fibering replacement after unconstrained
minimization fails.  Its theorem does not transfer the logarithmic edge, the
signed intermediate profile required by 0256, the `r`-weighted finite-radius
operator, a prescribed constant-curl core, or physical `C-infinity` zero
extension.  Those are precisely the remaining Candidate-II rows.

## Convex compact free boundaries with an anchored core

[Jeon--Shahgholian, *Math. Ann.* 389 (2024), Theorems
1--3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-023-02653-3) constructs
nonnegative quasi-concave compact-support solutions outside a prescribed
compact convex set.  Its hypotheses require a globally nonnegative right-hand
side satisfying the paper's two-sided sublinear bounds, and the construction
prescribes a constant value on the inner set.  The regularity supplied for the
minimizing solutions is `C^{1,alpha}` at the theorem's level.

This is useful topology guidance for an outer-shell construction: an anchored
convex set plus nonnegative absorption can yield one bounded quasi-concave
positivity component.  It is not a matching theorem for 0255.  The balanced
0253 profile necessarily has a signed transition, its inner Bessel solution
requires both value and normal-derivative matching rather than constant data
on a prescribed set, its finite-radius operator has an explicit cylindrical
weight and spectral term, and the physical target requires all flat spatial
jets.  Importing only the quasi-concavity conclusion would drop every one of
these load-bearing hypotheses.
