\[

\[
 \mathcal N_R(\Phi_\eta)

## Candidate I — degenerate defining-function inverse

On the logarithmic collar use

\[
 \phi_R=e^{-1/T_R},\qquad T_R>0.                       \tag{11}
\]

Attempt 0253 reduced (2) to

\[
 T_R^2\Delta_R^*T_R+(1-2T_R)|\nabla T_R|^2
 =\frac{r^2}{R^2}\mathcal G_\eta(T_R)-\lambda^2T_R^4, \tag{12}
\]

where `mathcal G_eta` is smooth at `T=0`, equals the unchanged explicit edge
function there, and gives

\[
 T_R=0,\qquad |\nabla T_R|=r/R
 \quad\hbox{on }\partial D_R.                          \tag{13}
\]

The unknowns are the interior solution, a disk-like free-boundary graph near
the straight circle, the radial center/barycenter gauge, and `eta`.  The
candidate must construct compatible weighted spaces in which the degenerate
linearization of (12) is Fredholm after the vertical gauge and radial border,
prove tame or ordinary inverse bounds uniform enough for one finite large
`R`, and match to the positive-label interior equation without losing spatial
flatness.

Success requires:

1. evaluation of (10) and a proved adjustable zero rather than an assumed
   orthogonality;
2. kernel/cokernel identification, boundary graph equation, and a right inverse
   for the full coupled linearization;
3. nonlinear estimates for the singular-label/regular-defining-function
   composition;

   `{|x|<a_R<R}`, so revolution gives an axis-separated solid torus;
5. `C-infinity` zero extensions of velocity, vorticity and (3), without a

An `O(R^-1)` residual plus a formal inverse, or an inverse on a space that has
silently projected away the radial forcing, does not establish this route.

## Candidate II — variational compact support with profile design

Choose an auxiliary smooth bounded meridional domain `Omega_R` separated from
the axis and large enough that the desired support can lie strictly inside it.
For the same `g_eta` and `G_eta'=g_eta`, the exact weighted functional is

\[
 \mathcal E_{R,\eta}[\phi]
 =\int_{\Omega_R}\left[
   \frac{R^2}{2r}(|\nabla\phi|^2-\lambda^2\phi^2)
   +rG_\eta(\phi)\right]dr\,dz.                        \tag{14}
\]

Its admissible class starts in `H_0^1(Omega_R)` with nonnegative functions and
must be refined as required by the strong-absorption potential.  The actual
first variation, not a pointwise analogy, must yield (2).  A critical point
earns compact Euclidean support only after proving that its positivity set is
compactly contained in `Omega_R`; otherwise the auxiliary wall is part of the
candidate.

Candidate II must establish:

1. finiteness and `C^1` differentiability of (14), coercivity or the exact
   constrained/Nehari replacement, and attainment by a nonzero critical point;
2. a compact-support/dead-core theorem applicable to the weighted operator and
   the logarithmic `s log^4(1/s)` law, with support strictly away from
   `partial Omega_R`;
3. one connected disk-like positivity component rather than multiple lumps,
   axis contact, or a wall-supported state;
4. convergence or a comparison argument tying that component to
   `Phi_eta`, hence preserving the exact inner `g_eta=0` interval and the
   nonzero nondegenerate core;
5. the exact radial translation virial obtained by differentiating (14) under
   compactly supported radial translations, and its compatibility with (10);
6. enough free-boundary regularity to pass from the weak critical point to the
   physically `C-infinity` fields and pressure in (3).

The wall, a support constraint, Schwarz rearrangement, or a prescribed disk
may help construct a critical point but cannot by itself prove the natural
axis-separated disk support.  Translation or radial symmetry of an autonomous
straight functional cannot be imported into the explicitly `r`-weighted
