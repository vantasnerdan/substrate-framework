## Derivation from the physical Euler orbit

For a volume-preserving flow generated by xi, pushforward of vorticity gives

    delta omega=curl(xi cross omega0),
    delta u=P(xi cross omega0),
    delta² omega=curl(xi cross delta omega).

Here P is the orthogonal Leray projection, including its harmonic component;
the selected internal tangents have zero harmonic velocity at the background.
Differentiate E=rho integral |u|²/2 twice. At a
Beltrami background omega0=lambda u0, integration by parts yields

    E''=rho integral[|delta u|²+u0.(xi cross curl(delta u))]
       =rho integral[|delta u|²−delta u.curl(delta u)/lambda].

The verifier calculates both lines independently. This is an orbit energy
second derivative, rather than the static displacement restriction in 0038.
Define the KKS form by

    Omega(xi,eta)=rho integral omega0.(xi cross eta).

Its sign follows from i_X Omega=dE: with Euler generator X=u,
Omega(u,eta)=rho integral u.(eta cross omega)=dE(eta). Consequently, when
Omega=B dq wedge ds, the local canonical momentum is p=B s and the
quadratic action is B s q_dot−H2. This fixes the sign before calculating a
frequency. Smooth generator flows define an actual local two-dimensional
surface in the Euler orbit. A nonzero B makes its restricted form symplectic;
the quadratic action is the exact linear action of that explicit constrained
ensemble. It does not assume that all omitted Euler variations vanish.

## Positive two-generator sector on an actual tube

Physical coordinates have period 2 pi ell; x,y,z below are dimensionless.
Use the actual stationary smooth Beltrami field from 0038:

    u0=(−b sin y,a sin x,a cos x+b cos y),
    curl u0=u0/ell, a,b>0.

The nearby level sets of a cos x+b cos y around (0,0), crossed with the
axial circle, are smooth invariant tube tori. Set the dimensionless
generators, with physical displacement ell times each, to

    xi_q=(−sin y,sin x,0) cos z,
    xi_s=(cos x sin y sin z, sin x cos y sin z,
          −2 sin x sin y cos z).

Their divergence is exactly zero. The first rotates the noncircular core
at z=0 by q and the core at z=pi by −q, so q is a physical relative
cross-section orientation amplitude. The second is an axial shape
deformation with horizontal incompressible return in the same fluid. No
external fixed source supplies its reaction or stiffness. Both orbit
velocity tangents have nonzero vorticity and zero harmonic velocity.

Exact Fourier Leray projection and cell integration give, per physical volume,

    H_qq=−rho(17a²−60ab+17b²)/240,
    H_ss=5rho(a²+b²)/96, H_qs=0,
    B=−rho ell(a−b)/4.

Therefore the FULL two-dimensional Hessian is positive and the KKS form is
nondegenerate whenever

    17(a−b)² < 26ab, a != b.

The explicit noncircular cell a=2b is a simple member of this open positive
family; no observed modulus or frequency selects its amplitude ratio. It gives

    H_qq=7rho b²/48, H_ss=25rho b²/96, B=−rho ell b/4,
    L2=B s q_dot−(H_qq q²+H_ss s²)/2.

Eliminate the conjugate shape, s=B q_dot/H_ss, obtaining

    L2=I_q q_dot²/2−K_q q²/2,
    I_q=6rho ell²/25, K_q=7rho b²/48,

This is a positive ANGLE action derived from smooth stationary Euler with
the conjugate shape and its return flow retained before elimination. It is

## Localization and continuity bridge

The positive sector can also be made compactly supported in the entire
periodic Beltrami field considered on R³. This provides a precise action
quantity to transport through local Beltrami approximation. Take a smooth
cutoff chi_R(x)=chi(x/R), equal to one on the unit-ball rescaling and zero
outside twice that ball. The zero-mean Fourier generators admit bounded
periodic vector potentials A_i with curl A_i=xi_i. Set

    xi_i^R=curl(chi_R A_i).

These are exactly divergence-free and coincide with the physical generators
in the bulk. Write f_i=xi_i cross omega0=v_i+grad pi_i and choose bounded
periodic B_i with curl B_i=v_i. All such potentials exist here because the
finite Fourier fields have no zero modes. Then

    P(xi_i^R cross omega0)−curl(chi_R B_i)
      =P[(grad chi_R cross A_i) cross omega0
          −pi_i grad chi_R−grad chi_R cross B_i].

Leray is an L² contraction. The right-hand side is O(R^(1/2)) in L²,
whereas the bulk tangent norm is O(R^(3/2)). The curl error obeys the same
bound by direct differentiation of these smooth finite-Fourier fields.
In the helicity term one may remove P exactly because
integral Pf.curl(Pf)=integral f.curl f. Consequently the energy Hessian and
KKS matrix, divided by R³ integral chi², equal their periodic matrices plus
O(1/R). Weighted periodic averaging has this same bound, obtained by one
integration by parts on each nonzero Fourier harmonic. All constants are
finite norms of the displayed fields and fixed cutoff, not fitted values.
For sufficiently large finite R, strict positivity and nondegeneracy persist.

For fixed localized generators these matrices depend continuously on a
Beltrami background in C¹ on their support. Thus the local approximation
results in [EPS 1210.6271, Theorem 8.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.6271) and
[1505.01605, Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.01605) have an explicit
positive action quantity to preserve, with physical rescaling retained.
Identifying the supported core segments with a prescribed closed knotted
EPS tube is a geometric construction still to perform. The original
noncontractible periodic tube is not relabeled as such a knot.
