# 0153 — one stationary field with positive mean response and prescribed tubes

Owner /root. Parent P251 / issue198 unchanged at v0.175.0.
Construct the same-field join between0151's whole-field mean response and
0147's optical/tube geometry, not an independent example for each conjunct.

Candidates: a dominant elementary Beltrami wave plus a smooth angular
Herglotz insertion approximating prescribed local constant-curl tube data;
the small exact elliptic perturbation of0151; and a finite-periodic
multi-region constant-curl approximation with controlled action measure.
The insertion's Fourier measure is continuous before periodic quadrature,
so its normalized periodic energy can be small while its local tube jet
and global derivative bounds remain finite. This property is to be proved,
not assumed from local Runge approximation.

Target: exact stationary same-curl Euler field with actual prescribed
elliptic/toroidal and EPS knot regions, finite positive energy density,
and actual finite-time mean response close to the positive reference
at the natural spatial scale. Retain full pressure, physical common mass,
optical density/action and all conditional licenses. A vanishing-density
limit cannot silently supply a finite microrotation inertia. Optical
response errors are assessed relative to their own nonzero action, not
against an unrelated bulk absolute error.

Selection criteria: one actual global field and law, topology and local
jet control, uniform global norms, explicit spectral/action measure,
full Euler/Lin comparison at declared times/wave numbers, and nonzero
physical optical response. Fixed finite wave numbers may precede the
periodic quadrature to avoid a false uniform inverse-Bloch estimate;
an exact second derivative or acoustic-time limit needs its own bound.
Inputs0145/0147/0151 and EPS approximation are used at their actual
hypotheses. Exact Fourier/Green identities and analytic estimates are
the first oracle; no empirical comparator or numerical solver is planned.
