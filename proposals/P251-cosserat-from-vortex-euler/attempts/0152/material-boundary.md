# The actual square parcel: positive balance, but a distinct internal spin

Use the actual smooth stationary pair and its Kelvin-prepared histories
from0149. At the reference instant choose the centered square fundamental
domain D as one material parcel. The preparation displacement is supported
strictly inside D and translates the two cores oppositely. The initial
parcel domain is therefore exactly D for every preparation amplitude.
Its full mass is rho A, and its centroid momentum is zero. All velocities
and pressures, including their prepared variations, are periodic.

The fixed-domain identity of0149 is exact:

    S_E=rho integral_D r cross u=-rho/2 integral_D r² omega.

Opposite-edge weighted circulation terms cancel since r² agrees on the
opposite square edges. Its initial variation is the actual orbit momentum
p=-rho Gamma d a/2 up to the controlled finite-core second-moment term.

However the exact material Euler spin balance from0051/0052 is

    dS_D/dt=-integral_boundaryD r cross(p_phys n).             (1)

At the reference instant each opposite edge pair cancels in(1). Its
translation vector is parallel to its normal, and pressure agrees at
paired points. This is true for the unperturbed pressure and EVERY
periodic prepared pressure, without setting the pressure perturbation to
zero. Thus

    delta S_D(0)=p(0), delta S_D,dot(0)=0.                    (2)

For the genuine positive pair director mode, its exact point-limit action
instead gives p_dot=-kappa theta. In the nonzero angle phase theta(0)!=0,

persists for sufficiently small cores. The material-parcel law S_D=p
over a finite interval is therefore refuted for this square-parcel route
by an actual pressure/boundary consequence, not absence of a proof.

The mechanism is exact angular momentum transport across a fixed spatial
cell. The Eulerian angular momentum satisfies

    dS_E/dt=-integral_boundaryD r cross(p_phys n)
             -rho integral_boundaryD (r cross u)(u dot n).

The first term vanishes on the square at every instant for periodic data;
the second need not. The corresponding material boundary moves and keeps
that momentum inside the parcel. A fixed Eulerian cell spin equal to p
does not make the transported parcel's spin equal to p.

gamma=2Gamma/A and I_D=rho integral_D r². The exact identity is

    S_D=J_core+(gamma/2)I_D+B_D,
    J_core=-rho/2 integral_D r² q_core,
