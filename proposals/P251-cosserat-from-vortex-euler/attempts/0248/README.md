## Candidate A — balanced compact-ring molecule with steady continuation

This is the primary new failure-derived candidate. Start from the reviewed
0211 compact Euclidean closed-core ring and place a finite cubic/octahedral
orbit of rigidly moved, orientation- and time-reversal-related copies in one
large periodic supercell. Choose the orbit and signs from exact group moment
conditions so the total impulse and every symmetry-allowed leading translation
or rotation forcing vanish. Positive tag weights and the whole-field law stay
positive; signed circulation or response amplitudes are physical field data,
not negative probabilities.

The new construction is not the uncorrected superposition. It must derive the
full periodic interaction residual with the complete Green kernel, retain all
neutral translation/rotation/circulation rows, and solve the zero-frequency
steady Euler correction on the product of ring neighborhoods in the
group-equivariant subspace. The intended analytic mechanism is a bordered
weighted inverse plus contraction or implicit continuation after the exact
finite group cancellations. The corrected Bernoulli function and pressure
must be reconstructed so the final field satisfies stationary Euler exactly;
absorbing only a uniform drift does not count as stationarity.

At a fixed sufficiently large but finite separation ratio `d=P/R`, the target
is an exact periodic stationary field containing the same compact Euclidean
finite-core tubes, not a zero-density `d -> infinity` limit. With `N` rings in
one cell, the physical line/tag coefficient density has the form

```text
rho_meas(d) = whole_law_Gram * mean_tag_fraction
              * N * j_ring(d) / P^3,
```

with the appropriate ring-length factor retained inside `j_ring(d)` or written
separately once the source convention is opened. The proof must show
`j_ring(d)>0` after the steady correction and record its difference from the
isolated-ring value, the tube volume fraction, complete ambient mass, field
energy density, pressure norm, and interaction/correction costs. Choosing one
finite `d` after a proved positivity margin earns usable positive density;
letting `d` diverge before normalization does not.

The same corrected stationary field must then carry the reviewed 0204/0226
finite-window curved/history, action, and current supplier. The transfer target
is a quantitative perturbation statement for the actual Euler/Lin propagator,
pressure projection, observation rows, phase/energy forms, and current through
the parent spatial order. Every cost must enter the existing preparation/error
diagonal before the parent imports the result. Similar geometry alone is not a
source-transfer theorem.
