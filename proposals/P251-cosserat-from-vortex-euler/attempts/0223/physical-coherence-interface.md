# Physical clock/current coefficients left by the actual pressure repair

This is the finite-output continuation of the compact graph construction.
It identifies which real coefficients still need construction. It is not
a demand that the entire Euler state be an autonomous finite subsystem.

## 1. The first physical clock correction is real and tunable

On this actual cell, reflect b to -b. Denote its scalar action by R.
The exact operators have R psi=psi, RH=HR and RT=-TR. The remainder
R_operator in (7) of the second-jet source commutes with this reflection;
H^-1 T psi anticommutes. The regular closed-orbit phase is chosen so
R conjugate(exp(il theta_o))=exp(il theta_o). These are geometric
identities of the nonlinear cell, not a prescribed Floquet frame.

The Euclidean physical angle row A in (3) of that source is R-even:
both ab and T reverse sign. Its nonzero reference carrier amplitude
C=A(s) is consequently real. Choose the approximate inverse kernels
by averaging them with these exact reflection/conjugation symmetries.
This preserves their error and norm estimates.

In the formal single-carrier coefficient interface of (5) of the
compact-preparation source, the anti-linear R-conjugation makes the
secular correction Z1 odd and the constant correction Z0 even. Hence

    A(Z1 s) is purely imaginary,
    A(Z0 s)+B(-s) is real.                          (1)

The second term in the latter assertion follows directly from the
odd factor ab in B and its explicit -i. Thus the actual first angle
jet has the form

    theta(t,k)=e^(-i nu t)[C+k(a-i C v t)]+O(k²),   (2)

with real a,v. Here v includes the genuine pressure-complement row;
it is not asserted to equal the label Doppler psi. For smooth bands
(2) is the corresponding frequency-integral interface plus the
explicit inverse/band errors; it is not an isolated eigenstate claim.

An actual carrier-center derivative E'(0)=e changes v to v+nu'(E)e.
Because the actual nonlinear nu'(E) is nonzero on the selected annulus,
e=-v/nu'(E) cancels this observed first clock derivative. An actual
input-amplitude derivative removes the constant a, including the
reference-amplitude change C'(E)e. Neither operation renames the
physical angle or deletes the complementary Euler field. All of their
physical G/S/phase/energy changes remain the rows already derived.

## 2. The real second-order clock condition is not a band-center identity

Normalize C=1. A real coherent coefficient family before this first
derivative change can be written

    theta=e^(-i nu t){1+k(a-i v t)
                 +k²[c-i(b+a v)t-d t²/2]}+...,     (3)

with real coefficients, retaining actual approximation errors. An
autonomous harmonic through this order requires d=v². A carrier
shift by frequency derivative e_nu changes

    v -> v+e_nu,
    d -> d+2v e_nu+e_nu²,

so the invariant d-v² is unchanged. A second carrier derivative can
change b but cannot repair that invariant. These statements concern
the actual observed columns, not their frequency average. The new
pressure source has not yet been shown to give d=v², or a correlated
same-field family canceling its nonzero value. Root0187's correlation
mechanism is a competing METHOD; its independently constructed mode
coefficients are not silently substituted for these Euler rows.

## 3. The spin comparison has its own exposed coefficients

After selecting a real paired family, write its second coefficient as

    theta2=e^(-i nu t)(a20+a21 t+a22 t²),
    G2=e^(-i nu t)(g20+g21 t+g22 t²),
    F(q1)=e^(-i nu t)(f10+f11 t),
    J(h0)=e^(-i nu t)j00.                          (4)

These are precisely the actual rows of the second-jet source. They
give, without a canonical-momentum substitution,

    S2=e^(-i nu t){-i nu g20+g21+i(f10+j00)
              +[-i nu g21+2g22+i f11]t-i nu g22 t²}. (5)

For a chosen kinetic/physical chart, comparison with
j theta2_t+j2 theta0_t uses (5) directly. It has not been solved by
matching the angle's coefficient d alone. Depending on that chart,
j2 is either a measured gradient-inertia row or an explicitly required
target; it is not an adjustable value inferred from phase controls.

The hybrid translation first-K row uses only G0=j theta0. G2 enters
curl G first at K³ and is therefore not independently required to
equal j theta2 merely to establish that first-K coupling. Retaining
(4)--(5) avoids adding such an unnecessary condition while keeping
the real spin and current comparison available to the full action.
