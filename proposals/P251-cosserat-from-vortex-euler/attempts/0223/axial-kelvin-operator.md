## Exact solenoidal representation and full pressure

    xi=(h,J grad s+ik grad f), f=H^-1 h.              (1)

generally a separate harmonic row; it vanishes on the actual C2-even

(a,b)->(-a,-b), normal xi,w are odd, and the axial-only full operator
preserves this symmetry. This is a physical symmetry statement, not
deletion of the harmonic pressure row.

Removing only a genuine full Bloch gradient from xi cross omega gives
the exact Kelvin velocity w=P_k r with

    r_X=Ts+ik[psi s-grad psi.grad f],
    r_perp=(s+h)grad psi+ik psi J grad f.             (2)

Let

    C=div[(s+h)grad psi],
    d=C+ik T(s-f)-k²[psi s-grad psi.grad f],
    P=(H+k²)^-1 d.                                  (3)

Then the COMPLETE pressure projection is

    w_X=Ts+ik[psi s-grad psi.grad f+P],
    w_perp=(s+h)grad psi+ik psi J grad f+grad P.       (4)

The constant pressure row is not singularly discarded. Since H psi=psi,
<grad psi.grad f>=<psi h>, its exact mean is
<P>=-<psi(s-h)>. This cancels the axial mean of(4). On the nonzero
normal Fourier modes the denominator is |q|²+k², uniformly bounded
away from zero for fixed small k. These formulas follow from the full
three-dimensional Leray projector, not a planar replacement.

centered reference Q. Its centroid variation vanishes in this reflection

    delta_chi=chi_psi[Ts-ik grad f.grad psi],
