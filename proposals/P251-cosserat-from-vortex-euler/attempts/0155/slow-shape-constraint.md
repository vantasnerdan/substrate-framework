# Simultaneous slow-shape action and overlap: an exact joining constraint

This is a new calculation within the still-active stationary radial m2
candidate, not a no-go for0155 or for actual material tube directors.
It corrects the inference that the individually attainable positive
clock and half-overlap can simply be combined on one radial tag.

Take f_n(x)=exp(-x/2)L_n^1(x), dmu=x dx, and0<=chi<=1. Write

    A=integral chi f_n dmu,
    B=integral chi x f_n dmu,
    C=integral chi dmu,
    N=integral f_n² dmu=n+1.

The leading physical covariance-angle clock and canonical-spin overlap
of `elliptic-core-algebra.md` are

    gamma/cD=-(2n+2)+B/(2A),
    eta=A²/(CN).

Changing the real mode sign allows A>0 without changing either
observable. A positive or zero clock then requires B>=4(n+1)A.
For t=1/[3(n+1)], this implies

    A <= integral chi f_n[1+t(x-4(n+1))] dmu.

Apply Cauchy--Schwarz in chi dmu and then chi<=1. Exact Laguerre
recurrences give

    integral x f_n² dmu = 2(n+1)N,
    integral x² f_n² dmu = 6(n+1)²N,
    integral f_n²[1+t(x-4(n+1))]² dmu=N/3.

Therefore

    gamma>=0  ==>  eta<=1/3.                             (1)

The half-overlap eta=1/2 is thus unavailable with a positive clock in
THIS stationary nonnegative radial covariance-marker class, for every
radial index n. Corrections vanishing with delta_e cannot remove its
fixed1/6 margin. The bound is not a universal Euler bound: it uses the
same radial marker in the covariance angle and spin, chi<=1, and the
specified leading Laguerre family.

The positive slow-shape candidate remains meaningful at smaller eta.
For n=0 a sufficiently narrow invariant annulus centered at x0>4 has
gamma approximately (x0/2-2)cD>0 and eta>0. This is an actual level-set
tag, not painted lobes. Its slow-period control and equality of physical
spin with the desired action inertia remain separate constructions.

A failure-derived alternative observes the angle of one actual invariant
boundary while retaining spin in a larger material parcel, including its
ambient part explicitly. For a ground-state boundary at xb>4 and an
outer disk of radius-coordinate xd, the initial overlap becomes

    eta_boundary=4 exp(-xb/2)
       [1-(1+xd/2)exp(-xd/2)].                            (2)

For4<xb<2 log8 the upper limit of (2) is larger than1/2, so a unique
finite xd gives eta_boundary=1/2 and positive *initial* boundary clock.
This is a concrete initial-time repair, not yet a finite-time one:
different radii travel at different axial speeds, so the parcel spin
contains their complete Laplace/Fourier history, whereas the boundary
has its own phase. A successful finite-time construction must match or
control that actual dephasing. The larger parcel is not called the
inner tube. These distinct candidates remain in the continuation ledger.
