# 0063 — the two physical structure-removal limits

Main owns this fixed limit reconciliation for the normalized stationary
ensemble. The selected coherent core population and the entire fluctuating
Euler background are different objects. Removing only the retained spin
population leaves the background affine shear energy and yields the
divergence-free Navier–Cauchy sector. Removing all background vorticity as
well yields the linearized Euler fluid sector. Both remove the degenerate
spin coordinate BEFORE dividing by its vanishing inertia.

The existing CST005 all-coefficient L_v scaling remains a valid conditional
regression of the second limit; it is not relabeled as removing marked
coherence at a fixed nonzero Gaussian variance. Exact determinant scaling
and the unreduced mass/energy matrices are the oracle. The full microscopic
coefficient provenance remains with 0057/0059, not this limit algebra.
No empirical comparator, numerical solver or small-ratio inference is used.
