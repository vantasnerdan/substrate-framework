# The physical branch map fixes the locking term and its curvature shift

This is an exact algebraic implication with an explicit physical supplier
interface. The observations and full forms in its hypotheses are NOT
declared to exist by the algebra. They are the active Euler constructions
in0223/0226. Incompressibility restricts U to transverse polar vectors;
Phi is an unrestricted axial vector. Work at one common real laboratory
K and set C=i[K cross], so C is Hermitian and C^2=|K|^2 P_T.

## Actual branch coordinates and the complete kinetic metric

Let A denote the acoustic branch's physical U amplitude, and B the
optical branch's physical Phi amplitude. Normalize those two literal
observations, not a phase-space coordinate substituted for them. The
first-gradient physical branch rows required here are

    U=A-beta C B,       Phi=B+C A/2,    beta=j/(2rho).       (1)

Equation(1) is the second-jet model map. An actual supplier may have
remainders; they must be smaller than the action/current scale where
this implication is used. The optical row is exactly the leading
hybrid-current relation when G=j Phi. The acoustic row expresses the
physical rigid-rotation response, which is a separate supplier fact.

Write (U,Phi)=T(A,B) and M0=diag(rho I_T,j I). The full branch kinetic
metric that gives this physical mass is

    Mb=T^* M0 T
      =diag(rho I_T+j C^2/4,
             j I+j^2 C^2/(4rho)).                        (2)

The off-diagonal kinetic blocks cancel because rho beta=j/2, with the
Hermitian curl convention retained. Equation(2) is EXACT for the model
map(1), not a neglect of its second-gradient inertia. Both branch
metrics are positive. Inverting T and pulling back(2) returns exactly
M0 at every K. An actual inherited metric differing from(2) leaves its
corresponding physical mass or cross term; it cannot be set to M0 by
calling the branch amplitudes physical. Its construction/normalization
is part of the supplier interface.

## Full energy and the second spatial jet

Suppose the actual normalized branch action has no remaining cross
blocks at the claimed order and has squared-frequency rows

    acoustic: a |K|^2,
    optical: nu^2 I+c_T |K|^2 P_T+c_L |K|^2 P_L,

where rho,j,a,nu are positive. Its complete conserved stiffness in
branch coordinates is Kb=Mb times these rows. Pull back BOTH forms:
`Kphys=T^-* Kb T^-1`. Expanding the exact expression through degree2,
using `T^-1=I-Q+Q^2+O(|K|^3)` for T=I+Q, gives

    K_UU=(mu+alpha)|K|^2 P_T,
    K_U,Phi=K_Phi,U=-2alpha C,
    K_Phi,Phi=4alpha I+gamma_T |K|^2 P_T
                              +gamma_L |K|^2 P_L,       (3)

with coefficients DERIVED from those actual branch inputs:

    mu=rho a,             alpha=j nu^2/4,
    gamma_T=j(c_T-alpha/rho),   gamma_L=j c_L.           (4)

The transverse curvature shift is essential. Optical frequency
curvature is not itself gamma_T/j: part of the optical dispersion is
the physical translational reaction. Conversely the acoustic branch's
leading restoring coefficient is mu/rho, not (mu+alpha)/rho, since the
relaxed microrotation carries its required first-gradient response.

One can also see the locking coefficient directly from the energy:
the leading inverse optical row is B=Phi-CU/2, so its energy contributes
`j nu^2 |Phi-CU/2|^2/2`. Its mixed coefficient is -j nu^2/2 and its
translational curl square is j nu^2/8. These yield the SAME alpha in
both off-diagonal blocks and the diagonal terms. This uses the measured
spin j and actual clock nu; it is not the retired straight-tube tension
substitution alpha=L_v T/6.

To obtain positive bulk couple-curvature coefficients requires
`c_T>alpha/rho` and `c_L>0`. Merely positive optical branch curvature
does not supply the first inequality. The actual preparation or mode
must establish those margins and its density/normalization scales.

## Equations, boundary information and precise limits

With physical mass M0, equation(3) gives the incompressible linear
micropolar equations (and pressure multiplier) in the canonical
convention. It leaves longitudinal spin free to fluctuate; only the
displacement is solenoidal. This is a second-spatial-jet statement.
The exact pulled-back model generally has higher rational spatial
terms, so an exact all-K determinant is not replaced by its degree2 jet.

The two bulk curvature coefficients do not determine all three local
gradient-energy coefficients. Writing
`gamma_T=c_s+c_a`, `gamma_L=2(c_s+c_tr)` leaves the familiar boundary
divergence freedom: changing c_s by d, c_a by -d and c_tr by -d changes
the local energy by `d[tr(G^2)-(tr G)^2]`. For G=grad Phi this is
`d div[(Phi.grad)Phi-Phi div Phi]`. Thus the bulk equations alone do
not identify the local boundary couple traction. Its full microscopic
gradient/action or boundary flux is an additional supplier row if that
local constitutive statement is claimed. No arbitrary member of this
family is silently assigned as a derived boundary modulus.

The actual joint interface is therefore: the two physical branch
observation rows(1), the inherited phase/mass(2), full energy and cross
blocks, nonzero physical spin/density, correct optical curvatures, and
the requested local boundary gradient information. Given those, the
bulk locking and dispersion implications(3)--(4) follow without a
further microscopic ansatz. Without any one of them, this proof names
the precise next construction; it does not substitute a conditional
matrix match for the user's full Euler objective.
