# Conservative orientation response under a local product Haar closure

Let the unresolved orientation state of each coherent cell be R_a in SO(3).
Let z denote all remaining geometric and internal variables. The declared
ergodic contrast has conditional law

    dP(R_1,...,R_N | z) = product_a dHaar(R_a),

independent of a putative coherent frame Q_a. Crucially, R includes the
complete orientation-dependent state or its covariant conditional law,
not just the visible core axis with hidden frame-locked variables omitted.
Let E(R_1,...,R_N,z) be its actual finite-energy interaction, including
all pair interactions and fluctuation energy. Assume integrability and,
when differential response is used, the usual dominated derivative bounds.

Define the coarse static energy with local frame shifts by

    Ebar(Q,z)=integral E(R_1 Q_1,...,R_N Q_N,z) product_a dHaar(R_a).

Right invariance of normalized Haar measure permits S_a=R_a Q_a at each
site independently. Therefore Ebar(Q,z)=Ebar(I,z) EXACTLY, including finite
angle changes. Every first and second derivative with respect to Q and
relative neighboring Q vanishes. Discrete conservative torque and couple
stiffness vanish before taking the affine continuum limit, so that limit
does not need a spurious cancellation of positive energy terms.
Position/strain-dependent energy may survive the integration and supplies
the translational sector; this proof asserts nothing about its modulus.

For the SO(2) subgroup, E=K[1-cos(theta_1-theta_2+q_1-q_2)] is an exact
exposing example. Independent uniform phases give Ebar=K>0 but every
coherent torque and curvature is zero. Uniform MARGINAL phases with the
correlation theta_1=theta_2 instead give
Ebar=K[1-cos(q_1-q_2)], hence a positive stiffness K at zero relative angle.
One-point isotropy and a vanishing signed frame map are therefore insufficient.
The same distinction applies to SO(3), by the product-Haar proof above.

The coherent ensemble used in 0048+ is not this contrast ensemble: it ties
the physical angles between its circulation-reversed members and varies
their conjugate fluid momenta independently. Its positive Schur inertia and
stiffness are fully consistent with the present zero response. Confusing
circulation pairing with independent orientation ergodicity would erase the
very mechanism being constructed.

For a dynamical no-Cosserat closure one additionally resolves no coherent
angular probability current after the fast equilibration/time averaging.
Uniform orientation density alone allows nonzero rotational current; the
Haar theorem proves a conservative static response, not zero current or
zero finite-frequency susceptibility. The combined statistical premises
are independently falsifiable through conditional correlations and currents.
They retain the original N6 no-coherent-spin closure while supplying the
previously absent conservative-energy implication without deleting fluctuations.
