## 1. Exact action correction, not frequency averaging

First isolate the constant-clock part of the actual C-CST-011 response;
its physical clock, mass and current errors are restored below. Write
f(p_*+d)=omega0²+A d+B d²+O(d³), with B>0. Every member has the
same physical initial angle/rate. Its columns are

    F=cos(sqrt(f)t), G=sin(sqrt(f)t)/sqrt(f).

These are a soluble response jet, not an oscillator inserted in place
of Euler: the transfer to these rows is the quantitative content of the
actual material-clock construction. Perform the actual coherent average
and Wronskian pullback of `coherent-action.md`, with geometric weight c_s.
At the complete second spatial jet define

    H(t)=[omega0² t²-sin²(omega0 t)]/(4omega0⁴),
    Q(t)=sin²(omega0 t)/(2omega0²).

Then the derived Wronskian and both action coefficients are

    W_s=1-c_s A² k² H(t),
    M_s=J_s[1+c_s A² k² H(t)],
    K_s=J_s{omega0²+c_s k²[B+A²(omega0² H(t)-Q(t))]}, (1)

through k². J_s=j0+J2,s k² is the actual INITIAL phase mass, including
carrier normalization and packet-envelope derivatives. The complete
mass-rate connection is M_s,t=J_s c_s A² k² H_t. No frequency or
individual mass was averaged after elimination.

Equation(1) is genuinely time dependent when A!=0. At that order the
exact actual mean equation can also be written with the retained branch
memory of0138:

    q_tt+(omega0²+c_s B k²)q
          -c_s A² k² integral_0^t sin(omega0(t-r))/omega0 q_free(r)dr=0.

On the actual two-dimensional coherent initial-data family the integral
equals Q(t)q_free+[sin(omega0t)cos(omega0t)-omega0t]/(2omega0³) q_free,t.
This reproduces the mass-rate and potential terms in(1). Their omission
would silently replace a prepared-history action by an autonomous one.

## 4. Exact, conditional and approximate statements

The exact statement is the time-dependent physical action obtained from
the actual averaged Euler phase and its registered observations. Its
Hamiltonian and all currents are explicitly those of the moving chart.

The autonomous reference is a declared conditional second-gradient
continuum with computed stationary reference coefficients and recorded
physical derivative maps. Its equations follow exactly from that
conditional action. Actual prepared Euler histories approximate it on
the selected fixed time window with the above natural-scale coefficient
errors; it is not an exact autonomous theorem about all those histories.
The already accepted exact C-CST-009 conditional construction remains
separate authority and is not reproved merely by freezing(1).

An exact autonomous prepared second jet would need the actual scalar
rows to make A² H_t and the remaining clock/mean connections vanish (or
an additional genuine stationary preparation construction). This is the
precise stronger row, not a requirement for an all-k invariant manifold.
The controlled diagonal realization can meet a predeclared positive
relative tolerance at finite parameters without claiming that row; its
full joint license additionally uses the physical maps and scaled current
and gradient bounds in the accompanying files.

The new actual0175 counterexample is distinct from optical branch variance:
on u=(cos z+sin y,sin z,cos y), the whole-law Kelvin-D response satisfies
R_D^(n)(0)=[-4/15,0,0,0,-2/25] for n=0,...,4. Thus its acoustic
f2_tt=-4/15-t^4/300+O(t^6) is not constant. This refutes a universal
constant-lambda/stationary-law cancellation, not0170's controlled bound
or C-CST-009's conditional action. Optical band-edge selection cannot
remove that independent actual acoustic row. It does not follow that
every coupled physical U/Phi action is nonautonomous: eliminating Phi
itself creates memory, and corrected joint preparations may change the
bare mean rows. The test concerns the displayed preparation, not a new
requirement that its isolated mean must close before a coupled continuum.
