u=(-psi_z/r,F(psi)/r,psi_r/r), with physical density rho>0. The circulation
law F and Bernoulli law are explicitly derived inputs, not fitted moduli.

but finite ring radius. Swirl is chosen afterward at an explicitly finite
small value if the proof requires that order. No uniform multi-limit
statement is assumed.

new geometry has a nonzero periodic velocity on that circle and surrounding
invariant material tori. Its period, transverse rotation number and twist
are actual trajectory quantities. Literal curl u=lambda u, generalized

propositions. Only their displayed equations can identify which holds.

adding swirl. Its actual passive transport and moving-tag centroid,
