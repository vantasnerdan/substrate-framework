# 0046 — an intrinsic common-angle Euler rotor

Main owns this directory. Parent: P251 N3/N4, continuation of 0040/0042.
This candidate is generated by the mismatch between a material rigid-body
kinetic lift and a fixed isovortical Euler leaf: appending a rigid rotation
velocity also changes vorticity, so the two reductions cannot simply be
combined. The proposed repair is to derive both common and relative angle
inertias on a single four-generator Euler orbit before eliminating either
momentum. No assigned rigid-body mass or target inertia enters this route.

Frozen construction: an actual nonaxisymmetric stationary Beltrami tube in
a finite, rotationally invariant cylinder with periodic axial coordinate.
The common rotation is the domain's exact SO(2) symmetry. Its canonical
partner is an explicit compact circulation-preserving variation with nonzero
angular-momentum derivative, supplemented by a negative-helicity cage
variation to obtain positive energy. A different axial harmonic supplies
the positive internal core-angle/cage pair; orthogonality keeps their full
quadratic matrices separate. All boundary circulation and curl-inverse
conditions are included before reduction.

Selection criteria: exact smooth Euler field, physical common/core angle,
one shared KKS action, derived positive inertia, explicit material-domain
and ambient/EPS compatibility. No empirical comparator or fitted coefficient.
There is no numerical remainder; exact PDE, symmetry, positive-integral
bounds and finite-dimensional Routh algebra are the appropriate oracle.
The finite-cylinder result does not itself prove that this boundary can be
inserted unchanged into an arbitrary prescribed EPS knot or a tiled medium.
