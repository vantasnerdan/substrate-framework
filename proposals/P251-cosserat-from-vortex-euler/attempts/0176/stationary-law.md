# Explicit stationary-phase preparation does not remove spectral variance

This executes registered candidate C at its simplest positive-action
representation. It changes the PREPARATION from a painted phase sum
to a stationary marked phase law; it does not claim this covers every
stationary Euler marked law.

For each actual positive normal-mode phase with frequency omega>0,
use the stationary uniform phase distribution on its finite canonical
ellipse, with its physical linear observation q. A mixture over modes
has the positive normalized autocorrelation measure dnu(omega): the
mode weights are its positive population weights times the squared
physical observation amplitude. Conditioning by the observed initial
configuration (the covariance/Riesz preparation) gives the actual
normalized linear response

    F(t)=integral cos(omega t)dnu(omega), integral dnu=1.

The conjugate common initial-rate response is retained separately;
one does not average already eliminated masses. The initial action
normalization is fixed by that same covariance and phase metric.

Even this EXACT stationary phase law is not a single autonomous
oscillator unless its observed frequency support collapses. If
F_tt+w0²F=0 on an interval with F(0)=1,F_t(0)=0, then

    w0²=integral omega² dnu,
    0=F_tttt(0)-F_tt(0)²
     =integral omega⁴ dnu-(integral omega² dnu)².

The last quantity is the nonnegative variance of omega². It vanishes
only on one squared frequency (opposite frequency signs describe the
same real mode and may be combined). This is not an empirical or
numerical stability test. It is the exact fourth-time derivative of
the proposed stationary physical response.

For a common-carrier spatial jet,0172 gives the more local version:
the coherent gradient mass contains a positive sum/integral of the
squares of actual first frequency derivatives. Whole phase or time-
reversal stationarity does not cancel that even term. A law supported
on genuine physical band-edge modes can remove it; selecting such
modes is a new actual operator/observation construction, not a consequence
of stationarity itself. The physical current rows still have to come
from those same modes.

Thus the explicit uniform-phase positive-action preparation is
established, but its proposed automatic exact autonomous closure is
refuted by the named spectral-variance mechanism when more than one
squared frequency is observed. This verdict belongs only to that
preparation. Stationary-domain normal modes with nonzero physical
overlap remain a constructive next route; equation(10) of the companion
file shows that an overlap smaller than1 is not a coupling no-go.
