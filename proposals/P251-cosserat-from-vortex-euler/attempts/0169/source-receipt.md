# Primary-source licenses for the global localization continuation

Sources were selected in the validated0169 contract before body use.

[Gavrilov, arXiv1810.08020v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08020v1)
constructs a smooth steady velocity supported near a circle. Sections2-3
first build an isobaric local field, then use a pressure cutoff supported
away from the central circle: the uncut swirl is not smooth on that circle.
Thus the final object is a toroidal shell, not a freely specified smooth
elliptic-core jet. Section4's generalized-Beltrami terminology concerns
Bernoulli/pressure dependence; it does not assert curl u=f u.

[Constantin-La-Vicol, arXiv1903.11699](https://cims.nyu.edu/~vicol/CLV1.pdf)
give the localizable Grad-Shafranov system in equations22-23. It imposes
both the stationary elliptic equation and a separate speed/streamfunction
constraint. Theorem2 gives the local field; smooth compact support follows
by omitting its central nonsmooth swirl. This is not an arbitrary-core
global-extension theorem. Their disjoint-template assembly is compatible
with exact Euler, but does not supply a cross-template interaction.

[Ambrosetti-Struwe1989](https://www.rism.it/images/2022/11/The_monotonicity_trick_and_applications/Ambrosetti-Struwe-1989.pdf)
is the full Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis108,97-109 paper,
not the shorter Applied Mathematics Letters announcement in the inventory.
Its positive boundary vorticity produces a discontinuous extended
nonlinearity (introduction and section1). Theorem4.2 establishes a bounded
vortex core and an actual global traveling-ring solution; it is not a
C-infinity freely prescribed-core theorem. Smooth flat-onset alternatives
remain a distinct existence problem, not supplied by this citation.

No source above is promoted as evidence for a new acoustic/optical
transfer. The following localizability and interaction calculations are
new route analysis in this attempt. The full parent remains active.
