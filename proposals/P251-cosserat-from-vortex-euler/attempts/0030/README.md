## Exact local construction

Take a two-dimensional uniform-vorticity contrast patch of circulation Γ>0,
constant area A=πab, semiaxes a>b, and angle θ. Extrusion gives a straight Euler
tube. Its exterior linear flow is

    u_ext = (e x − Ω y, Ω x − e y),   e>0.

The total vorticity is 2Ω outside and 2Ω+ω inside, where ω=Γ/A. Put

    λ=a/b>1, Q=λ+λ⁻¹>2, D=sqrt(Q²−4), C=ρΓA/(8π).

At fixed Γ and A the canonical angle momentum is p=−CQ, minus angular impulse
per unit length. The shape-dependent renormalized physical Hamiltonian is

    H(Q,θ)=−Cω log(Q+2)+CeD sin(2θ)−CΩQ.

The self-energy logarithm and cross-energy retain their physical signs. The
cross term follows independently from ρΓe⟨xy⟩, since the exact uniform-ellipse
moment is ⟨xy⟩=(a²−b²)sin(2θ)/8. Hamilton's equations yield

    λ_dot=2eλ cos(2θ),
    θ_dot=Ω+ω/(Q+2)−eQ/D sin(2θ).

These are the Kida equations, including the Kirchhoff limit
θ_dot=ωλ/(λ+1)² when e=Ω=0. The imposed linear field has unbounded energy on the
plane; H is a renormalized patch Hamiltonian with specified exterior flow, not
the energy of a compact isolated vortex ensemble.

For any Q0>2 choose the stationary exterior rotation

    θ0=−π/4, Ω0=−ω/(Q0+2)−eQ0/D0.

Differentiate the full H at fixed Ω before imposing this equilibrium. Its
quadratic Hessian in (δθ,δp) has zero mixed term and

    K=H_θθ=4CeD>0,
    H_pp=(ω/(Q+2)²+4e/D³)/C>0.

The canonical action δp δθ_dot−(H_pp δp²+Kδθ²)/2 therefore eliminates δp
algebraically and gives

    L2=I δθ_dot²/2−K δθ²/2,
    I=C/(ω/(Q+2)²+4e/D³),   frequency²=K/I.

This derives an actual ANGLE stiffness and an inertia from Euler's patch action.
It does not identify a quadratic angular-rate energy with an elastic potential
and does not choose K to fit an optical gap. Units per tube length are
[H]=[K]=kg m/s², [p]=kg m/s and [I]=kg m. K tends to zero when e tends to zero
at fixed noncircular shape with Ω adjusted as above. At λ=1 the angle chart
loses meaning; the result is stated on λ>1. Positivity is proved only within
the exact elliptic-shape manifold, not against every vortex-patch boundary mode.

## What the conditional isotropic contraction would supply

If a cell strain eigenframe β is transported by the macroscopic material polar
rotation, then δθ−δβ=n·(Φ−curl(u)/2) for its tube direction n. An isotropic
length average gives

    W_lock=L_v K |Φ−curl(u)/2|²/6,
    alpha=L_v K/12,    j=L_v I/3,    4 alpha/j=K/I.

These factors are the same action's stiffness and inertia; there are no two
unrelated definitions of alpha. Equal counter-circulation populations can
cancel the background angular-impulse term at this scalar level, but extension
to a moving three-dimensional triad requires its own symplectic reduction.

This contraction is conditional. A strain eigenframe is a velocity-gradient
object; it is not automatically the polar rotation of a material displacement.
The Euler dynamics of the exterior source, the work that changes β, and its
back reaction must be included. For prescribed β(t), H depends explicitly on
time and dH/dt=(∂H/∂β)β_dot (plus amplitude/rotation work); energy conservation
of a closed micropolar medium does not follow from the local oscillator.
Asserting the material-frame identification without a microscopic source would
postulate the very coupling being sought. No wryness term follows from this
single-cell calculation.

## Mutual-vortex continuation, with an exact angle energy

Replace the prescribed exterior strain by another patch. For a circular source
patch of circulation Γ2, its exterior logarithmic field is exact. At separation
d>a+b_source the interaction with the ellipse is

    H12=−ρΓΓ2/(2π) ⟨log|d exp(iβ)−z|⟩_ellipse.

The uniformly convergent exterior series is

    H12=−ρΓΓ2/(2π) log d
        +ρΓΓ2/(2π) sum_{n≥1} [M_2n/(2n d^(2n))]
                                      cos(2n(θ−β)),
    M_2n=Catalan(n) ((a²−b²)/4)^n.

The script independently integrates the first three even moments. The leading
anisotropic term is B cos(2(θ−β)), with

    B=ρΓΓ2(a²−b²)/(16πd²).

For ΓΓ2>0 it has positive angular Hessian 4B at θ−β=π/2. The complete series
is an exact frozen-shape interaction; the quadrupole term alone is an asymptotic
approximation. Dependence on θ−β gives equal and opposite torques without an
external reservoir. Neither the transverse orientation nor positivity of this
one Hessian component proves a stationary or stable freely moving pair.

The circular source is deformed by the other patch. Its exterior field ceases to
be exactly monopolar, and the target ellipse sees spatial derivatives above
linear order. Thus two arbitrary ellipses are not an invariant manifold of the
full Euler equation. The Melander–Zabusky–Styczek moment model is explicitly a
Hamiltonian truncation, not an exact interacting-patch solution.

Even within that model the pair's translation participates in the canonical
action. For centered patches with Γ+Γ2≠0, the orbital angular impulse is

    J_orb=ρ ΓΓ2 d²/[2(Γ+Γ2)].

Common-rotation reduction fixes the sum of orbital and shape angular impulses;
it changes the shape Hessian and hence the derived I. Adding an independent
ρ|u_dot|²/2 term without this reduction would not establish the required action.
The next concrete route is a stationary interacting-patch branch (including
contour-shape corrections), its constrained symplectic Hessian, then a lattice
or isotropic pair closure of that same interaction energy. Pair second spatial
moments could supply wryness only after those constructions and continuum
scales are specified. Smooth EPS tube embedding is another open dependency.
