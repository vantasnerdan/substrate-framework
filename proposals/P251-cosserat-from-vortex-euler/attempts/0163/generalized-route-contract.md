# Append-only candidate: ordered generalized-force-free acoustic transfer

Registered by root and centrally validated before new implementation.
This continuation follows the full constant-curl current/action sign,
not an isolated term or numerical eigenvalue.

Keep the same psi, v, density, full periodic cell, physical momentum and
Euler action. Use W_C=sqrt(C+lambda^2 psi^2), C>0, which gives an actual
stationary generalized-force-free field. In the explicitly moving axial
frame U0=sqrt(C), compare h_C=W_C-U0 with the planar field. Derive all
pressure, axial shear, mean and phase-action errors. Choose each finite
nonzero k and finite acoustic window first; C may then be chosen finite
and sufficiently large to meet a prescribed physical observation/action
error. This is an ordered family, not fixed-C homogenization.

The first proof route is an acoustic-adapted full-operator Duhamel bound,
using the already proved planar group and its exact mean/translation
current variables. The intended bound is proportional to a derived power
of ||h_C||/|k|, with every power determined before claiming a window.
The stronger next route uses r=b-(W'/zeta')eta and a W-dependent slow
corrector to seek fixed-C continuity. Failure of that stronger route does
not undo the ordered family. Local optical trapping requires its own
computed W derivatives; constant-curl optical formulas are not imported
unchanged. No numerical comparator or soft-spectrum design is used.
