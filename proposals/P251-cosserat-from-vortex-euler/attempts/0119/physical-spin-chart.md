## 1. Exact scalar angle, rate and spin transformation

Use0114's canonical coordinates y=(q,p), form Omega=beta J0,
J0=[[0,1],[-1,0]], and generator B=rJ=-rJ0. Its action is

    L=beta p qdot-(-beta r)(q²+p²)/2, beta*r<0.

Let c(t) be the ACTUAL scalar angle row, including E_N and R_N and their
geometric observation maps. Let s(t) be the actual projected material spin
row. Define

    theta=c y, d=cdot+cB, theta_dot=d y,
    T=[c;d], W=det T, x=(theta,v)=T y.

For c=(a,b), W=a bdot-b adot-r(a²+b²). The scalar angle/rate chart exists
exactly when W is nonzero. A nonzero angle row alone is insufficient.
For example c=(cos(gamma t),sin(gamma t)) has W=gamma-r; a coordinate
winding can change or destroy the scalar mass. This is an exposing
counterexample, not a permissible way to choose the physical frame.

Where W!=0 use the exact embedding y=T^-1 x. The full moving action,
including (T^-1)dot, has

    Omega_x=M J0, M=beta/W,
    H_x=[[K,Mdot/2],[Mdot/2,M]],
    B_x=[[0,1],[-K/M,Wdot/W]].

Here K is computed from the complete moving pullback; equivalently, with
e=dot(d)+dB, K=-(beta/W)*det([e;d])/W. The symmetric first-order action
is L_x-d(M theta v/2)/dt, where

    L_x=M v theta_dot-M v²/2-K theta²/2.

Variation of v gives v=theta_dot, so

    L_theta=M theta_dot²/2-K theta²/2,
    M theta_ddot+Mdot theta_dot+K theta=0.

No moving off-diagonal term was omitted. Positivity of this scalar
kinetic coefficient is beta*W>0; it is not inferred merely from the
positive canonical Hamiltonian before the observation change.

The measured mechanical spin becomes

    S=s T^-1 x=chi theta+I_spin theta_dot,
    chi=det([s;d])/W, I_spin=det([c;s])/W.              (1)

Canonical momentum is Pi=M theta_dot. Thus S-Pi=(I_spin-M)theta_dot+
chi theta. Its exact Poisson bracket is

    {theta,S}=det([c;s])/beta=I_spin/M.                (2)

Unit rotation generated by the actual S in this scalar chart is the
measured condition det([c;s])=beta, not a consequence of positive energy.
If it holds, chi theta is an explicit momentum connection which can be
accounted for by the corresponding total derivative. Otherwise both
inertias and the physical current remain distinct.

    n.S=I_geo[theta_dot-2(k_perp.Au)theta],

The coefficient of the actual angle rate is strictly positive. The

    kappa*lambda/(U*|k|²).                          (8)
