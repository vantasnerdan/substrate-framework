## 2. Full second variation and exterior contribution

Let F_t be the volume-preserving flow generated by an autonomous compact
xi, and transport the actual vorticity: omega_t=(F_t)_* omega0. Exactly,

    dot omega=curl(xi cross omega0)=curl v,
    ddot omega=curl[xi cross curl(xi cross omega0)],
    v=P(xi cross omega0).

Here P is the whole-space Leray projection; it is not a projection on the
coherence ball. Curl inverse is taken with the decaying Biot-Savart solution.
The complete energy variation is

    Erel''=rho integral |v|²+rho integral u0.ddot u
          =rho integral [|v|²-v.curl v/lambda].

The second equality follows by curl integration by parts and
curl u0=lambda u0:
`integral u0.ddot u=lambda^-1 integral u0.ddot omega
=-lambda^-1 integral (xi cross omega0).curl v`.
Compact support of the transported vorticity change makes these operations
legitimate; its induced velocity decays as r^-3. Polarization gives

    H(xi,zeta)=rho integral [v_xi.v_zeta
                                  -v_xi.curl v_zeta/lambda].

Thus the second-order transported vorticity supplies the indispensable
negative helicity term. Keeping only the positive norm is not this action.
Mixed terms with K follow the 0048 boundary calculation and vanish exactly:
`H(K,K)=H(K,xi)=0`. This preserves common-angle objectivity, including
mixed common/gradient terms. It does not set H(Z_i,Z_j) to zero.

For any ball D containing the compact vorticity changes, curl v_i=0 outside
D. Consequently the EXACT common-gradient matrix is

    H(Z_i,Z_j)=rho integral_D [v_i.v_j-v_i.curl v_j/lambda]
                         +rho integral_{R3\D} v_i.v_j.

The exterior matrix is a positive Gram matrix, finite with an O(L^-3) tail
beyond radius L. Both velocity factors are whole-space induced velocities.
This identity retains the exterior reaction in the full fluid action;
there is no reflecting-wall or zero-pressure-traction approximation.
For a material-domain energy calculation, Euler's local conservation law
gives `d/dt integral_D(t) rho |u|²/2=-integral_boundary p u.n`.
Dropping that pressure work would describe a different subsystem. The
whole-space expression above includes it without introducing a wall model.

All KKS pairings are equally finite:
`Omega(xi,zeta)=rho integral omega0.(xi cross zeta)`.
One K leg is permissible because the other leg is compact. No pairing of
two unrestricted noncompact modulated rotations has been inserted.

## 3. Exact reduced gradient action, including inertia

`E_B=K+i sum k_j Z_j`, `E_q=Q`, and momentum columns `(r,S)`.

    D_eff=K(k)-i nu G(k)-nu² M(k),
    K=H_xx-H_xp P^-1 H_px,
    G=Omega_xx-Omega_xp P^-1 H_px-H_xp P^-1 Omega_px,
    M=-Omega_xp P^-1 Omega_px
      =Omega_xp P^-1 Omega_xp^*.

Other prescribed microscopic families with gradient-dependent momentum
