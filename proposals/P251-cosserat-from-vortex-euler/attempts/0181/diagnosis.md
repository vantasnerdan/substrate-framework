The first run reached seven passing identities, then the zero-frequency
curvature check failed. Substituting the entire function nu(x)=0 inside
an unevaluated derivative and calling doit also sets its derivatives to
zero. That describes an identically zero function, not a transverse zero
at one point. Replace the first and second derivative jets by independent
symbols before substituting only the zeroth jet. The proof identity
(nu²)''=2(nu')²+2nu nu'' and the scientific construction are unchanged.

The proof's already integrated G=-Dg has that correct minus sign.
The corresponding pre-integration spin row is
pi[-omega z(A+B)+i(omega-2Omega)r pP/sigma²]. After integral z exp(ipz)
=-i Z', this agrees with the proof's equation(12). Both verifier
expectations are corrected; no proof formula changes.
