## Independent physical-map derivation

For a transverse helicity, write t=+|K| or -|K|, beta=j/(2rho), and
d=1+beta t²/2. The two-by-two physical map and exact metric are

    T=[[1,-beta t],[t/2,1]],
    T* diag(rho,j) T=d diag(rho,j),
    det T=d>0.

This independently exposes both the cancellation of the kinetic mixed
row and the two retained gradient masses. The cancellation uses the
physical factor one half in BOTH branch observations; it does not hold
for the old unmatched relative-angle map. Both helicities have the same
positive determinant and metric. Longitudinal Phi is unchanged.

Let f_a=a|K|² and f_o=nu²+c_T|K|². Direct multiplication of the exact
inverse, before truncation, gives

    K_UU=rho [f_a+beta t² f_o/2]/d,
    K_U,Phi=rho beta t [f_a-f_o]/d,
    K_Phi,Phi=j [f_o+beta t² f_a/2]/d.

The other mixed row is its Hermitian conjugate; rho beta=j/2 ensures
equality in this scalar helicity representation. Through degree two,
these are

    K_UU=(rho a+j nu²/4)|K|²,
    K_U,Phi=-(j nu²/2)t,
    K_Phi,Phi=j nu²+[j c_T-j² nu²/(4rho)]|K|².

Thus mu=rho a, alpha=j nu²/4,
gamma_T=j(c_T-alpha/rho), gamma_L=j c_L. This agrees with the actual
canonical `micropolar_fourier_stiffness` convention, including the sign
of i[K cross], the sum in longitudinal spin curvature, and both
Hermitian coupling blocks. It is not a derivation of alpha from tube
tension. Positive transverse bulk curvature requires c_T>alpha/rho,
not merely c_T>0. The raw theorem states that requirement explicitly.

The exact inverse contains d in its denominator. Consequently the
second-spatial-jet operator is not substituted for the exact higher-K
pencil. Omitting either required branch gradient mass would change the
physical metric at the very retained order. The source's mutation and
exact-pencil predicates expose both errors.

The boundary-divergence statement is also correct. Varying the three
local curvature constants by (delta c_s,delta c_a,delta c_tr)=(e,-e,-e)
keeps gamma_T and gamma_L fixed, and changes the energy by
e[tr((grad Phi)²)-(div Phi)²], a total divergence. Therefore the new
bulk theorem has not invented a microscopic boundary couple traction.
The requested local boundary row remains an additional supplier datum
if that stronger local constitutive statement is used downstream.

    h^-D |K|³ / |K|² = h,

fixed source derivative list determines the h exponents; raising its

small because an observed current is small.

arbitrary fixed source as K tends to zero, nor an acoustic-time bound
