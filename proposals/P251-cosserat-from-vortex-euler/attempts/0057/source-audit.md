# Primary source receipt

Enciso, Peralta-Salas and Romaniega, *Beltrami fields exhibit knots and
chaos almost surely*, arXiv:2006.15033v1 (2020),
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15033>. The downloaded PDF and its text extraction
are preserved alongside this receipt.

Checked source results: Proposition 3.2 gives almost-sure smoothness;
Proposition 3.4 supplies a translation-invariant spherical spectral
covariance; Corollary 3.5 normalizes the point covariance to the identity.
Proposition 3.7 establishes translation invariance and ergodic volume
averages. Proposition 3.8 gives positive probability to every local C^k
neighborhood of a Beltrami field. Theorem 1.2 gives positive-volume-density
knotted invariant tori. Section 4 constructs robust invariant structures.

The paper's polynomial spectral amplitude is not presumed rotation
invariant. This attempt explicitly mixes its law over Haar rotations and
reflection/time-reversal partners. Translation ergodicity of that mixture
is not claimed or required for an ensemble action. Nor does the source
derive a micropolar action, a shear modulus, a common-rotor mass, or our
selected affine attachment: those are separate constructions below.
