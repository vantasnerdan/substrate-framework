# Exact material centering, boundary-spin bridge, and its constructive repair

## 1. One joint Euler action before any spin reduction

Let a material partition have reference cells D_a0. Write its full material
maps as `g_a(t,label)=X_a(t)+h_a(t,label)`, with

    M_a=rho |D_a0|,
    integral_Da0 rho h_a=0.

X_a is the MASS CENTROID, not a convenient vortex center. Differentiating
the mean constraint removes the kinetic cross term exactly:

    integral_Da0 rho |gdot_a|²/2
      =M_a |Xdot_a|²/2+integral_Da0 rho |hdot_a|²/2.

Incompressibility depends on grad h_a. On a shared material face, retain
`X_a+h_a=X_b+h_b`; its Lagrange multiplier is determined by the Euler
pressure traction, not by a supplied spring. A phase-space form of the SAME
material Euler action is

    S=integral dt sum_a [P_a.Xdot_a
       +integral_Da0 pi_a.hdot_a
       -|P_a|²/(2M_a)-integral_Da0 |pi_a|²/(2rho)
       + pressure*(det grad h_a-1)
       + centroid and shared-face constraints].

Here integral pi_a=0, P_a=M_a Xdot_a, and the internal velocity momentum is
pi_a=rho hdot_a. The shared-face constraints remain when one varies X_a;
they supply `M_a Xddot_a=-integral_boundary p n`. Rotating h_a gives
`Ldot_a=-integral_boundary r cross p n`, with
`L_a=integral h_a cross pi_a`. These are precisely the physical variables
and pressure reactions in 0051. There is no new mass coefficient in this
action: its translational density is rho before any constitutive reduction.

This identity licenses extracting the centroid sector from the Euler action.
It does NOT license adding the same mass sector a second time to an orbit
Hamiltonian which already contains its cell-mean kinetic energy.

## 2. The stationary EPS cell and the explicit mean-energy correction

Choose a closed invariant material EPS solid torus D. Because u0.n=0,

    integral_D u0_i=integral_boundary x_i u0.n=0.

Its base centroid velocity is zero exactly. Put the common-rotation center
at `X_D=|D|^-1 integral_D x`, and K=e cross (x-X_D). Choose an axis with
nonzero physical rotation tangent vK; the finite core-jet observations in
0048 are unchanged by moving the common rotation center. A nonzero Beltrami
field cannot be invariant under all rotations about X_D, so such an axis
exists. Its rotation of the entire domain preserves its zero mean.

For a geometric orbit variation generated by xi, the COMPLETE first mean
variation includes the moving-domain term:

    C_D(xi)=delta V_D
      =|D|^-1 [integral_D v_xi
                +integral_boundary u0 (xi.n)],
    v_xi=P(xi cross omega0).

For compact xi inside D the boundary term is zero. For global K it is
essential: together the two terms give C_D(K)=0, by rotating both u0 and D.
Using only the fixed-domain integral of vK would create a spurious mean.

Since V_D0=0, the exact centroid kinetic Hessian is the rank-at-most-three
Gram matrix

    H_centroid(xi,eta)=M_D C_D(xi).C_D(eta).

Consequently the internal ENERGY Hessian obtained by removing this sector
is `H_shape=H_orbit-H_centroid`. There is no linear base-velocity term.
For compact tangents,

    M_D |C_D(xi)|² <= rho integral_D |v_xi|².

The 0048 cage velocities have uniformly bounded L² norm; their positive
orbit Hessian grows linearly in the finite carrier magnitude. Thus this
explicit mean-energy subtraction is bounded and preserves positivity at a
larger finite carrier. Also H_centroid(K,xi)=0, so the common symmetry row
is not spoiled by correct moving-domain centering.

An energy subtraction alone is not a symplectic reduction: if C_D(xi) is
nonzero, the selected orbit direction also changes centroid momentum. The
corresponding centroid/shape connection must then be retained in the first
order action. Leaving the old KKS form untouched after only subtracting this
Gram is not established. The next construction removes that ambiguity by
projecting the actual phase tangents into zero mean momentum.

## 3. Executed fixed-response projection of the physical mean

For each Cartesian i define the actual R³ Leray response

    w_i=P(1_D e_i),  f_i=omega0 cross w_i.

For every smooth compact divergence-free xi inside D,

    |D| C_D,i(xi)=integral_D v_xi,i
                 =integral xi.f_i=:c_i(xi).

The first equality is physical cell momentum; the second uses the same
ambient projection, not an unrelated inverse on D. Inside D, w_i is a smooth
harmonic curl-free field, since the distributional sources of 1_D are on
the boundary. Therefore f_i and curl f_i are analytic in the interior.

Choose a smooth nonnegative cutoff chi on a connected open region in D away
from the two physical core jets. Define fixed compact response generators

    eta_j=curl(chi curl f_j),
    W_ij=c_i(eta_j)=integral chi curl f_i.curl f_j.

For the projection use the exact class of this construction:
`xi=curl A` with A smooth and compactly supported inside D. Every core,
cage, fixed response, and momentum generator used here has this form.
W is positive semidefinite. Its nullspace is exactly the nullspace of the
three mean functionals on this curl-generated class: if a linear
combination of curl f_i vanishes on the support of chi, analyticity makes
it vanish throughout connected D; integration against compact A then gives
`integral xi.f=integral A.curl f=0`. This justifies the pseudoinverse on the
actual response range; it does not presume an arbitrary rank-three inverse.
On a solid torus, a curl-free field can have a nonzero period. Therefore
curl-free alone does not justify the stronger statement for every compact
solenoidal generator with nontrivial toroidal flux. Such additional flux
directions would require their own finite cohomology responses; they are
not used by the present construction. This topological precision was
identified by `/root/orientation_construction` while reusing the projection.

The explicit correction

    xi_centered=xi-sum_j eta_j (W^+ c(xi))_j

therefore satisfies every c_i=0 EXACTLY. It preserves both core jets because

At the stationary cell, a compact divergence-free displacement has zero

### The common-momentum independence test

Let `l(xi)=Omega(K,xi)`, and choose its fixed Riesz representative
`f_0=-rho vK` for the compact curl-generated class. The same cutoff defines

    G_00=integral chi |curl f_0|²,
    G_0i=integral chi curl f_0.curl f_i,
    Delta_common=G_00-G_0i (W^+)_ij G_j0 >=0.

This is the squared norm of the common moment functional after projecting
off cell-mean momentum. If Delta_common>0, its residual fixed response is
an explicit centered common-momentum partner, and the 0048 high-frequency
construction can be rerun in ker c with no supplied inertia. Positivity is
not inferred merely from l being nonzero: Delta_common is zero precisely
when l lies in the span of the mean functionals on this cell.

Thus the projection has converted a vague centering objection into one

for every EPS tube and chosen axis. Recomputing the centered internal KKS

## 4. Exact canonical-to-physical spin and momentum map

For the centered material cell define the bulk impulse and surface spin

    J_D=-rho/2 integral_D r² omega,
    B_D=rho/2 integral_boundary r² (n cross u).

The exact vector identity `curl(r²u)=2r cross u+r²omega` gives
`L_D=J_D+B_D`. For compact vorticity rearrangements contained in D, the
0048 moment about X_D equals the corresponding variation of J_D. Its pure
common-rotation component about that same axis is invariant when both field
and material domain are rotated. Thus the common canonical moment is the
bulk impulse, not the material spin with its generally nonzero B_D omitted.

Let the coarse boundary-spin distribution be
`B(x)=sum_a B_Da delta(x-X_a)` (or its smooth convolution), and put

    A_ij=(1/2) epsilon_ijk B_k,
    ax(A)=B,
    P_can=P_centroid+div A=P_centroid+(curl B)/2,
    S_can=S_physical-B.

This is an exact, explicitly stated current improvement. In the linear
balance, set `sigma_can=sigma_physical+partial_t A`, `mu_can=mu_physical`.
Then 0051 gives identically

    partial_t P_can=div sigma_can,
    partial_t S_can=div mu_can-ax(sigma_can).

For the full moving-center distributions use total fluxes
`F=sigma-T`, `G=mu-C`; T is symmetric. The same calculation is
`F_can=F+partial_t A`, `G_can=G`. If one chooses new convective fluxes for
P_can, their differences are put back into sigma_can and mu_can; they are
not deleted. Total angular momentum transforms as

    x cross P_can+S_can
      =x cross P_centroid+S_physical+div(x cross A).

Thus the missing surface spin has been transferred to an orbital current
improvement with an explicit boundary flux. Mass continuity is unchanged,
since `div(P_can-P_centroid)=div curl B/2=0`. But P_can is NOT in general
rho times the material-centroid velocity. The physical centroid momentum is
recovered by the displayed inverse map. A canonical displacement field used
in a local normal form must carry that observable filter; it is not silently
renamed as the centroid field. This is the bridge required by 0053.

For the actual time-reversal-paired orbit, the surface coefficients are also
computable without a new modulus. Let v_r,v_s be its two momentum tangents,
and define the row

    t_j=(rho/2) integral_boundary r² e.(n cross v_j),  j=r,s.

With P the complete momentum Hessian and D the KKS diagonal of 0048, the

    B_surface,axis = t P^-1 D (Bdot,qdot)^T.

The static terms odd in circulation cancel, while this rate response

averaging. Spatial parity cannot be invoked to discard this axial boundary

canonical momentum, to be retained in the full kinetic normalization.
