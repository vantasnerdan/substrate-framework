# Leading translation tensors: physical mean, phase, and actual energy

Use a stationary mean-zero Euler field u. Its exact constrained material
phase is0154's eta, pi=rho(eta_t+P A eta), with A=u.grad_K. Lin's
physical Euler velocity is w=eta_t+Aeta-(Du)eta. Thus

    pi=rho P[w+(Du)eta].                               (1)

This is the actual cotangent; it is not the Eulerian velocity by itself.

## 1. Full Lin and honest coadjoint preparations are different

At K=0, fixing eta_D(0)=D while choosing the stationary physical velocity
T_(MD)=-(Du)MD gives

    eta_D,t(0)=(Du)(I-M)D,
    pi_D(0)=rho(Du)(I-M)D.                             (2)

Its microscopic mean is zero. With eta_V(0)=0, pi_V(0)=rho V, the
common initial phase form is indeed rho J. At finite K, the actual
solenoidal lift curl_K(T_(MD)/lambda) and(1) still give mean-zero pi_D,
so the same pairing persists. This preparation explicitly changes the
initial circulation class. It is not the Kelvin leaf generated by D.

There is nevertheless an ACTUAL zero-wave energy cost:

    H_DD(0)=rho ||(Du)(I-M)D||²/2.                     (3)

For constant D, A D=0 at K=0 and <D.Hess(p)D>=0, so no discarded
pressure term cancels(3). An observed free mean chart can hide that
internal energy in its moving embedding, but it does not make the energy
of a rigid physical macro translation zero. Thus phase rho J alone does
not establish the requested energy-compatible positive action.

The honest coadjoint constant generator MD has eta_D=MD and pi_D=0
at K=0, with zero translation energy. Its measured mean is MD and its
pairing with common V is rho(MD).V, not rho D.V. These facts must be
kept when interpreting a proposed tensor preparation.

## 2. Bloch transversality and whole-law observation

For one common laboratory K=k kappa, the actual constant Bloch amplitude
must be transverse. Consequently use

    E=P_kappa M D, P_kappa=I-kappa tensor kappa,
    eta_D(0)=E, w_D(0)=T_E,

not an inadmissible nontransverse MD. The body tensor rotates with the
ENTIRE field. If tr M=3, the whole-law mean of E is D on the physical
transverse bundle. Its pairing with the common V remains rho J after
averaging because E[M]=I. This is a declared correlated initial-displacement
law: individual realizations have mean E, not the same local mean D.
The common laboratory INPUT and the physical reconstructed mean remain D.

Preparing velocity by the same random tensor would instead give mass
rho E[N^T N], with N the physical projected velocity map. If E[N]V=V,
the excess is the nonnegative actual variance E[|(N-I)V|²]. Matching
the full kinetic energy rho|V|²/2 forces N V=V almost surely. A random
local boost cannot evade the velocity-column requirement by renaming
its larger canonical mass as the fluid density.

More generally eta_V(0)=0 and w_V(0)=V+z_V with mean z_V=0 have energy
rho(|V|²+||z_V||²)/2 at K=0. Keeping the physical leading kinetic
energy fixes z_V=0. A nonzero initial CONFIGURATION return could change
this conclusion through the actual Jacobi potential, but would be a new
physical phase/observation construction rather than a harmless velocity
correction. It remains an explicit alternative in the registered route.

## 3. The displacement-only tensor cancellation is a typed interface

For the unequal two-wave field used below and diagonal M, the actual
forcing coefficient is d_E=kappa_Y E_Y-kappa_Z E_Z. The complete
current0184 changes only through the d-weighted planar component. Its
whole-law forcing weight, including the transverse projection, is

    sigma_M=E[d d_E]=[4M_XX+19(M_YY+M_ZZ)]/210.         (4)

The sixth sphere moments in(4) arise from P_kappa; replacing E by MD
would wrongly give (M_YY+M_ZZ)/10. With tr M=3, setting sigma_M=0
requires M_XX=19/5 and M_YY+M_ZZ=-4/5. The static q_E current is

    R_D,static=sum_(i=Y,Z) s_i[7M_ii+tr M]/30,
    s_Y=B²/2, s_Z=A²/2.                                (5)

The mixed axial forcing contraction is a psi row, hence is conserved.
Unequal amplitudes can make(5) restoring. Equations(4),(5) concern the
D column only. They do not give a common-V partner, the finite-gradient
energy equality or the physical angle/spin closure. Those remain actual
equations to solve, not implications of a signed tensor coefficient.
