# Nonlinear axial first-integral return: actual field, energy and scope

The registered repair uses r=-d alpha/2+d[zeta psi+tau psi³], leaving
the particular cubic planar corrector and its eta,xi first-shell controls.
The added velocity (tau psi³,0,0) is an EXACT stationary linearized Euler
solution: the background is X independent and Tpsi=0. Its pressure
projection has no omitted component. For arbitrary whole-law (kappa,D),
E[d c_X]=0 pointwise, so its whole physical mean-current contribution
vanishes for any axial profile, not just for a fitted integral.

For A=1,B=2 the directly assembled complete energy coefficient is

    720 h2 = 720eta²+1620eta tau+864eta xi+432eta zeta-720eta
              +11025tau²+3564tau xi+3564tau zeta+2565tau
              +720xi²+720xi zeta-432xi+360zeta²-432zeta+2515.

The actual physical restoring row remains
a=eta/2+3xi/10-5/6. It was separately checked against

    R_D(0)=E[<(kappa.u)²>|D|²+<c,chi_t(0)>],
    c=(kappa.u)D+(D.u)kappa, chi_t=q_D-(kappa.u)D+z.

Thus energy and observation were obtained by independent contractions of
the full Euler/Lin field, not by reusing a target frequency in the energy.
At fixed actual a, minimization over the remaining three field controls
gives

    min(h2-a)=(439104a²+607712a+302987)/154272>0

for every a>=0. The unconstrained minimum is395869/658656>0 as well.
The positive quadratic is the precise mismatch of this specified family.

The comparison h2=a is the energy match for a pure restoring acoustic
column without a separately excited internal optical energy. It is not a
requirement that every coupled Cosserat mean evolve autonomously. A physical
optical preparation could instead carry part of the energy and produce
its actual coupled stress history; that would require the actual angle,
spin and current rows rather than reclassifying this mismatch as invisible.
