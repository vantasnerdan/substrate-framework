DECISION SUMMARY
1. Native ordinary elphel really exports complex, all-saved-band matrix
   elements of the STATIC generalized perturbation
       D_ji = <psi_j | dH_SCF/dR - epsilon_i partial S/partial R | psi_i>,
   with INITIAL electronic energy, not the final or averaged energy.
   This is the generalized static eigenstate-response numerator in Dal
   Corso Eq.(23), not a demonstrated laboratory-frame off-shell Hamiltonian.
2. Do not conflate that screened numerator with the physical static total
   force. Dal Corso Eq.(14) uses a PARTIAL fixed-orbital derivative, occupation-
   weighted DIAGONAL elements, an explicit NLCC contribution, and the force
   is minus the energy derivative. Eq.(20) distinguishes the TOTAL screened
   derivative used for response from that PARTIAL derivative.
3. Overlap and moving augmentation terms are real, already included in
   native static DFPT. They must neither be dropped nor added a second time.
   But neither inspected paper supplies a complete dynamical USPP embedding
   prescription that turns arbitrary off-shell D into the requested physical
   force. Choosing a symmetric energy convention by Hermitizing D is not a
   source-established physical license.
4. No new exporter is needed just to obtain existing D. A separately typed,
   gauge-matched finite-q dS export is needed IF the chosen downstream route
   separates the generalized operator/metric terms; dS alone has not been
   shown sufficient here for a laboratory-frame force. An embedding-based
   route additionally needs its actual map/derivative or corresponding
   connection, plus its physical dynamical authority. Do not invent them.
5. Giustino distinguishes an approximate screened-vertex golden-rule model
   from a phonon self-energy with a bare–screened vertex structure and a
   static subtraction. These are not interchangeable just because both are
   often called electron–phonon coupling. Native double-delta linewidths,
   and even finite-energy gamma_tetra mode summaries, are not a full bath.
6. Actual QE Ni is GBRV Ni18/H1, NOT Ni10. GPAW pilot Ni10 and installed
   default Ni16 are different models; no identification or scalar rescaling
   between these spaces is licensed.

2. WHAT THE ORIGINAL ESTABLISHES: STATIC FORCE VS RESPONSE VERTEX

[PAPER] D Sec.II pp.2–4, Eqs.(1)–(15), constructs a variational USPP
functional with smearing, spin-resolved augmented density, NLCC, generalized
orthonormality and generalized KS eigenproblem. D p.3 states that its
entropy-like term is a genuine entropy/free-energy term for Fermi–Dirac
occupations; its more general smearing formalism is for efficient metallic
BZ integration. Generic smearing width is not asserted to be temperature.
D p.3 explicitly discusses ion-ion U_II separately; its subsequent special
USPP analysis concentrates on electronic force-constant contributions.

[PAPER] At the electronic ground state D Sec.II Eq.(14), p.4, is a static
ENERGY DERIVATIVE. Its sign is opposite the force. It is an occupation-
weighted diagonal expression, plus NLCC. Crucially its partial derivative
is at fixed orbitals (definition following Eq.(15)); it is not the total
self-consistent derivative in Eq.(20). The augmentation density kernel K
and overlap S are distinct objects: Eqs.(5),(8), p.3. Omitting moving K,
projectors or overlap terms would change this static theory.

[CODE] Corresponding ground-state routines are separate from elphel:

PW/src/force_us.f90:60–88 uses displaced beta projectors; :124–131 contracts
same-band weighted products with deeq-et(ibnd,ik)*qq_at; :161–183 applies
force sign/factors and adds addusforce. PW/src/addusforce.f90:32–40 explicitly
identifies moving-Q forces, :84–100 uses the total effective potential,
:123–174 assembles Q and phase/gradient factors. PW/src/force_cc.f90:10–12
is the separate NLCC contribution. Native total force is therefore not
obtained merely by naming an arbitrary all-band elphel matrix “force.”
This is source inspection, not a computed force check.

[PAPER] D Sec.III Eqs.(19)–(23), pp.4–5, differentiates the STATIC generalized
KS eigenproblem. The TOTAL derivative includes induced Hxc via Eqs.(20),(21).
Eq.(22) gives the eigenvalue derivative. Eq.(23) specifies off-diagonal
wavefunction derivatives through the initial-energy generalized numerator,
assuming a nondegenerate epsilon_i at this step; the paper says the
degenerate generalization is straightforward, but supplies no campaign
implementation/gauge contract for it. That numerator is directly relevant
to native D. It is not a statement that arbitrary off-shell matrix entries
are matrix elements of one energy-independent physical force operator.

[PAPER] D Appendix B p.14 fixes plane-wave/Fourier normalizations B1–B10;
p.15 B18/B19 gives orthonormalization coefficients, B20/B21 fixed-response
augmentation integrals and B22 induced-Hxc integral. B25 has RHS

following B24 says only 3I depends on self-consistent Hxc change. B31 supplies
augmented induced density; B32–B37 supply several force-constant terms,
including the special augmentation and orthonormalization terms. It does
not replace all static force constants by an unqualified |D|^2 bubble.

[INFERENCE] The original licenses static generalized response and static
forces/force constants under its assumptions. It does NOT by these formulas
license an energy-origin change applied only to eigenvalues, arbitrary
Euclidean renormalization of pseudo-orbitals, an off-shell “repair” by
Hermitization, a unique laboratory embedding, finite-energy friction, a
positive physical bath, or time-dependent USPP electron-ion propagation.
Algebraic reciprocity, energy-shift covariance, orthonormalization choices
and embedding sufficiency are questions for the separate oracle, not new
results of this source slice.

5. SCREENING AND STATIC SUBTRACTION: WHICH LOSS MODEL?

[PAPER G] Sec.III.B.2 p.10, Eqs.(30)–(38), constructs an EFFECTIVE KS
one-body electron–phonon Hamiltonian; it explicitly notes the sharp-
quasiparticle approximation. Sec.III.B.4 p.13, Eqs.(50)–(54), describes
DFPT vertices as the statically RPA+xc screened ionic perturbation. The
following paragraph explicitly identifies quasi-static/adiabatic screening,
not a fully frequency-dependent screened interaction.

[PAPER G] Sec.V.A.1 pp.22–23, Eqs.(123)–(128), partitions static Pi^A=Pi(0)
into the reference phonons and Pi^NA into corrections. Sec.V.A.2 pp.24–25,
Eqs.(135)–(139), retains frequency-dependent/off-diagonal self-energy and
relates pole shifts/broadening to it under stated approximations.
Sec.V.A.3 pp.25–27, Eqs.(140)–(145), is especially important:
- Eq.(140) explicitly uses W_e(omega)-W_e(0).
- Eq.(141) has a bare coupling, an electronic proper polarization, a
  dynamically screened coupling, and a static subtraction.
- Eqs.(143),(144) distinguish g from g^cc; the text equates g^cc with g*
  at zero frequency, not at arbitrary finite frequency.
- Eq.(145) is a practical approximation with vertex Gamma set to deltas,
  KS G at equilibrium, RPA+xc dielectric response, frozen frequency
  dependence of the screened vertex, and spin-degenerate/time-reversal
  simplification. It uses bare g^b times screened g*, NOT |g|^2, and a
  zero-frequency subtraction. The source says sums run over ALL KS states.
- Eq.(146) is the familiar finite-energy golden-rule rate after replacing
  g^b by g; p.27 explicitly calls the matrix-element choice somewhat
  arbitrary and requests assessing the correct bare–screened structure.
  These are the review's stated distinctions, not a new campaign theorem.

[INFERENCE] Two different viable but conditional routes therefore exist:
(1) An explicitly declared effective independent-electron KS-type model
    with a physically justified force/vertex, physical occupations, full
    finite-energy transition coverage and the screened-vertex approximation
    may use the Eq.(146) style golden-rule description within that model.
    It is not thereby the exact material phonon self-energy.
(2) A response/self-energy route following Eqs.(140)–(145) must maintain
    its bare–screened structure or state/validate the replacement, decide
    static versus dynamical screening, and preserve the static subtraction
    when using already statically screened DFPT phonons as reference.
    A |screened vertex|^2 positive-bath construction must not be identified
    with that mixed-vertex self-energy without additional analysis.
These routes still require the USPP off-shell physical convention in Sec.4.
No route is accepted for production by this audit.

[INFERENCE] Adding a full electronic static response to DFPT force constants
without matching the Pi^A/Pi^NA partition risks counting already-included
screening again. Conversely, simply deleting screening from an existing
vertex does not construct a consistent alternative model. Define reference
phonons/electrons, retained interactions and the subtraction together. A
finite-energy delta does not convert a static vertex to dynamical screening;
an arbitrary numerical eta is not a measured or computed bath lifetime.

7. NUMERICAL SMEARING IS NOT A PHYSICAL OCCUPATION CONTRACT

[PAPER] D Sec.II p.3 distinguishes a generic variational smearing functional
from the Fermi–Dirac free-energy case. G V.A.3 p.27 explicitly uses FD for
finite temperature, not Marzari–Vanderbilt cold smearing.

[CODE] Modules/wgauss.f90:15–21 defines MP, cold/MV (-1), and FD (-99);
:48–55 implements FD; :60–64 implements cold smearing; :68–87 plain Gaussian
and MP corrections. Modules/w0gauss.f90:12–17 identifies the derivative;
:43–49 implements the nonnegative FD derivative, while :54–57 explicitly
contains cold-smearing factor (2-sqrt(2)*x). The cold kernel is not a globally
positive delta approximation, and its occupation approximation is not a
physical FD distribution. This is a source/formula observation, not a new
computed counterexample, spectral-sign theorem or material failure result.
Any stronger mathematical passivity statement remains for the oracle.

[INFERENCE] A physical equilibrium independent-electron loss model needs a
specified monotone occupation prescription (e.g. FD with explicit mu,T),
not literal use of numerical MV occupations as thermal populations. Plain
Gaussian monotonicity likewise does not assign its width a temperature.
The static state/vertex was screened under its original occupation scheme;
reweighting a later transition sum with FD does not retroactively change
that response. Recompute consistent response or quantify/justify the
smearing-to-physical-occupation approximation in production. This is NOT a
finding that the frozen MV ground-state integration is invalid.
