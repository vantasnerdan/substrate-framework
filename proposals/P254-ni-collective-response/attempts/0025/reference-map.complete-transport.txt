## 8. Mathematical reconstruction versus physical ensemble/calibration

H0 reconstructed at the saved density is a well-defined held-reference operator. Its Hartree/PBE derivative can be reconstructed at that same density without assigning quantum probabilities to saved occupations.

A physical FD state is a **separate preparation choice**. It introduces chemical potential, physical temperature and ensemble constraints; if its density differs, it is not automatically the saved self-consistent reference.

Therefore:

- Retain MV occupations as raw metadata.
- Do not clip them into probabilities.
- Do not convert MV width to temperature.
- Do not invoke attempt0019’s common-mu full-spectrum FD construction merely to define H0.
- Do not require a new FD density to reproduce saved MV density before admitting a correct held-density operator.
- Conversely, do not claim that combining a newly chosen FD state with saved MV density/K/frozen curvature has established an equal-reference stationary material action.

Later physical preparation and calibration remain separate obligations. They are not blockers to this operator component.
