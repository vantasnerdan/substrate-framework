## Architecture and constructive next consumer contract

1. STATE CONTRACT. Provide each physical spin/k sector's original PW basis, orthonormal saved columns and caller-supplied raw probabilities in [0,1], plus explicit preparation/constraint authority. The existing rho0 definition is (orbitals * occupations) @ orbitals.conj().T. [INFERENCE] A rectangular saved-column implementation may declare the orthogonal finite-PW complement exactly empty as part of an auxiliary finite-rank quasi-free state. This is a valid preparation family, not evidence that the actual material complement has zero thermal population. Keep all declared nonzero source factors; do not choose occupations, an electron count split, a cutoff or temperature in this map. If the state is only parameterized, publish the contraction functional/operator factors, not fabricated fluctuation numbers.

2. SCALABLE INITIAL CONTRACTION. The reviewed definition is N*Tr(rho A (I-rho) B) per channel (physical_bilinear_preparation.py:98), with probe weights handled separately. [INFERENCE] Implement that same trace through occupied-factor contractions and full-PW actions: act B on source columns; apply I-rho to the resulting full-PW vectors using the saved-factor rho action; pair with A-adjoint applied to source columns and the source probabilities. For non-Hermitian probes this requires a genuine adjoint action. Stream bands/probes and retain only needed small overlaps/full-PW vector blocks. No d-by-d rho, d^2 basis of d-by-d matrices, or d^4 covariance is necessary for this initial selected trace. This is an implementation route requiring the new bridge oracle below, not a new physical identity or measured complexity result.

The identity in I-rho acts on the FULL finite-PW space, not only the saved bands. Thus empty target states remain available without empty-state eigenvectors. Replacing it by a saved-band identity or by I-Psi*Psi-adjoint would respectively lose the complement or erase fractional population/degenerate noise. In particular, neither dividing by f_i-f_j nor dropping zero-energy sectors is allowed. The reviewed f_i(1-f_j) contraction includes diagonal populations and all coherences within degenerate blocks. Saved Psi-adjoint*g*Psi alone is insufficient; consume the full gPsi outputs. Dynamic complement propagation and nonzero complement occupations are separate later requirements.

3. PROBE CONTRACT. Record the exact atom/direction or spatial sampling function, spin combination, sign, units, q and adjoint partner, PW source/target maps and normalization. For the present outputs the electronic force probe is -g(u), not +g(u); the latter is the positive ionic gradient. Current probes are the full111-coordinate H32-x and Ni1-x directions, not normal modes or a two-atom material. Density probes need explicit real-space weighting and number-density versus charge-density meaning. GammaIonicAction.local_action gives the existing FFT multiplication mechanism, but a physically normalized density-probe wrapper is still new work. Preserve the physical-grid/volume pairing in spin_pbe_kernel.py:106-108 and the explicit spin sum; no implicit factor two or guessed cell-volume factor. 4. OUTPUT CONTRACT. Expose state identity, operator identities, sector contributions, means, connected ordered AB, the separately requested BA, symmetrized part and expected commutator. AB does not conjugate B: for AB-adjoint pass the adjoint probe explicitly, exactly as the reviewed API specifies. Electronic, ionic and cross parts are distinct; absent ionic/cross authority stays null and total stays null with PARTIAL contributions. An initially electronic-only observable can acquire ionic coefficients under later pullback. Do not label a scalar electronic contribution TOTAL quantum force noise.

5. NORMALIZATION CONTRACT. UniformModeIndex permits complete uniform grids only: each spin contains each k once with weight 1/N. Its Gamma collective coordinates are sqrt(N) times centered CAR bilinears, and normalized collective ion u,p obey [u,p]=i. A finite-system Gamma illustration has N=1; the selected Gamma contribution to a chosen complete4^3 representation must carry that representation's weight. Do not pass two selected Gamma channels as a supposedly complete (4,4,4) UniformModeIndex or rename ten IBZ representatives ten regions.
