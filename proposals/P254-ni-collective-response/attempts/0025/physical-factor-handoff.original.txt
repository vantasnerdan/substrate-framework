### Integration APIs

- `ActionFactors(action, adjoint_action, operator_identity, adjoint_identity, units, pw_basis_identity, source_authority)`
- `FactorFunctional.from_actions(psi, probes, *, factor_identity, pw_basis_identity)`
  - Preserves raw factors; produces `gram`, `overlaps`, and directed `ordered_products`.
  - Computes \(S\), \(M_A\), and full-PW \(T_{AB}\), without PW-square density/operators.
  - Explicitly identical Hermitian action/adjoint arrays permit exact reverse cross-Gram reuse; no averaging or Hermitization.
- `functional.archive_payload() -> (arrays, metadata)`
  - Exports all Gram/M/T arrays with indexed keys and explicit `array_layout`.
  - Metadata retains operator identities/provenance; `state=None`, `density_coefficients=None`.
- `FactorPreparation(functional, density_coefficients, state_authority)`
  - Exposes `mean`, `ordered`, and `correlations`.
  - Checks Hermitian coefficients and the spectrum of \(S^{1/2}DS^{1/2}\); reports raw eigenvalues and roundoff allowance.
- `coefficients_from_orthonormal_populations(gram, populations, *, mapping_authority)`
  - Explicit optional symmetric-frame mapping \(D=S^{-1/2}\operatorname{diag}(f)S^{-1/2}\).
  - **Not for production absent separate preparation authority.**
- `NumberProbeGrid(fft_shape, pw_miller, cell_bohr, reciprocal_bohr_inv, pw_basis_identity)`
  - `.actions(psi)` and `.metadata()`.
- `gamma_probe_actions(psi, *, spin_one_based, pw_basis_identity, source_authority, gpsi_h32_x, gpsi_ni1_x, number_grid)`
  - Returns `force_H32_x`, `force_Ni1_x`, `number_cos_b1`, `number_sin_b1`.
  - Consumes archived **positive** gPsi; applies the force minus sign without recomputing ionic action.
- `CompleteGridNormalization(shape, authority)`
- `gamma_correlations(preparations, left, right, normalization, *, independence_authority)`
  - Keeps raw spin sectors separate; applies explicit \(1/N\) aggregate normalization.
  - Separates AB, BA, symmetrized, commutator and means. Ionic/cross/total remain null.
