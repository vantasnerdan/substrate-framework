## 3. Spin-PBE coefficient generation: the missing implementation

### Exact Native APIs

`Q/XClib/xc_lib.f90:60-140` exposes:

- `xc`: local XC, with the declared density/spin representation;
- `xc_gcx`: GGA energy and first derivative components;
- `dmxc`: local spin-XC derivative tensor;
- `dgcxc`: GGA derivative tensors.

The important layout distinction is explicit:

- `xc_gcx` gradient input: Cartesian component, point, spin;
- `dgcxc` gradient input: point, Cartesian component, spin.

`Q/LR_Modules/setup_dgc.f90:142-147` explicitly transposes between these arrangements.

### Selected functional and backend

`Q/XClib/qe_dft_list.f90:96-98` selects internal PBE as Slater + PW local terms and PBX + PBC gradient corrections.

- `qe_drivers_lda_lsda.f90:270-355,422-424`: local spin-XC, Hartree units.
- `qe_funct_exch_gga.f90:111-114`: PBEX is **without Slater exchange**.
- `qe_funct_corr_gga.f90:446-474`: spin-PBE correlation is **without its LDA part**.

Consequently, a full-PBE library output must not be added on top of those local terms without accounting for its definition.

### Native kernel recipe

| Source | Load-bearing behavior |
|---|---|
| `Q/LR_Modules/setup_dmuxc.f90:37-49` | Converts total/magnetization plus any core density to up/down before `dmxc`. |

The literal source constants are implementation conventions, **not proposed material-convergence gates**.
