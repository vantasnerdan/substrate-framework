SCIENTIFIC/API CONCLUSIONS
1. Complete coordinates and null sectors: physical_bilinear_coordinates.py:18-34,77-121 retains all d*d Hermitian matrices, including every population and both coherence quadratures. pack uses Tr(T_a delta_rho), and unpack uses sum_a T_a X_a. The expected commutator is N*Tr(rho[T_a,T_b]), not an inverse of that form and not divided by i. Thus equal occupations and degeneracies can have zero expected response but nonzero ordered variance; they are not discarded.
2. Finite volume and spin: UniformModeIndex.validate at coordinates:52-68 requires the entire integer BvK grid once per explicit spin and weight exactly 1/N. With X=sqrt(N)*(dGamma(T)-mean), independent CAR sectors give <X_a X_b>=N*Tr(rho T_a(1-rho)T_b). Physical density/force probes include 1/N, so the physical ordered covariance is sum_(k,spin) (1/N)*Tr(rho A(1-rho)B), not a squared-weight formula. Independent spin labels are summed once, with no implicit factor two. Ionic Gamma u,p are sqrt(N) times uniform cell variables; this matches Giustino's normalized collective-coordinate convention.
3. Signs, units, and trace dual: physical_observable_transfer.py:31-93 uses the bilinear trace pairing, not an elementwise dot or a Hilbert-Schmidt adjoint pairing. Cyclic trace independently gives A_dot=i[H,A]-w*sum_a p_probe[a]*g_a+w*sum_s density_s*(K.T*eta)_s, with eta_s=sum_c -i*Tr(A_c[density_s,rho0_c]); u_probe_dot=-Cfrozen.T*p_probe+sum_c -i*Tr(A_c[g,rho0_c]); p_probe_dot=u_probe/M. The implementation has these signs and weights. force_probe includes both -Cfrozen*u and the negative weighted electronic gradient, with frozen curvature once. Ry time, hbar=1, g in Ry/bohr, curvature in Ry/bohr^2, and the established Ry masses are consistent.
4. Preparation and ordering: physical_bilinear_preparation.py:83-118 contracts N*Tr(rho A(1-rho)B), the supplied ionic ordered form, and both electron-ion cross terms without conjugating the right probe. BilinearProbe.adjoint conjugates ionic coefficients and adjoints electronic matrices, so an ordered right-adjoint query is explicit. Symmetric and commutator outputs are respectively half-sum and difference of the reversed products. IonicPreparation enforces finite Hermitian PSD ordered moments with canonical antisymmetric part. The documented reference is independent gauge-invariant quasifree electronic channels, not a canonical fixed-number ensemble.
5. Completeness and representability: missing ionic or cross data remain None, and TOTAL is withheld until both are supplied. Hermitian cross matrices encode commuting initial electronic/ionic observables. The public contract explicitly places actual joint representability on the supplying state authority; a nonempty authority string is not a proof, and the API does not claim to certify arbitrary joint moments. The oracle supplies a genuinely admissible nonzero-cross example: a Bernoulli Fock mixture with occupation-conditioned displacements of a full-line canonical Gaussian. This boundary is deliberate, not an unresolved implementation finding.
6. Time transfer and consumers: traced probe helpers through dual, flatten/unflatten, pullback, two_time, correlations, and ordered_parts, plus their oracle consumers. No introduced contribution is silently dropped on these valid-input paths. two_time requires identical channel objects and compatible mode labels, pulls the two probes independently to the initial operators, and preserves PARTIAL versus TOTAL status inside the ordered result. It computes prepared C(t,s), not an assumed C(t-s), and explicitly records stationary_assumed=False. Expected-commutator preservation concerns the stated linearized closure, not exact interacting CAR/CCR operator evolution.
7. Independence and sensitivity: run_physical_bilinear_oracle.py:117-130 assembles the original density-matrix EOM literally; it does not call the production dual or forward derivative. Its coordinate generator/exponential is compared with independently integrated selected probes. The ordered comparator at lines 152-171 explicitly lifts matrices to Fock operators and sums conditional ionic moments, rather than reusing the production Wick contraction. Full-coordinate comparisons, asymmetric complex probes, basis/phase rotations, and the separate complete-BvK test expose the trace/transpose/adjoint and normalization distinctions. Reusing the settled CAR constructor does not make this a reuse of the production contraction.
