## Architecture and preparation boundary

The settled deterministic chain is:

**actual bundles → \(H_0\), bare \(g_{\pm q}\), FD reference and spatial \(K_q\) → same-frequency screened response → ionic gradient plus own-producer frozen curvature.**

The quantum attachment must supply **the initial electronic state, ordered two-point correlations and commutator information**, then connect them to the retained electronic variables of that same action. Retained-memory elimination can carry their unresolved-initial contribution; it cannot manufacture the ensemble.

Four distinctions are load-bearing:

1. **Raw occupation versus quadrature.**  
   Current `F/qe_pw_response_frames.py:204-222,316-341,370-373` constructs raw FD occupations, then multiplies them by `source_kweights`; `PWResponseChannel.weighted_occupations` already includes that weight and explicit spin. Pauli factors require raw source **and target** occupations. Absolute regional fluctuations additionally require a declared BvK/observable normalization; neither weights nor electron counts are independent Bernoulli probabilities.

2. **FD-shaped smearing versus physical temperature.**  
   The recorded `degauss/common_mu` can define an **auxiliary independent-\(H_0\) preparation**. Calling it the physical temperature of coupled electrons, ions and environment requires separate justification. Giustino’s thermal definitions and 0007’s physical-Gibbs tests do not supply that identification.

3. **Zero mean versus zero fluctuations.**  
   The centered full-Fock construction in `0007/check_response.py:11-21` is precisely a two-point object after subtracting the mean. Zero electronic perturbation mean does not provide—or remove—the required electronic noise. A real-tangent covariance alone does not supply its ordered quantum correlator or commutator.

4. **Retained evolution versus relaxation.**  
   Explicit electronic transitions and finite memory retain information. Physical population relaxation, irreversible loss and equilibrium fluctuation–dissipation matching require additional physical preparation/environmental input; neither \(\operatorname{Im}z\) nor a finite bath supplies it.
