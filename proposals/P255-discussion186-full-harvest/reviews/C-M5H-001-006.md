## Adverse finding and parent correction

The first draft said the R13 `(2,3)` clock generator was **not** a compact symmetry of the unrestricted certified action. False: constant spatial `SO(3)` rotations preserve `eta`, conjugate all curvature tensors and preserve the trace powers. The reviewer identified the missing last boundary: this particular rotation moves the **nondegenerate fixed exterior** `diag(8,1,delta,0)`. The corrected [proof](../proof.md), [decision](../harvest-decision.md) and C-M5H-001 exclusion state action symmetry but no admissible fixed-exterior time-dependent rigid orbit or proved physical Noether charge. A targeted regression compares a nonzero spatial commutator before/after a `(2,3)` rotation and separately asserts the same rotation changes the exterior. The family-restricted zero-cost result survives; the global-minimizer inference remains unlicensed because eta boost directions can have negative static signs.
