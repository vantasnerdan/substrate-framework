The [19:04:13Z follow-up](https://github.com/vantasnerdan/substrate-framework/discussions/186#discussioncomment-18604313) reports 22 eta-negative/Frobenius-positive fields, an `r26_checks.py` wall calculation, and finite-lattice strand, moment, census and spin readings. The **local rank-one identity** is exact without that script: if a smooth field `M` depends on one coordinate only, then at most one spatial derivative is nonzero and every `F_ij=[∂i(Mη),∂j(Mη)]` vanishes. A one-coordinate rotation along the isospectral vacuum orbit also has zero trace-potential cost, regardless of its gradient. Independent symbolic multiplication checked the zero commutator. Thus the curvature-plus-spectral-potential action does not control a single gradient in this local class. A wall meeting another texture, a pinned transverse boundary or a core need not be one-coordinate or vacuum-valued, so this identity and an `h/2` finite-lattice result do **not** establish zero continuum tension for a charged strand, nor remove every possible `delta` constraint.

For an **ungauged, hypothetical** positive term `κ Σ_i Tr(∂iM ∂iM)` and the fixed-amplitude pair block `s0 I+b0 R(2kφ)`, independent differentiation gives `Σ_i Tr(∂iM ∂iM)=8 k² b0²/r²` outside the core and hence `16π κ k² b0² log(L/ξ)` on a planar annulus. In this restricted comparison the five positive-index partitions `{1,1,1,1}`, `{2,1,1}`, `{2,2}`, `{3,1}`, `{4}` have sums of `k²` equal to `1`, `1.5`, `2`, `2.5`, `4`. This is a **logarithmic cost of ungauged isolated rays**, not an ordering theorem for interacting charged configurations. A smooth one-direction orbit wall rotating its pair-block phase by `π` over width `W` instead costs `2π² κ b0²/W` per unit wall area: positive for fixed width, but no width-independent positive floor if spreading is allowed. A specified gauge connection may cancel asymptotic angular derivatives, so the ungauged logarithm and partition ranking cannot be carried over to `κ Tr(DM DM)` without a separate gauge-field and boundary-energy calculation. No potential or connection is chosen by this review.

Finally, Report-017's `δ_M=2v0 v0ᵀ−η` norm is **field-dependent**, not the constant Frobenius norm on boosted fields (they coincide in the frozen time frame). For `η=diag(1,-1,-1)`, `v0=(cosh χ,sinh χ,0)` and an admissible spatial-rotation matrix `F12=1, F21=-1` with all other entries zero, independent symbolic multiplication gives `Tr(F δ_M Fᵀ δ_M⁻¹)=2 cosh(2χ)` versus Frobenius `2`; both are positive. Eta-negative/Frobenius-positive samples therefore show the eta/Frobenius sign distinction, **not** that Report-016 descent has a stable endpoint or that those sample magnitudes use the exact `δ_M` contraction. Report-016's own shrinking charged family and unresolved stationary branch remain a distinct question.
