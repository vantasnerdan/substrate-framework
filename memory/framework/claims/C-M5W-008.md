# C-M5W-008

## Statement
The accepted statement is reproduced exactly from the claim registry.

C-M5W-008 (symbolic_verified; thin-wall bag stability split): Within the C-M5W-005 reduced thin-wall family at fixed omega, the radius-reduced bag energy F_omega(R) = 4 pi sigma(omega) R^2 - (4 pi/3) p(omega) R^3 has exact derivatives F' = 4 pi R (2 sigma - p R) and F'' = 8 pi (sigma - p R); hence at the stationary radius R_c = 2 sigma/p the second variation is F''_omega(R_c) = -8 pi sigma(omega) < 0 exactly, since sigma > 0. The fixed-omega bag is therefore a Morse-index-at-least-one saddle of the reduced radial problem - a critical nucleation bubble, not an energy minimum - closing the radial (volume-vs-surface) part of the linear-stability frontier named in C-M5W-005's exclusions. Further, along the stationary family the physical charge Q(omega) = omega I(omega) with I > 0 (the slice inertia density f^2 + 4b^2 is nonnegative and nonzero on any nontrivial bag; recorded interiors iota_B = 2.395 to 2.404), and across the C-M5W-007 rungs R and Q_true decrease strictly as omega increases, so the family satisfies the dQ/d omega < 0 criterion (numeric ingredient, corrected normalization, certified in C-M5W-007), and the envelope identity dE_data/dQ = omega holds on the family by the chain rule on the accepted Legendre scope dE_w/d(omega) = -Q of C-M5C-001. A constrained-minimum conclusion for the fixed-charge ensemble is NOT asserted: C-M5C-001 supplies the Legendre reduction and envelope identity but no constrained radial Hessian theorem, and the exact second-variation calculation that would supply it is open frontier.
