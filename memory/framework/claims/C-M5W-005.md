# C-M5W-005

## Statement
The accepted statement is reproduced exactly from the claim registry.

C-M5W-005 (symbolic_verified; thin-wall bag selection, charge closure, and comparison): Within the reduced thin-wall family at fixed omega above the deep-branch crossing, the rotating-frame thin-wall functional is F_omega = 4 pi R^2 sigma(omega) - (4 pi/3) R^3 p(omega), with the branch pressure p(omega)=-V_omega(B(omega))>0. Its exact stationarity in R is the volume-vs-surface law R=2 sigma/p. The inertia and charge are I=(4 pi/3) R^3 iota_int + 4 pi R^2 iota_wall and Q=omega I. The static energy is E0=F_omega+omega^2 I/2, and the physical relative-equilibrium energy is E=F_omega+omega Q=F_omega+omega^2 I. The envelope identity dE/dQ=omega holds exactly for any nondegenerate stationary family, and the branch pressure obeys dp/d(omega^2)=iota_int/2. If sigma tends to a finite positive limit as omega^2 approaches omega_*^2 from above, p tends to zero and R tends to infinity. This reproduces the wall-at-infinity/quiescent-exterior arrangement of C-M5C-003, while C-M5W-003 supplies the two-sided isofrequency arrangement at the energy-bookkeeping level: two arrangements of the same orbit-fixed-locus mechanism, not an identity of their solution families and not an M5.32 equivalence.
