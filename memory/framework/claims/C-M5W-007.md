# C-M5W-007

## Statement
The accepted statement is reproduced exactly from the claim registry.

C-M5W-007 (numeric_evidence; rotating S1 bag family above the Maxwell frequency): For omega^2 = omega*^2 + delta with delta in {0.001, 0.002, ..., 0.007}, the C-M5C-001 fixed-charge bag problem on the C-M5W-001 slice admits stationary spherical wall-bag solutions, constructed by delta-continuation with kink-shape seeds and accepted by the predeclared production criteria: solver termination recorded per rung, mesh stability of the extracted radius (|dR_mesh| <= 1.5e-3 R_read; largest observed 5.0e-4), and bounded residuals (res_rms 2.9e-5 to 2.0e-3). Across the computed window the family follows the exact C-M5W-005 selection law R = 2 sigma_0/p(omega) with agreement chi = R_read/(2 sigma_0/p) tending to 1 monotonically as delta -> 0+ (|chi - 1| is consistent with a near-quadratic power law, endpoint log-log slope 1.718 across the window; chi = 0.999562 at delta = 0.001 down to 0.987601 at delta = 0.007), the envelope identity dE/dQ = omega holds to <= 1.9e-4 relative at every finite-difference check across the family (delta = 0.002..0.006; truncation-limited) evaluated at the physical charge normalization Q = omega I, and the rung-1 bag energy matches the exact thin-wall critical value E_crit = (16 pi/3) sigma_0^3/p^2 formed at the rung's own pressure to 0.16%. The radius and the physical charge decrease strictly as omega increases across the rungs (Q_true from 2.3351e10 to 6.5152e7), so dQ/d omega < 0 on the certified family. The archived Q_trap column of attempts/0003/bag_results.json was formed as omega^2 times the inertia integral; the corrected columns Q_true = Q_trap/sqrt(w2), the corrected envelope, and their consequences live in attempts/0005/bag_results_corrected.json with the derivation recorded in attempts/0003/CORRECTIONS-2026-09-02.md.
