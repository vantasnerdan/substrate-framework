# C-M5W-007

## Statement
The accepted statement is reproduced exactly from the claim registry.

C-M5W-007 (numeric_evidence; rotating S1 bag family above the Maxwell frequency): For omega^2 = omega*^2 + delta with delta in {0.001, 0.002, ..., 0.007}, the C-M5C-001 fixed-charge bag problem on the C-M5W-001 slice admits stationary spherical wall-bag solutions, constructed with a moving-coordinate, phase-fixed radial BVP and asymptotic Robin data. Every rung terminates with solve_bvp status 0 at tolerance 1e-8 (maximum collocation residual <=1.00e-8 and boundary residual <1e-10), and the corrected radial Derrick residual is <=3.3e-10 relative. Tolerance variation from 1e-6 to 1e-10 moves the mid-rung radius by 1.43e-7 relative, while matching exponents 12 to 16 move it by 7.31e-10 relative. Pressure is evaluated as the exact canonical value p=-V_omega(B(omega)), not by the first-order endpoint product. Across the window R_m decreases from 1218.761785 to 174.138125 and chi=R_m p/(2 sigma_0) tends to one as delta->0+ (chi=1.000328 at delta=0.001 and 1.002286 at delta=0.007; endpoint log-log slope of |chi-1| is 0.998). The envelope identity dE/dQ = omega holds to <= 1.9e-4 relative at every finite-difference check across the family (delta = 0.002..0.006; truncation-limited) at Q=omega I and physical energy E=F_omega+omega Q. The rung-1 rotating-frame energy matches the exact thin-wall critical value E_crit=(16 pi/3)sigma_0^3/p^2, formed with the exact rung pressure, to 0.16%. Radius and physical charge decrease strictly across all six adjacent pairs (Q from 2.3430e10 to 6.8628e7). Attempt 0006 supersedes the pressure, charge, virial, boundary, and solver-status defects preserved in attempts 0003 and 0005.
