## Question and Positive Deliverable

P248 asks which exact, numerical, and physical claims in the issue-184 paper
survive clean-room reconstruction and what minimal framework-native construction
fulfills every load-bearing intended role when the paper's route fails. The
positive deliverable is a complete purpose-aware claim graph, claim-appropriate
SymPy, Lean, and SciPy evidence, executable replacements for every broken
load-bearing step, reusable package APIs, individual review of every proposed
framework claim, impact-bounded replay, synchronized accepted state, and an
empty in-boundary debt ledger. A critique, failure inventory, or partial repair
does not complete the issue.

## Implementation and Oracle Plan

The implementation will place reusable tensors, actions, kernels, solvers, and
transformations under `src/substrate_framework/`, focused tests under `tests/`,
and finite formal statements under `formal/SubstrateFramework/` when Lean adds
real assurance. Proposal scripts will import package APIs and use
`substrate_framework.verification` rather than duplicate check helpers.
SymPy handles exact variations, inverses, determinants, contractions,
conservation identities, dimensions, and limits. Lean handles finite exact
statements with audited imports, proof escapes, axioms, and physical encoding.
SciPy is reserved for genuinely unresolved roots, integrals, ODEs, BVPs, PDEs,
or spectra with declared equations, domains, boundary data, precision,
discretization, tolerances, error norms, solver-status gates, refinements, and
an independent method or soluble limit. Near-zero spectral, force, or energy
gates trigger `small-ratio-numerics` before their design freezes. Every custom
oracle receives a representative sign, coefficient, convention, boundary, or
source mutation. GitNexus impact runs precede changes to existing symbols and
detect-changes bounds the final replay.
