# Physics Erdős Loop

The elite outcome is the requested positive object, promoted with its real
dependency closure. Bank useful route results, then pursue the next construction.
A tally, a smaller question, or a polished obstruction earns only the scientific
progress it actually supplies. Apply the reward, supervision, continuation,
authority, ten-achievement, and terminal-PR contract in `AGENTS.md`; this skill
adds the execution details rather than duplicating that contract.

## Load the decision-changing references

- Load `../physics-discovery/SKILL.md` before choosing an open mechanism or
  constructing a replacement route; it owns focused briefs, analogies and
  generation methods. Fixed proofs and routine checks can proceed directly.
- Read [governance.md](references/governance.md) for claim, convention, invariant,
  release, or canonical-API changes.
- Read [oracles.md](references/oracles.md) when selecting or auditing verification.
- Read [problem-deconstruction.md](references/problem-deconstruction.md) before
  freezing a multi-rung campaign or a representation of an object, ensemble,
  localization, mode, stability, force, or interaction.
- Load `small-ratio-numerics/SKILL.md` before designing or replaying soft
  eigenvalue, Morse-index, stability-edge, force, or splitting measurements
  within roughly three orders of the numerical floor, including reused code.
  Record applicable error-budget, zero-mode, eigenpair, lambda_min/lambda_2,
  observed-order, and jitter prescriptions as measured, bounded, or explicitly
  unresolved below the floor.
- Load `theorem-synthesis/SKILL.md` for higher claims composed from accepted
  atoms; reground periodically during long synthesis work.

failure-derived candidates expand the universe append-only. The existing

does not require a separate campaign or completed promotion plan.

## Achievement 2 — the analytic bridge that earns the computation

Follow the typed chain in problem-deconstruction: object, symmetry/conservation,
ensemble/full functional, admissible space, analytic structure, observable,
irreducible remainder, approximation, verdict. Each node records
`positive_intent`, `requires`, `pass_licenses`, `does_not_license`,
`maximum_verdict`, `failure_scope`, and `unlocks`.

Work exact elimination, complete variations (including background-zero terms),
symmetry reduction, scaling, bounds/coercivity/monotonicity/convexity, virial
identities, limits/asymptotics/perturbation, and applicable existence, uniqueness,
compactness, or no-go theorems. The analytic-closure receipt names the strongest
earned result, one unresolved proposition, why the current ladder leaves it,
and frozen numerical design freedoms. It need not prove every unknown analytic
method impossible. Earlier sampling retains its hypothesis-generation/debugging
role; fresh frozen design and execution earn production evidence.

The supervisor reviews equations, function spaces, quantifiers, excluded modes,
and alternative approaches before implementation grows. Use current GitNexus
or AST/source impact analysis for dependencies and omitted implementation paths;
direct mathematical reasoning supplies the separate proof/approach audit.

## Achievement 3 — reusable construction and a replayable argument

Put equations, constants, conversions, solvers, and transformations in
`src/substrate_framework/`; thin campaign scripts call those APIs. Keep imports
side-effect-free and derive quantities from defining inputs. Search existing
helpers before adding one. Re-anchor edits by current content; use AST-aware
edits for nested/multi-site changes and a full-read rewrite after repeated stale
patch failures. Reuse [verify_claim.py](assets/verify_claim.py) and
[verify_pde.py](assets/verify_pde.py) where their actual predicates fit.

Use shared `substrate_framework.numerics` for IVP, MOL, BVP, refinement and
`trapezoid_integral`. Mutable standalone scripts use `np.trapezoid` or a safe
two-step fallback. Inspect direct/imported/dynamic legacy `np.trapz` access and
eager nested `getattr` defaults. Preserve native aborts of immutable sources and
capture explicit alias-only replays; compatibility errors leave the scientific
candidate unadjudicated. Claim code still owns equations, discretization, data,
norms, thresholds, and interpretation.

Create append-only numbered attempts. Capture source, first-run command,
environment, stdout/stderr, elapsed time, and verdict together. Commit the
load-bearing derivation and script/output pairs; exact prose arguments retain
their proof role, while scripts establish only their evaluated predicates.
Record `route_verdict` (established, refuted with mechanism, or blocked with
missing construction), `evidence_scope`, active node, earned/remaining
constructions, routes considered/tried/remaining, and next decisive action.

A numerical refutation earns its scope through licensed object/ensemble/
observable/admissibility, representation coverage, and a converged error
enclosure excluding success. Nonconvergence, branch loss, continuum drift,
singular normalization, imposed support, or incomplete perturbations instead
earn `NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED` or `REPRESENTATION_SCOPED` evidence.
Diagnose implementation, method, representation, candidate, target, or foundation;
repair, reformulate, and generate materially different candidates accordingly.
Use physics-discovery when the next construction is unclear. Preserve the
assumption or transformation that explains the outcome and the alternatives
it opens. Each new attempt changes a real scientific choice, not just its
evidence threshold; established results also seed new constructions.

## Achievement 4 — an exposing oracle, not a green counter

Audit the analytic/objective bridge first, then the strongest practical oracle
under oracles.md. A claim review consumes raw artifacts and frozen criteria,
not a preferred conclusion. Count lexical check sites, runtime checks, assertion
nodes, terminal tally, and OS exit status separately; dynamic execution may
legitimately differ. `CheckLedger` returns status-zero success, not a positive
check count as an exit code.

Use meaningful input/sign/normalization/convention mutations for custom
verifiers; kernel proofs earn statement/import/axiom/physics audits instead.
Structural predicates evaluate the defining objects rather than copied literals,
stand-ins, or fixed periods. For forms, expand all graded Leibniz/cyclic terms;
separate nonvanishing, closedness, global non-exactness, periods, extension
dependence, and gauge descent.

Eliminate shared intermediates before crediting independence: identical ODEs
with identical initial data already share their trajectory by uniqueness;
downstream equations with the same solution set are regressions. Cross-sector
matching earns explicit field, kinetic-metric, action-measure, and coefficient
maps. Numerical evidence records routine/algorithm, precision, mesh/domain,
data, tolerances/status, error norm and scale-sensitive bounds. PDE evidence
adds spatial/temporal refinement, stability, conservation or controlled
dissipation, and a method cross-check or soluble limit.

## Achievement 5 — strongest useful review and promotion

Apply the one substantive review plus one bounded correction transaction from
`AGENTS.md`. Use `claim-review.md` per proposed/changed claim and
`evidence-attachment-review.md` for coherent evidence groups. Lead with the
strongest meaningful result, unsupported extension, minimum repair, and evidence
that restores stronger scope. Adjacent observations remain follow-ups unless
they directly invalidate the claim, used dependency, or affected consumer.
After two independent evidence passes, new contrary evidence earns further
narrowing; otherwise escalate the shrinking review chain to the user.

Compare framework fit before data fit. An independently reproduced conflict
with canon earns a separate foundational-revision proposal, at least two repairs,
minimum coherent migration, independent review, and full replay. Accepted canon
controls promotion; explicit conditional artifacts and inquiry remain possible.

Promote through the governance reference: four independent statuses, accepted
closure, reusable APIs/tests, consumer replay, registry/release, immutable
campaign, generated docs, and synchronized memory. Release membership follows
`review: accepted` / non-null `accepted_in`, including accepted qualified
claims, rather than an `epistemic: active` filter. For migration edit
`migration/dispositions.yaml`, preserve remaining subclaims, and regenerate
the source queue. Terminal dispositions carry structured reasons and existing
evidence paths; mixed units mapping accepted claims use `qualified`.

Use `scripts/validate_changed.py` once at the frozen content boundary; changed
foundations/shared machinery/contracts or uncertain impact earn full replay.
Reuse unchanged receipts; prose/counts/record-only changes get record checks.
Run validation and commit separately. A final promotion-summary attempt can
start explicitly in progress and finish with record-sensitive validation.

## Achievement 6 — continue the objective; honor an explicit pause

Keep active campaign progress on its branch. Positive completion requires all
ten achievements; scientific exhaustion requires the whole certificate in
`AGENTS.md`, not a route count. Then submit the terminal PR (exhaustion uses
`Advances`, not a positive-completion claim). A user-requested freeze is honored:
preserve all work, model/provenance boundaries, receipts, and exact next route.

When delegation is authorized, assign a bounded child contract, disjoint paths,
raw inputs, requested verified model settings, and a return point. The supervisor
owns integration and alternative generation; reviewers perform their frozen
transaction without recursively commissioning audits. Consolidate recurring
lessons into the relevant contract/skill/template instead of appending duplicate
rules. Reward the next useful construction, not ceremony.
