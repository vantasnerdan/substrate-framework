# Theorem Synthesis

The elite result is a useful new implication, not a restated atom or isolated
special value. Apply `AGENTS.md` and `physics-erdos-loop/SKILL.md` for the shared
reward, supervision, continuation, and promotion contract.

## Achievement — identify the missing glue

Read current release, relevant accepted claims and source. Instantiate
`memory-templates/theorem-synthesis.md` and validate its proposal before proof
work. State the exact higher theorem, at least two distinct accepted dependencies,
structural gap, assumptions/exclusions/consumers, and core or interpretive layer.
Audit dependency use against accepted scope; prior acceptance remains intact.
An explicit hypothesis earns a useful conditional interpretive theorem. A missing
bridge is an open construction, not a refutation of the atoms.

A fixed theorem uses `target_kind: fixed_theorem` and one sound proof route;
open mechanisms compete under frozen structural criteria. Use
`scripts/find_synthesis_candidates.py` and current graph/AST structure for
discovery hints; the supervisor reasons independently about omitted approaches.
The explicit assumption boundary, not a statement-length cap, controls scope.

## Achievement — prove the composition

Use SymPy for exact identities, substitutions, elimination, and finite chains.
Use Lean where it proves the actual finite statement; prefer an end-to-end
theorem using shared definitions. Audit statement, imports, proof escapes,
axiom footprint, and physical encoding. Meaningful mutation belongs to custom
translators/symbolic/numerical verifiers, not a ceremonial mutation of the kernel.
Lean is complementary, not a universal prerequisite.

Keep proof, empirical applicability, and interpretation separate in
`verification_evidence`. Measurements/simulations test nature and consequences;
they supply neither the proof of an exact implication nor hidden derivation inputs.

## Achievement — promote the strongest supported theorem

Record `category: synthesized` and:

```yaml
composition:
  dependencies: [C-SECTOR-001, C-OTHER-001]
  structural_gap: <missing accepted implication or identity>
  glue:
    method: lean  # or sympy
    artifact: <repository-relative proof path>
    entrypoint: <theorem or verifier entrypoint>
```

Declare `exclusions`; interpretive claims also declare
`hypothesis: {label: H-name, statement: ...}`. Interpretive claims may consume
core claims; core dependency closure stays independent of interpretive premises.

Freeze the new claim and used edges for one substantive review and one correction
check. Preserve the strongest supported result with the minimum truthful repair;
adjacent concerns become follow-up unless they directly invalidate composition.
Recheck changed glue/consumers, reuse unchanged receipts, and retain accepted
atoms' authority. Prose/count updates receive structural review rather than
new scientific audits.

Use `scripts/validate_changed.py`: additive leaf theorems with unchanged existing
contracts/consumers can stay scoped; changed foundations, shared machinery,
existing claims or uncertain impact earn wider replay. Complete registry,
accepted release, generated records, and memory together. Supporting subclaims
and failed proof routes stay on the campaign branch until the higher objective
succeeds or the `AGENTS.md` exhaustion certificate is earned. Honor explicit
user pauses with durable state; a partial theorem is progress, not completion.
