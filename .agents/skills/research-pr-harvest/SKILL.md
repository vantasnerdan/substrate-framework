# Research PR Harvest

The elite harvest lands the strongest useful positive statement with the minimum
truthful repair. Keep three decisions separate: artifact merge, scientific claim
promotion, and whole-goal completion. Provenance and reuse are valuable; they
acquire accepted scientific authority through governance, not by merging.

Apply `AGENTS.md` for the reward, terminal campaign, ownership, and review
contract. This skill serves existing external PRs or terminal campaign PRs;
active local campaigns preserve intermediate utilities and subclaims as branch
commits while continuing their parent objective.

## Achievement 1 — a clear, authorized boundary

Read the PR, goal issue, base release, accepted scope, diff, tests, and discussion.
Load `physics-erdos-loop` for physics and the applicable code-review/impact
workflow. Use current ripwire or AST/source analysis for changed symbols and
consumers; independently reason about the science and omitted alternatives.

Identify exactly one canonical issue that predates even the draft PR.
Use `Advances #N` for incomplete higher goals and `Fixes #N` for full success.
For an external PR missing that issue, create it, preserve the original PR as
provenance, and open a compliant focused harvest afterward.

A user-supplied PR authorizes a non-author/non-implementer agent to process its
normal lifecycle: review/comment, metadata, corrections, focused harvest, eligible
merge, qualified closure, and issue update. Use a distinct merger by default.
The user or repository owner may explicitly direct an authoring agent to self-merge
a named PR; record the override separately from independent scientific review. Preserve
contributor history, unrelated branches, the user's objective, and promotion
boundaries. Report an actual permission/protection barrier with the exact next
action rather than assuming broader authority.

## Achievement 2 — useful units and minimum repairs

Group independently mergeable units or materially different risks, not every
file or theorem entrypoint. Examples: utility, exact identity, solver/verifier,
conditional component, speculative composition, or a reviewed durable narrative.
Record each unit's statement, dependencies, evidence, consumers, and usefulness
without the favored headline.

One substantive pass plus one correction check answers three questions:

1. Correct under stated inputs and conventions?
2. Materially novel or reusable enough to avoid reinvention?
3. Locally complete, compatible, proportionately validated, with no unresolved
   defect inside its own scope?

External units can earn these without finishing the parent goal; a terminal
campaign PR additionally supplies success/exhaustion evidence. Test actual
derivations rather than copied formulas; make parameters, conventions and
assumptions explicit. Evidence attachments earn their exact proof, corroboration,
regression, applicability, or provenance role.

A current correction names a falsifier, absent/circular load-bearing step,
unsupported used dependency, or broken in-boundary consumer. Adjacent concerns
remain one compact follow-up; new evidence, not a later reviewer's preference,
earns their elevation. Lead with the strongest supported core, unsupported
extension, minimum repair, and evidence restoring stronger scope.

Classify units: **merge unchanged**, **correct then merge**, or **leave in PR
history**. Correct-then-merge names one repair and landing check and remains
`request changes`, `active refactor`, or `active harvest` until resolved.
A support gap is not refutation. Preserve useful scope through quantifier,
hypothesis, layer, evidence-role, naming, or convention correction.

The **terminal-close** test for an unmerged PR is: all reusable units landed
elsewhere; every remaining unit has evidence of incorrectness, non-novelty or
unmaintainability; explicit author/owner withdrawal; or a landed superseding
implementation. Record qualifying evidence and replacement links. A finite
repair, missing merger/dependency, or conflict with current canon keeps the
repair route live.

## Achievement 3 — focused landing with honest authority

For an inseparable external dump, select a focused harvest with implementation,
tests, and minimal API docs. Include memory/attempts/narrative only when they are
the reviewed durable artifact. A failed route earns landing through its reusable
theorem, counterexample, oracle, fixture, or repair—not its diary.

Keep accepted authority in the registry and generated records faithful. A correct
conditional API may land with explicit assumptions while a canon conflict earns
a separate `challenges` or foundational-revision proposal. The conflict affects
promotion, not truthful conditional utility. A newly discovered plausible route
in a purported terminal campaign returns scientific work to its campaign branch.

Keep the parent goal open when incomplete. Preserve speculative value in one
compact issue/PR frontier note: mechanism, hypotheses, strongest consequence,
the insight or failed assumption that motivates the next construction, its
decisive check, and source/API links. Route renewed scientific generation to
`../physics-discovery/SKILL.md` on the campaign branch. Open parent work is frontier;
hidden assumptions, unsupported promises, or broken consumers inside the
selected unit are repairs to finish before landing.

## Achievement 4 — proportionate evidence and a decisive next action

Run targeted tests/oracles, impact analysis, and one final repository validation
using `scripts/validate_changed.py`. An additive public export can remain scoped
when contracts are unchanged, consumers known, and targeted coverage passes.
Promotion/release, shared numerics/verifiers, governance semantics, changed public
contracts with consumers, dependencies, cross-sector conventions, or uncertain
impact earn full replay. Scheduled/requested full backstops remain applicable.
Process prose, templates, review records and count corrections get affected
structural checks and `git diff --check`, not unrelated scientific reruns.
Reuse unchanged receipts; rerun only stale predicates after repair. Avoid
meta-tests for reviewer identity, prose, tallies, or the validation ledger.
A user instruction to merge without further validation preserves existing
evidence/defects and proceeds within repository protection.

Update the canonical issue, not just the PR, with this compact record (`None`
for empty categories):

- Landed: unit, strongest positive statement, commit/PR, one receipt.
- Correct next: minimum repair and landing check.
- History only: unit-level terminal reason.
- Authority: promoted IDs or none; goal open/complete; issue predates PR.
- Lifecycle: author/implementer; distinct merger or recorded owner override;
  merged, active refactor/harvest, request changes, or terminal closed with evidence.
- Frontier: one decisive construction, question, or distinction the next action
  establishes; classify as verification, implementation/representation, or
  scientific construction. Name specific evidence that would overturn a finding.

Post when authorized, otherwise provide ready-to-post text. Keep source PRs live
while their repair/harvest is live. An existing-PR review may finish at its
operational disposition; an active scientific campaign continues its objective
under `AGENTS.md`. The great outcome is usable science, not a larger scorecard.
