# Physics Discovery

Earn a useful change in how the problem is seen, then turn it into a construction
that can be tested. Apply AGENTS.md for authority, user scope, continuation and
promotion; physics-erdos-loop owns claim execution. This skill supplies ideas
and their first informative checks. Its methods are a menu, not a quota or a
new acceptance checklist.

## Focus the question

Use the existing proposal/research contract as the home for a compact discovery
brief. Refresh it when the question or explanatory obstacle changes:

- The physical phenomenon or exact theorem sought, and why its answer matters.
- Sourced equations, domains, symmetries and known constraints. Separate the
  user's invariants, accepted premises and optional ansatz/representation choices.
- The unresolved tension: which two apparently reasonable requirements resist
  coexistence, or which implication is still missing?
- A useful nearby construction and, when available, a structural analogue from
  another area; identify precisely what transfers and what remains unmatched.
- The next calculation that could distinguish mechanisms or reveal structure.

Keep mandatory instructions and accepted authority available. Focus the working
brief on this problem; consult specialized verification and delivery details
when those decisions arise. Source facts remain traceable, earlier exposure is
declared, and the parent campaign retains its complete durable record.

## Retrieve relationships and transform the problem

Search by mathematical structure as well as physical names: a constraint and its
generator, a local-to-global obstruction, a soft direction coupled to a stiff
core, a conserved quantity under reduction. Reuse and new generation can proceed
together; a nearest accepted example supplies ingredients, not a prescribed form.
Verify a retrieved construction at source before relying on it. Distinguish a
recalled analogy worth investigating from an established imported result.

Choose transformations that address the actual obstacle:

| Discovery move | Construction to try | What makes the result informative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove an ansatz restriction | Enlarge the admissible class or restore an omitted degree of freedom while retaining user invariants. | Identify the term, mode or solution family the restriction excluded. |
| Change representation | Try variables, dual descriptions, quotient/global objects, or an observable adapted to the question. | Supply a map and its domain; show where the obstruction changes. |
| Transfer a structural analogy | Map a relation from another sector or field into the current problem. | Match objects, equations and hypotheses; name the first broken correspondence. |
| Take a controlled limit | Solve a simpler regime, separate scales, or perturb an exactly understood object. | State which feature could persist and the continuation or matching bridge it needs. |
| Work backward from a consequence | Construct the symmetry, functional, constraint or operator that could produce the desired behavior. | Derive a nontrivial consequence and identify assumptions newly introduced. |
| Use the obstruction | Ask whether a failure mechanism can supply a needed feature in another admissible construction. | Derive the changed mechanism rather than renaming the failed route. |
| Combine partial constructions | Join complementary results or search for one object satisfying several obligations. | Expose their shared variables, compatibility conditions and missing glue. |

For example, a symmetry-restricted stationary search leaves open a question about
the excluded perturbations; a coefficient resemblance invites a field/action map;
an ill-conditioned difference invites a direct equation for the difference.
These are prompts to derive something, not claims that a particular physical
mechanism exists. A useful seed includes an equation, mapping, variational
construction or toy model, plus its strongest consequence and missing bridge.

## Protect distinct approaches long enough to learn from them

Separate generation, development and comparison. Give a promising unfamiliar
construction enough exploration to expose its mechanism before comparing its
maturity with a polished familiar route. Prefer candidates that differ in
physical mechanism, admissible class or structural assumptions; parameter and
solver variants stay within their route family. Open-mechanism preregistration
and fixed-theorem exceptions follow AGENTS.md.

Choose the next investment by structural plausibility, explanatory reach and
how much the next check would teach. Preserve a speculative seed with a named
bridge in the existing frontier while another construction develops. Candidate
count, unusual vocabulary and claimed originality carry no scientific weight.
Known contradictions under the same hypotheses retain their force.

When delegation is authorized, a fresh discovery worker can first see the brief
and constraints without the favored route or its narrative. After its first
construction, reveal the history, remove duplicates, and reconcile known results.
Record the different inputs and returned ideas in the existing child contract.
This complements the continuity holder; it does not replace the independently
blinded reviewer required for exhaustion or claim review. Without delegation,
rewrite the question using another representation and keep the lack of
independence explicit. Personality labels alone are not diverse evidence.

## Turn surprise into a testable conjecture

Toy calculations and exploratory numerical samples can reveal patterns and
guide which conjecture to investigate. Record equations, inputs, exposure and
observations at exploratory scope, together with the conjecture they motivate.
Keep observations used to construct or fit a model visible as discovery inputs.
Freeze a discriminating consequence and a fresh design/execution to earn later
support; empirical prediction uses an unused observable, regime or held-out data
when practical. Repeating a fitted observation only checks reproduction.

For production numerics, physics-erdos-loop's analytic receipt and the applicable
small-ratio skill still govern evidence. Exploratory output retains its original
role. An exact theorem needs a proof; a mechanism needs its defining construction;
a claim about nature also needs applicability and empirical tests. Novel to this
campaign and novel to the literature are distinct descriptions: substantiate the
latter through relevant source comparison before claiming it.

Label a provisional assumption change and its consequences. An optional ansatz
can change within the objective; an accepted premise can be investigated
conditionally through the challenge path. A user-owned invariant changes only
with their authority. A conjecture's appeal supplies neither accepted imports
nor permission to rewrite the objective.

## Bank the generative result and continue

Use the existing candidate/attempt record: the motivating observation or analogy,
the transformation tried, the construction and its useful consequence, the exact
obstruction or missing bridge, and the next informative check. For a failed route,
record which assumption caused the failure and which alternatives remain open.
For a success, preserve the variable, correspondence, limit or unexpected
identity that unlocked it. Link runnable evidence where a calculation supplies
the result. Reuse these sourced lessons in the next brief instead of copying the
whole narrative or creating a parallel memory ledger.

For an explicitly requested workflow evaluation, compare the baseline and revised
context on matched problems with comparable compute. Judge materially distinct
constructions, useful consequences and survival of exposing checks; include a
fixed-theorem control so discovery overhead is visible. Keep method and outcome
separate. A structured workflow can improve search without proving that it did
so on this campaign. Delivery of this skill is not evidence of new physics.
